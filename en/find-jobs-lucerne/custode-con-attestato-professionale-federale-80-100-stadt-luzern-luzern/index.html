Full description

We focus on proximity and openness: At the city administration we live the du-culture – already in the application process. Organizing, cleaning, repairing, maintaining, monitoring – you ensure the smooth operation of our urban buildings.

Your tasks

  • You are responsible for the operation and maintenance of the school facilities and outdoor locations as well as the personnel and professional management of the cleaning staff (currently 5 employees)
  • Smaller repairs are done independently.
  • You organize and work in the building cleaning.
  • For the usage concerns in the properties of your own area, you are the contact and coordination point
  • When it comes to budgeting, you are actively involved and responsible for budget control in your own department.
  • Thematic cooperation in conversions and new buildings, renovations, procurement
  • You are the contact person for safety concerns (fire protection, EKAS)

Your profile

  • You have a basic craftsmanship training (EFZ) and a degree as a housekeeper with a federal certificate.
  • You bring several years of professional and managerial experience as a housekeeper.
  • You have good user knowledge in MS-Office.
  • You are a winning, team capable and service-oriented personality with high social skills and assertiveness.
