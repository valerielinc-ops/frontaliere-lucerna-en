Full description

This is what awaits you

You are at work with heart and enthusiasm and appreciate the open exchange within the team?

As an employee: in Service Sales / Sales Support you take care of the safety of our customers every day and support our insurance and pension consultants. In this way, you contribute to the trust in the furniture as the most personal insurance in Switzerland.

This is what you do with us

In this varied function, you act as a link between the insurance and pension advisors and our specialist departments on the management.

Your varied area of responsibility includes the administrative and organizational support of our insurance and pension consultants, the creation of offers, the processing of applications and the coordination of appointments. With your high willingness to provide services, you support our customers in personal conversation at the reception or on the phone and also handle simpler claims cases.

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed training in the insurance sector
  • High service orientation and enjoyment of teamwork
  • Sales- and customer-oriented as well as independent personality
  • Fluent German skills, other languages are a plus
  • Security in dealing with the Microsoft Office range and digital affinity

Working with us

At the Seetal-Rontal General Agency, you will be welcomed by a motivated, dynamic and experienced team. Excellent working conditions, great training opportunities and a modern working environment await you.

Your workload is 100% or by arrangement a high part-time workload. Your place of work is Hochdorf and the job starts in spring 2027 or by appointment.
