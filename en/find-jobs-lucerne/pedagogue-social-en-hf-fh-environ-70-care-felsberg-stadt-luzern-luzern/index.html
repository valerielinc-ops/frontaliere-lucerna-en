Full description

Are you looking for a meaningful task in which you can accompany and support children in everyday school life? In our school-supplementary supervision of the Felsberg Primary School you can expect a varied environment, a committed team and the chance to contribute your educational skills with pleasure. Tasks:

  • You actively participate in the care team and design a professional, child-friendly care and promotion.
  • According to the principles of "ama terra - fourchette verte" you prepare healthy and balanced meals.
  • You are a reliable caregiver for children and parents and have parent meetings (without evening appointments).
  • You will maintain contact with teachers, home maintenance and other specialists at the school.
  • You are happy to lead interns
  • You work mostly during the school weeks, Monday to Friday, from around 10.30 to 18.00. Requirements
  • You bring a completed socio-educational, socio-cultural or educational education.
  • You are ready to train and accompany specialists care and social pedagogues
  • You have experience and joy in accompanying children from 4 to 7 years old, supporting them and supporting them in their everyday life.
  • You empathize with the children’s world and deal with culturally and age-mixed groups.
  • You like to prepare small, healthy meals and design them with heart.
  • You convince through your empathetic, appreciative and team-oriented way and actively contribute to our school life.
  • You bring experience in the administrative and planning task area.
  • You work independently, reliably and communicate openly, clearly and transparently.
