Full description

The Lucerne Canton Hospital with its locations in Lucerne, Sursee and Wolhusen is a company of the LUKS Group and thus belongs to one of the leading hospital groups in Switzerland. The more than 8,500 employees are there with warmth, competence and commitment around the clock for the well-being of patients. The clinics and institutes of the LUKS Group offer medical services of the highest quality.

The digital transformation of care is a key success factor for high-quality, secure and future-oriented healthcare. As Chief Nursing Informatics Officer (CNIO), you will play a key strategic role within the LUKS Group. They combine nursing expertise with technological innovation, provide group-wide impulses and play a decisive role in shaping the digital development of care. You act at the interface between care, medicine, computer science and corporate development.

For our team we are looking for reinforcement immediately or by agreement. Tasks: • They design and are responsible for the Group-wide care informatics strategy and its implementation. As a central strategic contact, you advise the Chief Nursing Officer (CNO), represent the nursing perspective in relevant decision-making bodies and provide decisive impulses for the digital future of the LUKS Group. • They shape the further development and harmonization of digital care processes across all locations. In the Epic clinical information system in particular, you create the conditions for efficient workflows, high data quality and secure and patient-oriented care. • They identify strategic innovation potentials, prioritize digitalization projects and drive forward future-oriented developments such as artificial intelligence, data-based decision support and new digital supply models. You combine technological progress with measurable clinical and operational benefits • They lead and develop the Clinical Information Lab (CIL) team and, together with the CMIO, the leaders from care, medicine and IT, shape a sustainable digital culture of change. They promote interprofessional cooperation, strengthen digital skills and accompany challenging transformation processes Requirements • You have a recognised degree as a qualified nurse HF/FH, in-depth clinical experience and a Master of Science in Nursing. Complement a further university qualification in nursing informatics, health informatics or a related field of expertise Your profile ideally • They bring several years of proven experience in the strategic and operational digitization of the healthcare system, preferably in complex hospital organizations. You are familiar with clinical information systems, digital care processes and their challenges from an overarching perspective. Knowledge in Epic is an advantage • They have proven leadership and project responsibility and have successfully designed complex, interprofessional change and digitalization projects. They know how to bring different interests together, set strategic priorities and sustainably anchor changes • They convince as a strategically thinking, decision-making and integrative leader with a high level of communication competence, a pronounced will to shape and a secure sense of organizational contexts. They combine innovative strength with clinical judgment and a consistent focus on patient safety, quality and cost-effectiveness We offer: • You will assume a strategically important leadership role with group-wide impact and the opportunity to play a decisive role in shaping the digital future of care within a large, complex hospital organization. • You will receive an attractive professional design leeway, contribute your expertise directly to strategic decision-making processes and participate in the further development of the LUKS Group in close cooperation with the CNO and other central leaders. • You will design a forward-looking topic at the interface of care, technology and innovation and get the opportunity to accompany new ideas, digital solutions and innovative care approaches from strategic conception to successful implementation • She expects a demanding and dynamic management environment with diverse development opportunities, attractive employment conditions and a corporate culture in which interprofessional cooperation, personal responsibility and sustainable change are of great importance.
