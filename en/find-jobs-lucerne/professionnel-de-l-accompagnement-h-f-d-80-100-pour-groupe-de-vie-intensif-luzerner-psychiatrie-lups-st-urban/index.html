Full description

Over 90 employees in the areas of care and social pedagogy accompany and support people with mental and multiple disabilities. The Sonnegarte dormitory in St. Urban is an organizational unit of Lucerne Psychiatry AG. Professional support (m/f/d) 80-100 % For intensive housing

Their tasks

  • Complement of the team of intensive living groups with 3 adult people with cognitive and mental impairment, as well as a crisis intervention place
  • Accompaniment and support in individual settings, if necessary 2:1 support
  • Co-design of the daily routine on the residential group and support in the implementation
  • Close coordination with the team, for a common attitude and joint management of everyday challenges
  • Cooperation in leisure activities
  • Assumption of ministry and reference work

Your profile

  • Completed training as a FaBe / FaGe EFZ or similar training in the social or health sector
  • Experience and interest in accompanying and caring for people with disabilities and challenging behavior
  • Solution-oriented working style
  • Engaged, resilient and humorous personality

Benefits

  • Work in a modern new building with spacious and bright premises
  • Micro-dwellings
  • Offering of technical education and training
  • Co-creation opportunities
  • No night service operations
