Full description

Lucerne Psychiatry AG is a teaching hospital of the University of Lucerne and is represented with its offers at various locations in the Canton of Lucerne and in Sarnen, Canton of Obwalden. More than 1,600 employees from a wide range of professional groups and departments are humanely and competently committed to the well-being of patients. The offers are aimed at children, adolescents and adults up to old age. Personnel patient administration 80-100% (m/f/d)

Their tasks

  • Tariff-compliant billing of patient services
  • Processing of bill rejections
  • Care of the switch incl. Processing of stationary entrances (1 day per week)
  • Ensure full cost recovery for inpatient treatment
  • General office work (mail and postal services and information to internal and external bodies)
  • System maintenance and further development of the administration system (Nexus Hospis Pat NG)
  • Cooperation in organizational, process and application optimizations

Your profile

  • Commercial education with health professional experience or equivalent qualifications
  • In-depth knowledge of Swiss tariffs
  • Good communication skills and high social skills
  • Networked thinking, independence and personal responsibility
  • High affinity for IT applications

Benefits
