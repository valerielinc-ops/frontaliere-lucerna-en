Full description

This is what awaits you

You want to further expand your experience in the insurance industry and are an absolute team player:in? You enjoy customer contact, work reliably and keep an overview even in a lively working day? Then you are exactly right with us.

This is what you do with us

You are in the middle of the sales process and ensure that our insurance and pension advisors are optimally supported.

With your organizational talent, you create offers, process applications and coordinate appointments across all insurance sectors. At the same time, you actively contribute your sales flair. You recognize customer needs, think along and contribute to the emergence of convincing solutions from contacts.

Whether on the phone, at the reception on the general agency or in exchange with internal and external contacts - you meet people openly, service-oriented and with much pleasure. In this way, you make an important contribution to a positive customer experience and to the joint success of our general agency.

This is what you bring with you

  • Commercial training completed
  • Experience in the insurance industry or a comparable service environment
  • Joy of contact with customers
  • Sales and customer-oriented personality
  • Self-employed and responsible work
  • Security in dealing with office products as well as a high digital affinity
  • Flowing knowledge of German

Working with us

At the general agency Willisau-Entlebuch you will be received by a motivated and collegial team. Excellent working conditions and a modern working environment await you.

With 46 employees – including five apprentices – and our own claims department on site, we are strongly anchored in the region. Together we are committed to being a personal and reliable partner for our customers.

The Insurance Supervision Act requires insurance intermediaries to perform this function:in VBV Profile Allbranche. If this is not available, we support with tailor-made internal training.

This is what we offer

Participation in success

If we are successful, our employees also benefit. Each year, the distribution of a successful share and its amount is decided.

Training and development

We offer a wide range of internal courses. We participate in job-relevant training.

Advantages

Among other things, we offer up to 25% discount on bonuses, discounts on mobile phones and free subscriptions with flat rate and discounts at cultural events.
