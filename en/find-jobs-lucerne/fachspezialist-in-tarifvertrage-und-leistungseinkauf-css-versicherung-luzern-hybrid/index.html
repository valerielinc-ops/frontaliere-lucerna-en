Full description

From you comes a concentrated load of knowledge and the desire to advance us further. And an idea of how you want to shape your life. Take advantage of the opportunity to promote service purchasing, the basis for contract negotiations, and the networking of different service providers. From us comes a first-class employment package and the flexibility so that you bring your job and private life well. Let’s talk about a common future.

This is what your everyday life looks like

  • At the heart of it: You develop basic documents for service purchase, for example model contracts.
  • In the field of collective agreements, you will analyze current case law and be the contact person for legal issues.
  • When developing negotiation strategies, especially in the basic insurance sector, you actively participate.
  • In exchange with service providers, you clarify fundamental questions between the CSS performance billing and search for solutions together with the key account managers.
  • In the implementation of new tariff structures, you work and create appropriate sample documents.
  • With your figures flair, you analyze key figures of service providers and support the creation of negotiation guidelines.

You bring that with you

  • First things first: You have a university or university of applied sciences degree, ideally in business administration/economics, political sciences or law.
  • For interdisciplinary questions at the interface of health economics, law, politics and medicine you bring real interest.
  • Practice in health or insurance you have already collected or want to build it specifically.
  • With your strong analytical skills and quick comprehension, you quickly permeate complex collective bargaining and contract issues.
  • You communicate in German at native language level – every other national language is a plus.
  • Work Flexible: Adapt your work to your lifestyle annually with part-time options and other flexible models like home office and more.
  • Enjoy your wallet: Benefits such as bonus, Reka, discounted health insurance premium and others that quickly make up a few thousands a year.
  • Choose your holiday model: 5 or 6 weeks holiday? Your decision. Accordingly, you work 41 or 42 hours a week.
  • Improve yourself further: We like to see it if you educate yourself internally or externally and support generously with time and money.
  • Stay fit: Ergonomic workplaces, promotion of mental well-being and many other offers around your health.
  • Engage Socially: Does Life Mean Good to You? Then let others share your happiness and expand your horizons alone or in a team.
