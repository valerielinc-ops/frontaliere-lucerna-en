Full description

The Lucerne Canton Hospital with its locations in Lucerne, Sursee and Wolhusen is a company of the LUKS Group and thus belongs to one of the leading hospital groups in Switzerland. The more than 8,500 employees are there with warmth, competence and commitment around the clock for the well-being of patients. The clinics and institutes of the LUKS Group offer medical services of the highest quality.

For our team we are looking for reinforcement immediately or by agreement. Tasks: • In the tandem model, they work closely with our care professionals HF/FH and accompany our patients in their day-to-day care. • You support our patients in personal care, mobilisation and wearing and taking on nursing tasks as part of their training and on delegation • They accompany patients to therapies and examinations and respond sensitively to their individual needs • They support the day-to-day work of the station with organizational and logistical tasks such as filling up materials, distributing meals, errands and other domestic activities Requirements • You are trained as an assistant or Assistant Health and Social Affairs (AGS) EBA successfully completed • They meet patients with appreciation, empathy and high social skills and enjoy working in direct contact with people. • You are a resilient, flexible and reliable personality who likes to work in a team and takes responsibility in your own area of responsibility. • You are interested in active participation in day-to-day care, therapy and activation and carry out your tasks carefully and according to your competence profile We offer: • A progressive hospital awaits you, which will take a big step towards the future with its strategic orientation towards the Lean Hospital and the new hospital building, while you can expect a varied and challenging activity. • You work in a highly professional team with a flat hierarchy and culture and have the opportunity to actively shape the healthcare sector with your commitment • In addition to the beautiful place of work in nature, they benefit from very good social benefits as well as an active promotion of your personal further education, whether internally or externally, as well as the opportunity to expand and deepen your professional knowledge.
