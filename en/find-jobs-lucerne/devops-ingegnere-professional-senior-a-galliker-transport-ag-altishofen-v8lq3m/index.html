DevOps / Container Platform Engineer - Professional/Senior 80-100% (a)

You want to create tangible improvements for our software development teams and take responsibility with automation, GitOps and CI/CD? In this role, you actively contribute to the deployment and operation of our applications on OpenShift. As an interface between developers, architects, security and external partners, you continue to develop platform, processes and services, bring together perspectives and make decisions. You have plenty of room for your own ideas and your professional and personal development.

Galliker is one of the leading Swiss logistics and transport companies with national and international locations. From the Altis-Osboen site, a team of around 80 IT specialists serves over 20 branches throughout Europe and ensures smooth workflows in everyday work thanks to the most modern technology, the culture of ideas and strong teamwork. As a computer science team, we strive daily to bring technology and people together to deliver fast, high-quality and reliable software to our internal and external customers.

If you have the start-up/scale-up gene in you, improvement by simplification is your engine and you are excited about our culture of ownership, collaboration and transparency for best performance, then you are our DevOps engineer personality that we are looking for.

Field of responsibility

  • Responsibility for the operation and further development of central platform components such as OpenShift, Container Registry and Monitoring (On-Premise)
  • Structure, maintenance and further development of standardized build, test and release processes
  • Support and gradual modernization of our existing DevOps tool (TeamCity, IIS)
  • Proactive incident management to ensure stability and performance
  • Ensuring security, compliance and governance requirements in the deployment environment
  • Evaluation of new technologies and tools to continuously improve the performance of the

Platform and processes

  • Creation and maintenance of technical documentation and best practice
  • Support of our development teams in containerization, deployment and operation of their applications
  • Close cooperation with development, architecture and IT security

Requirements profile

  • Bachelor's degree in computer science, engineering or comparable qualification
  • Proven professional experience as DevOps Engineer or in a similar role in software development
  • Understanding for build, test and release processes in software projects
  • Proven experience with container technologies (e.g. Docker, kubernetes, OpenShift)
  • Experience with CI/CD systems, ideally ArgoCD, Tekton and TeamCity as well as with Git.
  • Knowledge in the management and securing of container registries (Harbor)
  • Experience with monitoring and logging solutions (ELK stack, Sentry)
  • Confident of security and compliance requirements in the container environment
  • Experience with C# and/or React Advantage
  • Quick perception, high analytical thinking and abstraction ability
  • Dedicated ability to keep DevOps processes simple and to focus on added value
  • Very good problem solving competence and security-conscious
  • Good, structured communication skills and proven experience of addressate-suited

Knowledge transfer to platform and processes at different target groups

  • Team-oriented, proactive working methods and high self motivation
  • Experience with ailan working methods and prioritization
  • Very good German and English knowledge in word and writing

Offer

  • Exciting transport and logistics projects
  • Cool, motivated team
  • Top modern IT infrastructures
  • Lots of personal responsibility and appreciation
  • Wide range of applications in the world of transport & logistics with international contacts in branches
  • Possibility of occasional remote work
