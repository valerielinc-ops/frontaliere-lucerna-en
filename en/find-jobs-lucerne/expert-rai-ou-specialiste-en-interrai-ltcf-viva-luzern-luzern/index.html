Full description

You are actively involved in the implementation of interRAI LTCF maintenance needs assessment – including training, guidance, monitoring and controlling.

  • You work in the team of the specialist office RAI. Together you are responsible for the assessment of care needs at Viva Lucerne.
  • You recognize the need for training among employees and initiate targeted measures for skills development.
  • You coach the RAI supervisors in the living areas and strengthen their professional safety.
  • You take on internal controlling tasks in connection with interRAI LTCF and ensure the quality of the data.
  • You code interRAI LTCF assessments competently and reliably.
  • You are the contact person for employees, residents, relatives and health insurers with questions about interRAI LTCF.
  • Requirements
  • Completed training as a RAI expert / RAI expert interRAI LTCF
  • In-depth experience with the interRAI LTCF classification system as well as a very high level of competence in the coding of RAI assessments
  • Very good knowledge in MS Office, RAIsoft and nursing documentation systems is mandatory
  • Strong social competence as well as the ability to coach, motivate and accompany employees
  • Pleasure in the interdisciplinary cooperation and cooperation with various nursing teams
  • Ideally, you have training in adult education or practical experience in the planning and implementation of training for groups
  • Cosa offriamo - Generous participation in external training and further education - Extensive internal advice in career planning
