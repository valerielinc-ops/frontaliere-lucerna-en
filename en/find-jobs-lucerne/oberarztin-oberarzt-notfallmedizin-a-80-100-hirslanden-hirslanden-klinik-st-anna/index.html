Full description

Location: Hirslanden Clinic St. Anna | Lucerne

Occupation by 1 November 2026 or by arrangement

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 73847

You work with heart and expertise in emergency medicine and want an environment in which good medicine and an appreciative cooperation count equally? Then we would like to meet you.

Our interdisciplinary emergency center at Hirslanden Klinik St. Anna offers a wide range of clinical services and a varied range of activities in acute medicine. Together, we ensure that our patients feel well looked after both medically and humanly in challenging situations.

To complement our specialist team, we are looking for a committed personality who takes responsibility, enjoys working in a team and wants to actively contribute her experience and her ideas.

This is what awaits you

  • You support our emergency patients independently, competently and with an eye for the essentials
  • You work closely with the various disciplines – uncomplicated, collegial and at eye level
  • You accompany assistant doctors in their further education and are happy to pass on your knowledge
  • You are involved in the further development of our emergency center and actively shape processes, quality and medical standards
  • If you have special interests – for example, emergency sonography, simulation, triage, teaching or process development – we are happy to create space for this.
  • You support our management team on medical, organizational and strategic issues

You bring that with you

  • Completed specialist training in general internal medicine or anesthesiology
  • Clinical experience and pleasure in emergency and acute medicine
  • Safety in clinical everyday life, a sense of responsibility and the ability to keep track even in dynamic situations
  • Joy of interdisciplinary cooperation and an open, respectful exchange
  • Communication, organisational skills and flexibility
  • But above all: a collegial personality that combines professional quality with humanity and team spirit

Why you can feel comfortable with us

It is important to us that you do not just start a job, but become part of a team in which you support each other. You can expect a versatile job with a lot of personal responsibility, modern work equipment and short decision paths.

  • A committed, interprofessional team with an open and appreciative cooperation
  • A broad spectrum of acute medicine and a varied clinical everyday life
  • POCUS ultrasound including thorax and cardiac sonography as an integral part of clinical everyday life
  • Opportunities for professional and personal development as well as for active participation
  • Space for your own ideas, interests and focuses
  • Attractive employee offers and a place to work in Lucerne – with the city, lake and mountains right outside the door

Have you become curious?

We look forward to hearing from you. We are happy to tell you more about our team, our emergency center and what awaits you with us.
