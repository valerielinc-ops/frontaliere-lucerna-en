Full description

As one of the world’s leading clinics, we develop an individualized treatment program with the aim of providing our patients with the highest possible degree of independence and freedom of life, thereby optimizing their quality of life. We address both patients from the Swiss cash system and international patients. cereneo offers highly specialized rehabilitation programs for people with neurological diseases and injuries, including stroke, brain injury, neurodegenerative diseases such as Parkinson’s, multiple sclerosis and many other neurological disorders.

Tasks

The aim of this internship is an introduction to neuropsychological diagnosis and therapy in adult patients in inpatient neuro-rehab setting. You will receive an introduction to the clinical-diagnostic neuropsychological activity, learning and carrying out a detailed hypothesis-guided mental status with anamnesis, application of test psychology methods, getting to know and carry out various therapy procedures, as well as creating reports and getting to know the clinical everyday life in neuropsychology in inpatient neuro-rehab.

What should you bring?

  • Bachelor's degree in psychology, possibly in advanced master's studies
  • Good basic knowledge in neuroscience, especially neuropsychology
  • Motivation to work independently
  • Good knowledge of German and English
