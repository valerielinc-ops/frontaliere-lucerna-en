Full description

For you, technology is more than just functioning? In Emmen, you work on modern, highly automated production, filling and packaging systems that make high-quality Emmi products possible every day. Together with an experienced team, you ensure that our systems run reliably, are continuously optimized and meet the highest quality and safety standards. Your know-how, your attention to detail and your commitment to day-to-day operations make a tangible contribution to the success of our location.

You can do that.

  • Maintain our filling, production, packaging and process plants, support new installations and conversions, and document all relevant steps for quality assurance.
  • Be ready for day-to-day operation with occasional piketting service and continuously optimize our plants and components to ensure smooth processes.
  • Use your metalworking skills (turning, milling, drilling, welding) and find solutions to technical challenges with a structured approach.
  • Work closely with technical purchasing to efficiently procure required spare parts, always complying with all safety guidelines.
  • Get involved in the project EOE (OPTIMO, KAIZEN, KVP) and take on further tasks to support Emmi process management.

You bring that with you

  • You have completed EFZ training as a polymechanic: in maintenance, agricultural/construction machinery mechanic: in or comparable qualifications.
  • Experience in the maintenance of production plants is an advantage, but motivated lateral entrants are also welcome!
  • Readiness for piquet and occasional weekend assignments.
  • Security awareness – your and your colleagues’ safety comes first!
  • Communication strength and team spirit are essential in our environment.
  • Good German skills in word and writing and office programs can be used well.

This is what we offer you

  • 5 weeks of vacation: Enjoy five weeks of vacation per year. From the age of 46, your holiday entitlement gradually increases to up to six weeks.
  • Attractive pension fund with flexible savings plans: Our Emmi pension foundation pays interest on your retirement balance above the legal minimum. Your contribution is based on your annual salary – without coordination deduction. Choose between standard or plus savings plan with 1% higher savings contributions.
  • Voluntary Success Sharing: Depending on the business success, you will receive a voluntary success Sharing. In doing so, we want to acknowledge your valuable contribution to the company’s success.
  • Individual development plan: With tailor-made development plans, we specifically promote your individual skills and support your long-term growth – a central component of our sustainability strategy. Learn more .
  • Cheap meals in the staff restaurant: For breakfast, lunch and break meals, a staff restaurant with discounted employee prices is available.
  • Free parking: We offer our employees free parking at the location.
