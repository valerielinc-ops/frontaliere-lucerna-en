Full description

Looking forward to work: 100% healthy Hirschmattstrasse 25, 6003 Lucerne Do you want to actively shape the further development of our central specialist application Lifeware Leben? You can accommodate the needs of different stakeholders, prioritize them sensibly and ensure implementation? Then we look forward to you.

This is what awaits you

  • As a product owner, you take on the professional responsibility for the lifeware system and develop goals and priorities together with the relevant stakeholders.
  • You take needs from the specialist areas, clarify requirements and bring them into an understandable, implementable and testable form.
  • You control the external provider Lifeware as part of the ongoing supplier management and ensure targeted cooperation.
  • You coordinate specialist tests and acceptances together with the participating employees from the departments.
  • You make sure that agreed services, dates and requirements are adhered to.
  • With suitable key figures and evaluations, you create transparency and well-founded decision-making bases.

You bring that with you

  • Completed training in business informatics or comparable education or training.
  • A good understanding of business processes, IT systems, interfaces and data.
  • Ideally, knowledge in the field of life or private provision.
  • A strong communication strength in dealing with internal departments, IT and external partners.
  • A hands-on mentality as well as joy in pragmatically and bindingly advancing topics.
  • Very good knowledge of German and ideally good knowledge of English.
