Full description

Luzerner Psychiatrie AG is a teaching hospital of the University of Lucerne, with services at various locations in the Canton of Lucerne and in Sarnen in the Canton of Obwalden. More than 1,600 employees from a wide range of professional groups and specialist fields are committed, with humanity and expertise, to the well-being of patients. The services are aimed at children, adolescents and adults through to old age.

Psychologist, Outpatient Transition Psychiatry 80-100% (m/f/d)

Your responsibilities

  • Conducting psychiatric assessments of adolescents and young adults
  • Integrated psychiatric and psychotherapeutic treatment of adolescents and their families during the transition to adulthood (transition psychiatry)
  • Participation in case discussions
  • Active coordination with internal interfaces to child and adolescent psychiatry (KJPD), adult psychiatry and the Crisis Intervention Centre (KIZ)

Your profile

  • Advanced or completed postgraduate training in psychotherapy
  • At least 1-2 years of experience in outpatient and/or inpatient child and adolescent psychiatry and/or adult psychiatry
  • Communicative, flexible and open personality with an independent working style and the ability to work across departments and in a networked manner.
  • Enjoyment of and curiosity about the spectrum of social psychiatry and working with people in adolescence

Benefits

  • Participation in external group supervision and continuing education programmes
  • At least 5 weeks of vacation, loyalty day from the 3rd year of service
  • Possibility of unpaid leave/sabbatical
  • Attractive social benefits (coverage of the KTG premium and two-thirds of the PK premium)
