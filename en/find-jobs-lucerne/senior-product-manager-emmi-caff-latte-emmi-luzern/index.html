Full description

A strong brand is looking for a strong personality! Ready to develop one of Switzerland’s most popular brands? You are responsible for the entire product management at home and abroad. Your role as a first point of contact in the product management area makes you an indispensable hub between different departments and positions.

You can do that.

  • You assume responsibility for the entire product management of Emmi CAFFÈ LATTE for the Swiss and international markets.
  • You plan, coordinate and manage product management projects on time and on time – in close cooperation with production, marketing, procurement and suppliers.
  • You take over the project management in the area of packaging, relaunches or product innovations as well as the partial project management in higher-level project committees and lead the overall overview of all product management projects.
  • You are responsible for the entire product management budget.
  • You carry the professional leadership of a product manager and are responsible for efficient project allocation.

You bring that with you

  • You bring several years of experience in FMCG product management.
  • You actively participate, act forward-looking and take responsibility – Your own initiative is a matter of course for you.
  • You will bring experience in managing challenging projects and stakeholder management.
  • You feel very comfortable in a networking role and keep an overview even in hectic moments at all times.
  • Your way of working is detailed and efficient.
  • You speak fluent German and English – every other language is a plus.

This is what we offer you

  • 6 weeks vacation: Enjoy six weeks vacation per year. From the age of 46, your holiday entitlement gradually increases to up to seven weeks.
  • Sabbatical & Holiday Shopping: Give yourself unpaid holidays or extend your holiday entitlement by up to two additional weeks.
  • Flexible working time models: We offer various working time models, such as flexibilities or trusted working hours, in order to meet the personal needs and wishes of our employees.
  • Home Office: With us you can flexibly spend up to 40% of your working time (at a 100% position) in the home office, whereby we support you as an employer with the technical equipment for your home office.
  • Attractive pension fund with flexible savings plans: Our Emmi pension foundation pays interest on your retirement savings above the legal minimum. Your contribution is based on your annual salary – without coordination deduction. Choose between standard or plus savings plan with 1% higher savings contributions.
  • Further education and development opportunities: At Emmi, the focus is on the further development of our employees: We offer talent programs, support training and further education and promote all skills for your professional growth. Find out more about our development offers here.
