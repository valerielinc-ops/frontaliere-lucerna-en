Full description

Become part of our team and immerse yourself in the world of modern production and packaging plants – where you can make a difference every day with your commitment to quality, safety and efficiency.

You can do that.

  • Equip the production lines in time with the required cheese.
  • Make sure that all goods and materials are properly supplied in the evening.
  • Store semi-finished products correctly and error-free.
  • Monitor the truck blade and check the receipt of goods according to delivery note and labeling.
  • Transport finished pallets and provide empty containers for production.
  • Order empty containers as needed and ensure their correct storage.

Why do you succeed?

  • Your experience in the production or food industry makes you a valuable addition to our team.
  • A forklift pass (SUVA-approved) is an advantage and helps you to perform tasks safely and efficiently.
  • Discover new things with an open mindset that promotes innovation and progress.
  • Show your flexibility by getting excited about the challenges of shift work (2-3 shift system).
  • You have good knowledge of German to ensure smooth cooperation.
  • Use your experience with SAP to operate efficiently and accurately in the digital work environment.

This is what we offer you

  • 5 weeks of vacation: Enjoy five weeks of vacation per year. From the age of 46, your holiday entitlement gradually increases to up to six weeks.
  • Voluntary Success Sharing: Depending on the business success, you will receive a voluntary success Sharing. In doing so, we want to acknowledge your valuable contribution to the company’s success.
  • Attractive pension fund with flexible savings plans: Our Emmi pension foundation pays interest on your retirement savings above the legal minimum. Your contribution is based on your annual salary – without coordination deduction. Choose between standard or plus savings plan with 1% higher savings contributions.
  • Voluntary child allowance: If you earn less than CHF 5,000, you will receive a voluntary allowance of CHF 100 per month for each child.
  • Further education and development opportunities: At Emmi, the focus is on the further development of our employees: We offer talent programs, support training and further education and promote all skills for your professional growth. Find out more about our development offers here.
  • Cheap meals in the staff restaurant: For breakfast, lunch and break meals, a staff restaurant with discounted employee prices is available.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Lucerne?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Lucerne's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Lucerne's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
