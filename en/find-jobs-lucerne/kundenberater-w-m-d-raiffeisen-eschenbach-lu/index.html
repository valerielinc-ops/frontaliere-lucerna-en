16.09.2026 Eschenbach LU

Become part of our team!

For Raiffeisenbank Oberseetal, a successful and locally oriented bank with offices in Hochdorf, Eschenbach and Inwil, we are looking for a personality as a customer advisor.

What do you expect?

  • Reception and support of customers at the bank counter
  • Advising customers in the basic products Saving, paying, investing and provisioning
  • Demand recognition and forwarding of customers to the responsible specialist consultants
  • Recognizing and exploiting customer potential
  • Expansion of the existing customer base through targeted acquisition of new customers
  • Cooperation in target group-specific market development measures

What do you bring?

  • Completed banking training or comparable training with experience in the financial performance sector
  • Independent and goal-oriented working method as well as pleasure in customer contact
  • Sympathetic and winning appearance
  • Reliability, flexibility and teamwork

We advise our customers with passion, competence and joy. Are you flexible, committed and love customer contact? We look forward to your application! Armin Müller Member of the bank management Why Raiffeisen?
