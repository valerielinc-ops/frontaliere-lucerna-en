Full description

Every day, around 3,000 employees of RUAG and RUAG Real Estate make a significant contribution to the security of Switzerland. They ensure that the Swiss Army and other operational and security organisations can perform their tasks comprehensively at any time.

You can move that

  • Supporting ICT architecture projects and advising specialist areas and stakeholders on ICT architecture issues
  • Documentation of existing ICT architectures and participation in the development of ICT target architectures in the ICT architecture
  • Analyzing business processes, organizational structures, computer systems and embedded systems with regard to their interactions
  • Applying and developing principles and guidelines for the ICT architecture under guidance
  • Participation in ICT architecture projects for the development of secure and flexible system landscapes using agile methods such as Scrum or SAFe
  • Model-based capture of actual ICT architectures and participation in defining ICT target architectures based on meta-models and ICT architecture frameworks (e.g. TOGAF, ArchiMate)
  • Timely, in the required quality and within the budget framework completion of allocated work packages in projects
  • Communicate with internal stakeholders and prepare the basis for decision-making on ICT architecture issues

You bring that with you

  • Completed studies in computer science, business informatics or similar training
  • Architectural or project certification is advantageous
  • Several years of experience as a solution architect
  • In-depth knowledge of enterprise and solution architecture, integration and security concepts as well as TOGAF, ArchiMate or comparable frameworks
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing, English is advantageous
  • Structured, analytical and solution-oriented working method with experience in agile methods as well as governance, compliance and security
  • Communication-strong, convincing and team-oriented personality with high personal responsibility and strategic thinking skills

Don't you meet all the requirements one hundred percent? Apply anyway. We are always looking for talented and motivated people who want to enrich our team. We attach great importance to diversity and equal opportunities and our goal is to build a diverse team in which everyone can fully develop their personal strengths.
