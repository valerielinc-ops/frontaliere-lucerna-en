Full description

We focus on proximity and openness: At the city administration we live the du-culture – already in the application process. Lead with expertise, take responsibility and actively shape the future of social services.

Your tasks

  • You lead the legal service with 5 employees professionally and personnel and are responsible for the further development of the team.
  • You will continuously develop structures, processes and workflows and optimize the interfaces to different contact groups in order to ensure efficient organization of social services.
  • With your conceptual skills, you contribute to the further development of guidelines, standards and strategic foundations and contribute significantly to the quality and legal certainty of social services.
  • You advise and support the employees of the social services in challenging legal issues and provide targeted training for knowledge transfer.
  • The development of demanding legal basis for decision-making is just as much part of your area of responsibility as the writing of decisions, legal documents and expert reports as the processing of objections, consultations, lawsuits and criminal complaints.
  • In civil and administrative proceedings, you represent the social services vis-à-vis court and administrative authorities.

Your profile

  • You have a completed study of law, of advantage with a lawyer's patent.
  • You have profound professional experience in the social sector and are familiar with the legal framework of a public administration.
  • You bring leadership experience or are motivated to take the next step into a leadership function.
  • You have experience in administrative, civil procedure and social security law and enjoy solving complex legal issues in a practical manner.
  • As a solution-oriented, service-conscious personality, you communicate in style and enjoy working in an interdisciplinary manner.
  • You are digitally affine, safe with the common Office applications and ideally familiar with tutoris.
