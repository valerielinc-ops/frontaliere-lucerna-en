Vehicle locksmith (m/f/d | 100%) HESS - The Swiss pioneer in vehicle construction Carrosserie Rothenburg is a branch of HESS AG, one of the leading Swiss vehicle manufacturers, and offers a varied working environment. In Rothenburg, our customers have at their disposal a comprehensive range of repairs and services for vehicles of all kinds - from buses and commercial vehicles to passenger cars and campers. Our orders are as diverse as the vehicles themselves. To complement our Repair & Service division, we are looking for a qualified vehicle locksmith or carrosseries commuter for the Carrosserie Rothenburg branch (LU). Do you have a passion for vehicles and would like to contribute your expertise to the commercial vehicle world? Then this is your chance! Apply today and become part of our dedicated team in Rothenburg (LU). Their tasks

  • Independent and professional execution of various repair work on buses and commercial vehicles
  • Welding, straightening, buckling, assembly and repair work
  • Carrying out installation and conversion work on the Passenger Stram bus
  • Dismantling, repair and assembly of car body parts
  • Execution of varied and challenging locksmith work Our requirements
  • Completed apprenticeship as a vehicle maker, carrosseries commuter, metalworker or other vocational training in a metalworking profession
  • Several years of professional experience are beneficial
  • High degree of independence and customer orientation
  • Teamwork, commitment and initiative
  • Motivated to learn new things We offer them
  • Interesting, varied and challenging activity
  • Moving working time
  • Performance-based conditions of employment
  • Opportunity for further education and development If you feel addressed, send us your complete application documents including letters of motivation via link (Apply now).
