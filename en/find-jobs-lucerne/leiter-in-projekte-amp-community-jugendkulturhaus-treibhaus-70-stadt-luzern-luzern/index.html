Full description

Do you want to shape the youth and cultural scene and get involved in the greenhouse? Then apply now and bring in your ideas.

Tasks

  • You develop, plan and realize socio-cultural projects for young people and young adults and promote their active participation.
  • With "Participation" projects you inspire young people for the greenhouse and its offers.
  • You will hold presentations at schools, network with youth meetings and build cooperation with relevant partner organisations.
  • Community building is more than just a buzzword for you: you strengthen existing networks, develop new forms of participation and promote a vibrant community.
  • Together with young people, you plan and organize the supporting programme in the afternoon, such as learning cafés, workshops or other low-threshold offers.
  • At events, you are present as an accompanying person and provide a supportive and connecting atmosphere on several evenings per month.

Requirements

  • You have a degree, ideally in socio-cultural animation, social work or pedagogy.
  • You already have experience in project work and ideally in the youth or cultural sector.
  • Young people inspire you. You approach them openly and manage to win them over to projects and offers.
  • As a creative and initiative person, it is natural for you to put ideas into action.
  • You work independently, organized and keep track of several projects.
  • You like to move around the Lucerne youth and cultural scene and gladly accompany young adults on their way.
  • Flexible working hours are no problem for you. You also like working irregularly as well as in the evening and on weekends.
