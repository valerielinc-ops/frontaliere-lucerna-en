Full description

In our customer service center and in our agencies, you support our customers during the 3-year training with advice and deeds - verbally and in writing. Your school education takes place in Bern (one to one and a half days a week). In addition, your knowledge is consolidated and trained in intercompany courses in Zurich. As a strong team, we support each other. Successes are best celebrated together. Sounds good? Then let's get to know!

This is what your everyday life looks like

  • Do you love contact with people? Perfect! In the customer dialogue you look after our customers every day – verbally and in writing, in German and French.
  • Your knowledge counts: You give clear and helpful information.
  • With our products and services you are very familiar and can inspire our customers.
  • You process requests in the customer information system.
  • Always a cool head: You are sensitive and helpful even with complaints.
  • You work on exciting projects and contribute your ideas.

You bring that with you

  • The A and O: You enjoy customer contact.
  • You like to think one step further and want to find the best possible solution.
  • You flourish in the team and look forward to celebrating successes together.
  • German and French are yours, whether oral or in writing.
  • With the PC and the IT tools you feel safe.
  • With your good to very good grades, you prove that you are motivated and ambitious.

Good arguments - typical CSS

  • Free travel throughout Switzerland: We give you the GA during your entire training period.
  • Enjoy your holidays: More time for yourself - with 6 weeks of holidays per year.
  • Save money: We fill your Sparkässeli thanks to free school supplies and up to 50% cheaper health insurance premium.
  • Cool occasions for you: Celebrate the start of your education with us, get to know other learners and enjoy CSS events such as the Samichlaus Party.
  • Learn new languages: Receive a grant for your language stay abroad or your language course.
  • Stay fit: We take care of you - with learning sessions on your health and health offers in and outside the workplace.
