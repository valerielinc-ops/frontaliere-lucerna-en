Welcome to our team! Spitex Horw is a future-oriented company with around 50 employees. Enabling our clients to live independently at home is our central concern. Competent and sensitive we advise and support people of all ages. Do you want to actively engage and develop? Do you enjoy a versatile, demanding and independent activity?

We are looking for agreement

Housekeeping specialist EFZ (50-100%)

Your responsibilities

  • Accompaniment, support and relief of elderly, sick and needy persons in the domestic sector.
  • Primary applications: house cleaning, textile care (washing, ironing), food shopping. If possible, all work is done together with the clients.
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation with nurses, relatives and other services.

They bring with them

  • Completed training as a specialist in housekeeping EFZ.

  • You have a valid ticket and have a car available.
  • Good knowledge of German.
  • An independent, responsible way of working.

We offer them

  • A varied task in a dynamic operation.

  • The opportunity to develop your professional and personal skills.
  • They have regular working hours (Monday to Friday on day duty) and benefit from good training opportunities.
  • A collegial, appreciative working atmosphere.
  • A team of 18 people awaits you.
  • Also as a re-entrance / -in are welcome with us.
  • Progressive, family-friendly employment conditions (including 5 weeks holidays).
