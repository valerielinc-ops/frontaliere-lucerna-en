Company and context

  • You will continuously develop the organization and promote digitization and modern forms of work within the service department.
  • You represent the service department vis-à-vis authorities, stakeholders, courts and the public and are responsible for internal and external communication.
  • In addition, you are a member of the extended management of the Financial Directorate and are involved in overarching topics. Requirements
  • We appeal to a convincing leader with the certificate of ability to lead a management office, with a completed study and/or a well-founded, higher education or further education in the legal, business or administrative area.
  • You can have several years of leadership and management experience in comparable organizations and thus have extensive experience in debt recovery and bankruptcy.
  • In addition, you will have in-depth knowledge of the SchKG, the associated federal regulations and the relevant case law.
  • You convince with strong leadership, communication and social skills as well as an appreciative leadership style.
  • You act decisively and appear confidently even in demanding and conflict-laden situations.
  • In addition, you have a high affinity for digitalization topics and experience in optimizing processes and workflows.
  • You think networked, act politically skilled and enjoy working with different stakeholders.
