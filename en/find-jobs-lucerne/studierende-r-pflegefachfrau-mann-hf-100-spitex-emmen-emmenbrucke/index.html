Full description

With around 65 employees, Spitex Emmen is a modern, versatile and committed employer. We provide high-quality ambulatory health care for the approximately 33,000 inhabitants of the municipality of Emmen and actively promote the nursing profession of tomorrow as a training company. We are pleased to be able to accompany regularly established and motivated personalities during their training. For the start of studies in 2026, a space is still available for Student / nurse / -man HF 100 % What awaits you with us

  • a lively, varied working environment – no day is like the other
  • a professional introduction and accompaniment during the entire study period
  • Extension of your experience in an external internship at the Swiss Paraplegic Centre Nottwil
  • large share of independent work with increasing responsibility towards our clients
  • Fitness during work – the eBikes are ready for use

Your tasks after a well-founded introduction with us are

  • Ensuring holistic support, support and care of our clients at home
  • Support and accompaniment of local relatives

What do you bring with you

  • Joy in a lively environment, sense of responsibility, independence as well as a good portion of humor and warmth

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Lucerne's migration office after the contract is signed.
