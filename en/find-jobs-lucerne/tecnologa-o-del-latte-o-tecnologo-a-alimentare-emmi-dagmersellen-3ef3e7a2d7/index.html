Full description

Heart, passion and unforgettable milk moments – this is what Emmi stands for. In Dagmersellen, 100 million liters of milk are processed into the finest cream cheese every year, making us one of the largest mozzarella producers in Switzerland. Become part of our success story.

You can do that.

  • You can reliably prepare our complex plants in the production of mozzarella and cream cheese.
  • You provide all the necessary tools and operate the four different systems.
  • In case of technical problems, you can use your knowledge to solve production problems and support the technicians.
  • You carry out cleaning and maintenance tasks independently.
  • You document your workflows and pass them cleanly to the next shift.

You bring that with you

  • You have the vocational training EFZ (milk or food technology) and perhaps already gained some experience as a plant manager: in a food business.
  • You show your flexibility with your readiness for shift work (3 shifts: 20:00
  • 4:00, Mo, 4:00
  • 13:00 and 12:00
  • 20:00 / seasonal assignments on Saturday).
  • You enjoy working in a team, are open, collegial, helpful and enjoy learning.
  • You master the German language in word and writing and you are technically interested.

This is what we offer you

  • 5 weeks of vacation: Enjoy five weeks of vacation per year. From the age of 46, your holiday entitlement gradually increases to up to six weeks.
  • Attractive pension fund with flexible savings plans: Our Emmi pension foundation pays interest on your retirement savings above the legal minimum. Your contribution is based on your annual salary – without coordination deduction. Choose between standard or plus savings plan with 1% higher savings contributions.
  • Voluntary Success Sharing: Depending on the business success, you will receive a voluntary success Sharing. In doing so, we want to acknowledge your valuable contribution to the company’s success.
  • Voluntary child allowance: If you earn less than CHF 5,000, you will receive a voluntary allowance of CHF 100 per month for each child.
  • Free parking: We offer our employees free parking at the location.
  • Cheap meals in the staff restaurant: For breakfast, lunch and break meals, a staff restaurant with discounted employee prices is available.
