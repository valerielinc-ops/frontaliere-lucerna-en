Full description

Good cheese requires more than the best milk. It needs people like you! With your technical understanding and your careful work, you ensure that our high-quality cheese products find their way to our customers every day in the best quality. Become part of our team and help make cheese enjoyable.

You can do that.

  • You serve, set up and monitor our modern facilities and ensure a smooth production process.
  • You ensure compliance with our quality, hygiene, safety and environmental standards according to Emmi Process Management (EPM).
  • You carry out quality controls and ensure the high quality of our products.
  • You recognize and repair disruptions in the production process and support the optimization of plants and processes.
  • You clean and maintain the production facilities and the working environment and control the production orders.

You bring that with you

  • Ideally, you have completed vocational training as a plant manager: in EFZ or in a technical or food-related area.
  • You bring experience from a production company, ideally from the food industry.
  • You have technical understanding and enjoy operating and setting up production facilities.
  • You are ready to work in 2- to 3-shift operation.
  • You communicate safely in German (minor level B1/B2) and convince through your reliable and team-oriented work method.

This is what we offer you

  • 5 weeks of vacation: Enjoy five weeks of vacation per year. From the age of 46, your holiday entitlement gradually increases to up to six weeks.
  • Sabbatical & Holiday Shopping: Give yourself unpaid holidays or extend your holiday entitlement by up to two additional weeks.
  • Attractive pension fund with flexible savings plans: Our Emmi pension foundation pays interest on your retirement savings above the legal minimum. Your contribution is based on your annual salary – without coordination deduction. Choose between standard or plus savings plan with 1% higher savings contributions.
  • Voluntary Success Sharing: Depending on the business success, you will receive a voluntary success Sharing. In doing so, we want to acknowledge your valuable contribution to the company’s success.
  • Further education and development opportunities: At Emmi, the focus is on the further development of our employees: We offer talent programs, support training and further education and promote all skills for your professional growth. Find out more about our development offers here.
  • Cheap meals in the staff restaurant: For breakfast, lunch and break meals, a staff restaurant with discounted employee prices is available.
