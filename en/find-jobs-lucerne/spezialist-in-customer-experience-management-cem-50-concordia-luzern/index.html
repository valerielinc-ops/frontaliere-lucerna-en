Full description

Looking forward to work: 100% healthy Bundesplatz 15, 6002 Lucerne You want to know how our customers really experience CONCORDIA? And do you like to make sure that their feedback becomes concrete improvements? As a CEM specialist, you take over this newly created function with a lot of creative freedom in an attractive amount of 50%. In the product management team, you bring the voice of customers into marketing and into the entire CONCORDIA.

This is what awaits you

  • You analyze existing customer journeys, uncover action areas and trigger improvements together with the business units. You accompany their implementation and thus establish a continuous improvement process step by step.
  • You will continuously collect customer feedback via our CX measurement and use it to optimize selected journeys and touchpoints.
  • You will continue to develop the CX measurement, establish new use cases and touchpoint measurements and work closely with external partners.
  • You systematically ask for notice reasons, evaluate them and derive the necessary measures – so that our notice rate remains low.
  • You promote positive customer reviews and define the process of responding to positive and negative feedback.
  • You keep an eye on customer satisfaction and customer feedback, review customer satisfaction studies and market comparisons and report on customer satisfaction.
  • You bring the customer and market view into the development of products and services as well as into processes and projects and strengthen customer orientation throughout the company.

You bring that with you

  • Customers are the focus for you. You listen carefully, put yourself in their situation and want to noticeably improve their experience.
  • You think analytically, like to work with data and feedback and derive concrete, implementable measures from it.
  • You are solution-oriented, tackle and stick to it until improvements are actually implemented.
  • You convince internal and external partners, enjoy working across teams and business units and win others over for your ideas.
  • You work independently and structured and set clear priorities.
  • You bring a higher education in business administration, marketing, market research or psychology – or a comparable qualification. Further training in customer experience management is a plus.
  • You have several years of experience in CEM, market research, product management or marketing and know methods such as customer journey analysis and CX measurement.
  • Knowledge of health insurance or the insurance and financial industry is beneficial.
  • You communicate stylishly in German, good knowledge of French is an advantage. You are confident in MS Office, especially Excel, and ideally have experience with feedback and analysis tools.

Even if you do not fully meet all requirements, we look forward to your application. Your place of work
