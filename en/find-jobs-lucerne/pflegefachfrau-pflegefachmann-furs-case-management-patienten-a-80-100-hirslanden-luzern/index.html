Full description

Location: Hirslanden Clinic St. Anna | Lucerne

Occupation by 1 January 2027 or by arrangement

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 72941

Working at Hirslanden Clinic St. Anna means more than just working. The cornerstone of our trusting and close cooperation is the family coexistence that we maintain together with our patients.

We provide our patients with seamless follow-up care after surgery, illnesses or accidents and thus contribute to the excellent quality level of our clinic.

YOUR TASKS

  • You will advise and inform about social, personal, financial and legal issues in the context of hospital stay and exit planning. In addition, you organize customized connection solutions, provide assistance if necessary and triage to suitable specialist positions
  • You maintain contacts and cooperation with external institutions and service providers
  • You can expect close interdisciplinary cooperation with the entire medical, nursing and therapeutic treatment team.
  • You have an active role in the design and further development of the Case Management service for our patients

Your profile

  • Completed training as a Dipl. Nurse with experience
  • Ideally, you have experience in the case management of an acute hospital or even have a completed further education in case management
  • Good knowledge of health and social security
  • Empathetic and integrating personality with high social competence
  • You like to work independently, but also in a team. You are solution-oriented and take responsibility
  • With your humorous and positioned nature you enrich our team

Our offer

  • Family environment with proximity to employees and patients
  • Balanced food option for only CHF 5.00 per meal
  • Paid changing time in the form of up to 4 additional free days
  • Support for further education and training, e.g. CAS Case Management
