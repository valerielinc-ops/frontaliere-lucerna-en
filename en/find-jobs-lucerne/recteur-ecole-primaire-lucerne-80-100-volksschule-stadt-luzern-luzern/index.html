Full description

We focus on proximity and openness: At the city administration we live the du-culture – already in the application process. Design the elementary school Lucerne of tomorrow. Bring your vision, your leadership and your will to create and move the future of education together with a dedicated team. It awaits you a key position with great creative leeway in the exciting environment of education, politics and administration.

Your tasks

  • You bear the overall responsibility for a high-quality elementary school in the city of Lucerne.
  • You lead the six-member management of the elementary school Stadt Lucerne and are also a member of the management of the Education Directorate.
  • You lead the rectorate of the elementary school with nine directly subordinate employees.
  • You are responsible for the strategic goals and performance mandate of the primary school with a global budget of over 125 million francs.
  • You represent the overall interests of the primary school and maintain an active network with offices in the city and the canton of Lucerne as well as with other cities in Switzerland. You participate in various working groups and committees.
  • Together with the management team, you will develop the elementary school in a forward-looking manner and provide impulses for innovative school and organizational development.

Your profile

  • You are a committed and convincing leader with a pedagogical high or technical college degree or equivalent education.
  • You are interested in education policy issues and the design of the public service in the education sector.
  • You know the internal administrative processes and have a good sense of political connections and decision-making processes.
  • You think ahead, develop visionary ideas and want to actively shape the elementary school.
  • You have proven experience in project and change management and move confidently in change processes.
  • You recognize the opportunities of the digital transformation and shape it in a future-oriented way.
  • You communicate clearly, transparently and addressable.
