Global Media Manager — Emmi

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Are you passionate about media and curious about what truly drives brand growth? Do you enjoy turning media expertise and data into practical recommendations and collaborating across markets? As Global Media Manager, you will help shape how we approach media across Emmi Group. Working closely with our local markets, you will strengthen our media capabilities, develop common approaches and help teams make effective investment decisions. You will report to the Global Head of Digital Marketing & Integrated Media and work in an international environment with stakeholders across our markets.

What impact you can have

  • You shape and support media strategies across our local markets, strengthening Mental Availability and contributing to long-term brand growth.
  • You advance our approach to Mass Reach Media, including channels such as TV and YouTube, and translate strategic principles into practical guidance for our markets.
  • You develop global best practices, playbooks and training to strengthen media capabilities and enable teams to make better media decisions.
  • You drive media effectiveness and campaign measurement, establishing meaningful standards for KPIs, testing and learning across the Group.
  • You further develop our Retail Media approach, with a focus on effectiveness, incrementality and business impact.
  • You partner with Marketing Directors, Brand Managers, agencies and external experts to evaluate media investments, identify opportunities and influence decisions across markets.
  • You keep Emmi connected to developments in the media landscape, translating relevant trends, marketing science and emerging capabilities into clear, actionable recommendations.

What you bring to the table

  • You bring 5+ years of experience in media strategy, digital marketing or media effectiveness, ideally gained in an international organisation or comparable environment.
  • You have strong experience with Mass Reach Media and understand how media contributes to long-term brand building.
  • You are familiar with digital media ecosystems such as YouTube, DSPs, Retail Media and Performance Marketing.
  • Experience in FMCG and knowledge of marketing science concepts such as Mental Availability, Category Entry Points, Distinctive Assets or Ehrenberg-Bass principles are strong advantages.
  • You combine an analytical mindset with the ability to turn complex information into clear and actionable recommendations.
  • You are comfortable working across markets and influencing senior stakeholders without direct authority.
  • You are fluent in English; additional languages are an advantage.

What we offer

  • Six weeks' vacation: Enjoy six weeks of vacation per year. From the age of 46 onward, your vacation entitlement increases gradually to up to seven weeks.
  • Attractive pension fund with flexible savings plans: Our Emmi Pension Foundation pays interest above the legal minimum on your retirement savings. Your contribution is based on your annual salary – without any coordination deductions. Choose between a Standard savings plan or the Plus option with 1% higher savings contributions.
  • Sabbatical & purchasing vacation leave: Treat yourself to unpaid vacation or extend your vacation entitlement by up to two additional weeks.
  • Voluntary profit sharing: Depending on the success of the business, you will receive a voluntary profit-sharing bonus, thereby recognizing your valuable contribution to the company's success.
  • Individual development plan: With tailored development plans, we specifically promote your individual competencies and support your long-term growth – a key component of our sustainability strategy. Find out more .
  • Focus on your health: We offer occupational health management, preventive measures and free social counseling for professional and private matters alike through our partner Proitera.
