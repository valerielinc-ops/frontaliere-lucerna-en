Fachperson Logistik Verbrauchsmaterialien — Brack.Alltron AG

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Tasks

  • You take consumables, check them and store them properly
  • You pick and provide consumables for our internal logistics areas on schedule
  • You monitor stocks, carry out inventories and record goods movements in the warehouse management system
  • You recognize material bottlenecks at an early stage and support you in replenishment planning
  • You ensure order, cleanliness and compliance with quality and safety requirements
  • You are actively involved in day-to-day business and support in the optimization of our processes

Requirements

  • Completed training as a logistics specialist EFZ and professional experience in warehouse logistics
  • Experience in handling warehouse management systems as well as good knowledge of German (at least B2); MS Office skills and a forklift pass are an advantage
  • You work independently, reliably and keep an overview even in hectic situations
  • You are physically fit and like to take part in everyday camp life
  • You are ready to work in shifts
  • You have an impeccable reputation and appreciate a responsible way of working

We offer

  • Perks: Attractive perks in the Brack online shop as well as lunch perks and staff restaurants in Mägenwil and Willisau.
  • Recreation: At least 5 weeks of vacation, in addition there is the possibility for holiday purchase and unpaid vacation.
  • Development: Internal training in the own Academy as well as financial participation in external further education.
  • Family: 18 weeks of paid maternity leave with extension and 4 weeks of paid paternity leave.
  • Insurance: Supercompulsory – 100% continued pay and full financing of the sickness allowance insurance by the employer.
  • Mobility: free parking, financial participation in public transport and pool vehicles for business meetings.
