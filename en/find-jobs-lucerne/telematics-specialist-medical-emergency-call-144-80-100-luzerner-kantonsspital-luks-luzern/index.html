Full description

The Lucerne Canton Hospital with its locations in Lucerne, Sursee and Wolhusen is a company of the LUKS Group and thus belongs to one of the leading hospital groups in Switzerland. The more than 8,500 employees are there with warmth, competence and commitment around the clock for the well-being of patients. The clinics and institutes of the LUKS Group offer medical services of the highest quality.

For our team we are looking for 1. March 2027 or by agreement reinforcement. Tasks: • As a specialist in telematics, you work closely with the specialist in charge of telematics and ensure the stable, secure and demand-oriented operation of central applications. • They coordinate maintenance, further development, releases, interfaces and change requests of the operational control system, all conversion systems and telecommunication means and ensure that technical solutions are optimally adapted to the operational requirements • As a central contact person, you advise and support the SNZ dispatchers, analyze disruptions and develop practical solutions for smooth operation • In addition, you actively participate in projects, take over the project management after consultation and make an important contribution to quality assurance and the further development of the department Requirements • They bring training in the security-relevant area (rescue, emergency call center, police, fire brigade), combined with ideally a proven further education in computer science. • Ideally, you will have experience in application management, telematics or a comparable technical environment, quickly capture technical connections and are familiar with ticketing systems as well as structured incident and request processes. • They have a good understanding of processes and understand how to accommodate, structure and translate operational requirements into workable solutions • They work independently, reliably and quality-consciously, keep an overview even in the event of system-relevant disruptions and then take responsibility We offer: • It expects a responsible and meaningful task in a highly relevant environment in which technology, processes and emergency care play directly together. • At LUKS, you work in a professional, dynamic and future-oriented healthcare company with a motivated team and short paths to different specialist areas. • You will be given the opportunity to actively develop the Department of Telematics, help shape projects and make a significant contribution to the quality and availability of central systems. • As part of the largest employer in Central Switzerland, you benefit from attractive employment conditions, internal training opportunities, further training opportunities and a wide range of benefits.
