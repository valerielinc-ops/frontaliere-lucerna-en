Full description

Become part of our dynamic team! Are you looking for an exciting education with diverse perspectives? Then you are exactly right with us! We are looking for motivated learner construction machine mechanics who are ready to grow and learn with us. During your apprenticeship, you will go through the various departments such as earth movement, tower cranes, concrete technology and auto cranes. So you get varied and exciting insights.

  • You will get to know the various construction machines and devices such as hydraulic excavators, wheel loaders, dumpers, bulldozers and loaders, telescopic loaders, high-rise cranes, truck mixers and auto cranes.
  • You check mechanical, hydraulic and electrical systems and learn to repair them properly if necessary
  • You diagnose errors and faults and find suitable solutions
  • You learn to carry out service and maintenance work independently and to organize the appropriate spare parts
  • You can understand and apply technical documents
  • Most of you work as a team in our workshops. At the end of the apprenticeship, you will accompany service technicians at their work. No day is like that. Qualification You bring with you:
  • Completed secondary school level A or B
  • Enjoyment of technology
  • Good grades, especially in mathematics
  • Interested in learning new things
  • Good understanding and organizational talent
  • Self-employment and responsibility
  • Teamwork and helpfulness Our offer A good start is crucial, especially when entering your professional life. A strong partner in education is therefore indispensable. Whether you are interested in an education with or without professional maturity. Liebherr-Baumaschinen AG offers you an exciting and versatile education. Have we awoken your interest? Then we look forward to receiving your online application. If you have any questions, please contact Nina Schneeberger. One passion. Many opportunities. Liebherr-Baumaschinen AG, based in Reiden (Switzerland), is responsible for the sales and service of earthmoving and material handling machines, tower cranes, concrete technology, special underground construction machines and mobile and crawler cranes in Switzerland.
