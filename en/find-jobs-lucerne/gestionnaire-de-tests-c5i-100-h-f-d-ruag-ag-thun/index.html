Full description

Responsibility

  • Responsibility for planning, conducting and documenting tests
  • Examination of requirements and preparation, maintenance and further development of test cases, test protocols and test evidence
  • Creation and maintenance of test scripting
  • Implementation of API and database testing as well as ensuring quality along modern development processes
  • Participate in the integration of tests into CI/CD and DevOps processes as well as support for deployment and release processes
  • Ensuring the link between requirements, test cases, results and defects
  • Provision of suitable test environments, maintenance of the configuration management of the test equipment and compliance with quality management requirements
  • Active participation in the optimization of test processes, test methodology and technical quality standards

Requirements

  • Higher vocational training in computer science or lateral entrants with several years of experience in software testing
  • In-depth experience as a test engineer, especially in test automation, test scripting and structured testing
  • Very good knowledge of test methodology and strategy, modern testing approaches and agile software development
  • Good understanding of modern DevOps processes
  • Knowledge of Jira, Xray and GitLab is an advantage
  • Communication strength, assertiveness and enjoyment of working in interdisciplinary teams
  • Negotiable German
  • English and/or French skills (level B2) are advantageous
  • Experience in regulated industries is beneficial
  • Military career as a senior non-commissioned officer or officer of advantage
