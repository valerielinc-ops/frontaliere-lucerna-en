fenaco is the largest agricultural cooperative in Switzerland. With more than 80 subsidiaries, we operate in Switzerland and Europe. Together, we ensure that valuable Swiss food from the earth to the table – de la terre à la table. To strengthen our payroll team in Sursee, we are looking for a Payroll HR Clerk with immediate effect or by appointment.

Matthias Reusser HR Business Partner

These tasks inspire you

  • Responsible for the correct maintenance of organizational, personnel master and contract data for an assigned personnel area (payroll and posting runs are carried out by central HR fenaco)
  • Recording and processing of all wage-relevant mutations from entry to exit including monthly plausibility check and quality assurance work (ICS)
  • Clarification and processing of payroll and accounting issues (account assignment, use of wage types, etc.)
  • Analyze and fix errors or ambiguities
  • Handling of tasks in the area of social insurance in connection with AHV, family allowances, EO, health and accident insurance
  • Clarification and processing of questions regarding time management (absences, balances) and finances (posting of wage types)
  • Creation of reports and statistics
  • Ensuring the implementation of adjustments and further developments in the area of responsibility
  • Contact person for HR Business Partners, HR Service Partners, line managers, employees as well as finance and time management managers in all payroll issues

We are looking forward to this profile

  • Basic commercial training with completed further training in the field of payroll, social insurance or HR Professional experience in a comparable function
  • In-depth knowledge of Swiss payroll features
  • Good knowledge of SAP HCM is an advantage
  • Experience in dealing with figures and questions relating to social security
  • Independent, precise and structured way of working

Discover your advantages

  • Training and development: We invest in the development of our employees. A wide range of internal training courses is open to everyone, and we support external training.
  • Family time: Mothers receive 100 percent of their insured salary during the 18 weeks of maternity leave. The other parent enjoys 3 weeks of family time at full pay.
  • Attractive vacation policy: Our vacation policy guarantees all employees at least 5 weeks of vacation. From 50. Year of age, it is 6 weeks and from the 60. Age even 7 weeks. In addition, the purchase of additional vacation days is possible.
  • Benefits and discounts: Our employees benefit from discounts on shopping and refuelling, credits on REKA money and discounted mobile subscriptions for them and their families, among other things.
  • Insurance benefits: We protect our employees with above-average social and pension benefits and offer special conditions on supplementary insurance with various health insurance companies for their entire family.
