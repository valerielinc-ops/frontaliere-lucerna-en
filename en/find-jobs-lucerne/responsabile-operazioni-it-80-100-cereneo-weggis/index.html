Tasks

  • Leadership and coordination of the clinic support team as well as ensuring daily IT operations
  • Support for workplace IT, user onboarding and lifecycle management
  • Cooperation with external IT service providers and infrastructure partners as well as continuous improvement of processes and service quality Projects & further development
  • Management and coordination of operational IT projects as well as implementation and optimization of clinical and administrative systems
  • Coordination of implementations with departments and internal stakeholders

  • Operational coordination of data protection and compliance activities

  • Structured, independent and responsible functioning

  • Very good knowledge of German and English
  • Studies in computer science, business informatics, medical informatics or a related field
  • Experience in healthcare or in a regulated environment
  • Familiarity with Microsoft 365, endpoint management, identity management or the operation of IT infrastructures
  • Experience with GDPR or operational compliance processes
  • Interest in healthcare technology, digital transformation and AI
