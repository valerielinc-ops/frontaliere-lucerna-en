Full description

Your opportunity in the neurorehabilitation of the future We develop a highly individualized treatment program for our patients that enables them to regain maximum independence and quality of life. We accompany them through all phases of rehabilitation – inpatient, outpatient and increasingly also in the field of telerehabilitation. As an assistant doctor or assistant doctor at cereneo expected You not only have an exciting clinical environment, but also the opportunity to become part of an internationally networked research and innovation landscape: We cooperate closely with the University of Zurich and ETH Zurich and bring the latest research results directly to the patient bed.

Their tasks

  • Independent care of inpatients with a broad spectrum of neurological diseases under the supervision of experienced cadre physicians
  • Use in a 3-shift model on the planned IMC and in rotating use on the standard station
  • Participation in the emergency service (with attractive secondary income)
  • Close cooperation with an interdisciplinary team of neuropsychologists, movement therapists, speech therapists, nutritionists, engineers and natural scientists
  • Opportunity to actively participate in clinical research projects and further develop your own scientific interests

What should you bring?

  • CH or EU state examination completed
  • MEBEKO recognition mandatory
  • At least 1 year of clinical professional experience
  • Strong interest in neurorehabilitation and enjoyment of interdisciplinary collaboration
  • Very good knowledge of German and English, other languages welcome
  • Strong communication, responsible, flexible and team-oriented

Our offer

  • Training with structure: detailed and transparent training concept within the framework of specialist training
  • Mentoring & Career Development: Mentoring system and targeted promotion of your clinical and scientific career
  • Science & Innovation: Opportunity to pursue your own research interests in cooperation with ETH Zurich and the Lake Lucerne Institute
  • Flexibility & Extras: Exemption and financial support for congresses and further education, ancillary income through pikett services
  • Modern infrastructure: Perfectly located working environment with a view of Lake Vierwaldstättersee, Pilatus and Rigi
  • Social benefits: Good social benefits (NBU, employer share pension fund 60%)
  • Parking & public transport: Free parking and access to e-charging stations, subsidy for public transport
  • Food: Lunch-Check with 50% employer participation, free coffee, tea, water & fruit
  • Discounts: Various discounts for leisure activities
  • Fitness: Shared use of fitness equipment

Our corporate culture

  • EXCELLENCE: Using the latest research findings and first-class service
  • CHARACTERISTICS: We are always there with all our heart. Of course, this also includes the sincere treatment of our patients: inside, but also between our staff
  • MOTIVATION: Joint goal achievement and support of patients: women to improve their quality of life
  • TEAM WORK: Reliability and mutual support in the team
  • INNOVATION: Research and testing of new therapeutic approaches and devices
