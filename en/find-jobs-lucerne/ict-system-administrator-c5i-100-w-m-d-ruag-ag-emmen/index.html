Full description

Every day, around 3,000 employees of RUAG and RUAG Real Estate make a significant contribution to the security of Switzerland. They ensure that the Swiss Army and other operational and security organizations can perform their tasks comprehensively at any time.

You can move that

  • Responsible for the operation, monitoring and further development of pre-production environments for safety-related systems and applications
  • Ensuring a stable and efficient system landscape during the integration and quality phase before production release
  • Manage and operate Linux-based systems and containerized platforms based on Kubernetes
  • Configure, monitor and optimize network components and communications infrastructures in the Cisco environment
  • Creating and maintaining technical documentation and supporting the continuous improvement of operational processes
  • Collaboration in incident, problem and change management (1st, 2nd and 3rd level support)

You bring that with you

  • Training or higher vocational training in computer science or several years of practical experience as a system administrator
  • Experience in the operation and administration of Linux systems
  • Experience in dealing with Kubernetes and containerized environments
  • Good knowledge of the network environment, preferably with Cisco technologies
  • Understanding integration, testing and operational processes in complex ICT environments
  • Experience with automation and scripting languages is an advantage
  • Self-employed person with strong communication and organisational skills
  • High customer and service orientation as well as a sense of user needs
  • Very good knowledge of German and English (level B1-B2)
  • Military career as a senior non-commissioned officer or officer of advantage

Do you not meet all the requirements one hundred percent? Apply anyway. We are always looking for talented and motivated people who want to enrich our team. We attach great importance to diversity and equal opportunities and our goal is to build a diverse team in which everyone can fully develop their personal strengths. We particularly encourage all individuals, regardless of gender, who may have concerns about accepting a job in an army-related environment to apply.
