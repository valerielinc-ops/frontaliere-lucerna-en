Full description

We focus on proximity and openness: At the city administration we live the du-culture – already in the application process. Do you want to empower children, help shape their development and make a difference every day? Then you are exactly right with us! The primary school Littau Dorf is run as a day school and offers you a lively environment in which you accompany and support children of the 1st to 6th primary class. Look forward to a varied everyday life, space for your socio-educational ideas and a dedicated team that welcomes you.

Your tasks

  • You actively participate in the care team and design a professional, child-friendly care and promotion.
  • You accompany and promote the children at meals and in leisure time.
  • You are a reliable caregiver for children and parents and, if necessary, have parent meetings (without evening appointments).
  • You will maintain contact with teachers, home maintenance and other specialists at the school.
  • You work mostly during the school weeks, Mo, Di, Do, Fr 11:00 - 17:30 Wednesday from 13:30 - 17:30

Your profile

  • You bring a completed socio-educational, socio-cultural or pedagogical education or a degree as a specialist supervisor.
  • You have experience and joy in accompanying children from 6 to 13 years old, supporting them and supporting them in their everyday life.
  • You empathize with the children’s world and deal with culturally and age-mixed groups.
  • You convince through your empathetic, appreciative and team-oriented way and actively contribute to our school life.
  • You bring experience in the administrative and planning area of responsibility.
  • You work independently, reliably and communicate openly, clearly and transparently.
  • The solution-oriented approach (LOA) is not a foreign word for you.

More on the spot
