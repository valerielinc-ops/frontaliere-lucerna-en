Full description

The Lucerne Canton Hospital with its locations in Lucerne, Sursee and Wolhusen is a company of the LUKS Group and thus belongs to one of the leading hospital groups in Switzerland. The more than 8,500 employees are there with warmth, competence and commitment around the clock for the well-being of patients. The clinics and institutes of the LUKS Group offer medical services of the highest quality.

For our team we are looking for reinforcement as of 1 February 2027 or by agreement. Tasks: • You take on a wide range of administrative, organisational and coordinative tasks and thus make an important contribution to the smooth operation of the Clinic for Anesthesia • As part of deputies, you ensure the reliable implementation of central administrative processes of anesthesia care and the medical service. Among other things, you support the deployment and surgical planning, personnel processes and the organization of courses and events. • They coordinate tasks around materials and infrastructure, support the administrative and organizational processes of the clinic and work closely with employees, managers and internal partners. • They take care of a wide range of service and organizational tasks to support everyday hospital life and thus make an important contribution to a pleasant working atmosphere as well as a well-groomed and welcoming environment. Requirements • they have commercial training, a comparable basic administrative training or have several years of relevant professional experience in a comparable capacity; • They move safely in digital work environments and bring joy and openness to quickly familiarize themselves with new applications and systems. • They work in a structured, independent and reliable manner and also keep track of different tasks and priorities in a dynamic environment. • They enjoy contact with people, communicate in an addressable and service-oriented manner and also appear friendly, solution-oriented and professional in demanding situations. We offer: • They assume a versatile hub function within the clinic for anesthesia and work closely with employees, leaders and various professional groups • A versatile and independent area of responsibility awaits you with insight into the personnel, planning and organizational processes of a modern acute care hospital • You work with specialists from nursing, medical, management and administration on a daily basis and thus gain a wide range of insights into the daily routine of a university acute care hospital. • You benefit from a large network and numerous benefits at the largest employer in Central Switzerland
