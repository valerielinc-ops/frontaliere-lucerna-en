Full description

Ready for a new challenge in the heart of Central Switzerland – without any night and weekend service? Then you are exactly right with us!
Medical Imaging Luzern AG is an X-ray institute of the Lucerne Canton Hospital with its locations in Lucerne and Sursee. Together with the radiology of the LUKS, we offer our customers a wide range of imaging diagnostics – from CT (Siemens), MRI (Philips 3T and 1.5T), mammography, to conventional X-ray and ultrasound.

For our team we are looking for reinforcement immediately or by agreement. Tasks: • They prepare our patients friendly and expertly for their examinations and accompany them throughout the entire examination process. • You work independently and responsibly on the various modalities and ensure a competent execution of the investigations • You bring your in-depth knowledge of MR and CT specifically into everyday work and continuously develop your expertise. • In interprofessional exchange, you contribute to reliable cooperation and patient-oriented medical imaging Requirements • You have a degree as dipl. Radiology specialist HF or equivalent training and in-depth knowledge of MR and CT • Even in the event of changing requirements, you work responsibly, flexibly and with a clear view of the patient’s needs. • You are openly involved in the team, actively support cooperation and share your expertise with colleagues • In your day-to-day work, you act independently, remain technically curious and use opportunities to further develop your skills in a targeted manner. We offer: • You work at an attractive and centrally located workplace within Medical Imaging Lucerne AG, a company of the LUKS Group • For your professional and personal development, you receive active support, in particular through paid further education • Free parking, a public transport voucher as well as free coffee, mineral water, fruit and chocolate make your everyday work easier • With an attractive pension fund, credited changing time, 16 paid holidays and the opportunity to buy an additional week of vacation, you benefit from concrete additional services
