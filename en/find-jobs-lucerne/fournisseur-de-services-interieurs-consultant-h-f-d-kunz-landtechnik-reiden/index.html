Kunz Landtechnik, part of Serco Retail AG, is a regional sales and service center for the global brand CLAAS as well as other premium brands of agricultural and special-purpose machinery. At the Langnau near Reiden location, we are looking for a salesperson / advisor to start immediately or by agreement.

Discover your benefits

  • Education and further training: We invest in the development of our employees. A broad range of internal educational opportunities is open to everyone, and we support external further training.
  • Attractive holiday policy: Our holiday policy guarantees all employees at least 5 weeks of holiday. From the 50. Year of age, it is 6 weeks and from the 60. Year of age, even 7 weeks. In addition, it is possible to purchase additional vacation days.
  • Family time: Mothers receive 100 percent of their insured salary during the 18 weeks of maternity leave. The other parent enjoys 3 weeks of family time at full pay.
  • Insurance benefits: We provide our employees with above-average social security and pension benefits and offer special conditions on supplementary insurance with various health insurers for their entire family.
  • Discounts and rebates: Our employees benefit, among other things, from discounts on shopping and fuel, credits on REKA-Geld, and discounted mobile subscriptions for themselves and their families.
