Full description

The Person-Centered Healthcare and Health Communication (PCH) Group is an interdisciplinary research group of the SPF. It examines how people with paraplegia and their relatives experience their everyday life and their health care – especially with qualitative methods. The aim is to use it to develop knowledge, tools and strategies that improve communication and decision-making in rehabilitation and in everyday care.

Tasks: • Coordination and implementation of data collection (especially qualitative, e.g. interviews, focus groups) • Management and preparation of qualitative data • Support for data analysis and interpretation • Cooperation in the preparation of ethics and research applications • Assistance in writing publications and presentations • Collaboration in an interdisciplinary team as well as with external partners and stakeholders The job is limited for 2 years. Requirements • You have completed a master’s degree in health sciences or a related field. • Experience in contact with patients (e.g. in a clinical or therapeutic context) as well as experience with qualitative research methods are advantageous. • Your mother tongue is (Swiss) German and you have excellent English skills in words and writing. • As a sociable, open and communicative personality, you succeed in dealing sensitively with study participants. • Knowledge of statistical programs (e.g. STATA, R) is welcome, but not a requirement. • With your structured way of working, you keep an overview and react flexibly to different requirements. • You are happy to take responsibility for your tasks and at the same time appreciate the professional exchange and cooperation in the team. We offer: You not only expect attractive employment conditions, but also numerous other advantages. Look forward to meaningful work in a modern working environment, flexible working hours and a wide range of further education opportunities. Come and experience our unique spirit. More information on parplegie.ch/benefits. Swiss Paraplegic Research (SPF) is part of the Swiss Paraplegic Group. The mission of SPF is to sustainably improve the situation of paraplegic people through interdisciplinary, clinical and population-based research at national and international levels. The areas of SPF's holistic research include functionality, mobility, social integration, equal opportunities, self-determination, quality of life, health care, and epidemiology and statistical methods. The SPF is a federally recognized and funded university research institution and an active link to academic and industrial research and development at national and international level.
