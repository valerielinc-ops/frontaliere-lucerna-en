Full description

Do you want to combine office work with a meaningful activity? And put customers first? Tasks:

  • You document civil status events from birth, marriage to death incl. judicial and administrative decisions.
  • Declarations (e.g. recognition, name declaration, gender change) are accepted and processed.
  • As part of marriage preparation, you check whether all marriage requirements are met.
  • You are conducting weddings.
  • You create excerpts from the civil status registers.
  • You advise our customers by phone or personally at the counter. Requirements
  • You have completed basic commercial training, preferably in a public administration, as well as the Swiss Federal Professional Card as a civil status officer.
  • You have practical experience in the task area and have a flair for legal matters.
  • You express yourself in writing and orally in the German language and bring your foreign language skills.
  • Your way of working is independent, reliable, service-oriented and sensitive.
