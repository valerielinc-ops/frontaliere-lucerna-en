Full description

Our customers retire well prepared, invest their money successfully, are optimally secured and save taxes, premiums and fees. This makes us the number one independent financial advisory service. For young and committed personalities, we offer an insight into customer advice as part of the WML internship.

  • You are attending the Wirtschaftsmittelschule and would like to obtain your vocational degree with this internship.
  • Financial topics such as shares and bonds as well as customer contact are fun for you.
  • You have a quick apprehension, a flair for numbers and are a motivated and established personality.
  • You learn every day from our experienced consultants.
  • After completion of the internship, there is the possibility of permanent employment (also part-time).
  • Surveys regularly show that employees classify the VZ as an above-average positive environment in which they can fully develop.
