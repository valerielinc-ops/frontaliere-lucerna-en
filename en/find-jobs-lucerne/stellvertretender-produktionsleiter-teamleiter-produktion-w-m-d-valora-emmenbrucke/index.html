Full description

Do you want to take responsibility, actively shape processes and, together with your team, ensure the highest quality on a daily basis? Then you can expect a versatile management task with creative leeway, personal responsibility and an exciting production environment. Tasks you will like

  • Planning and control of production including personnel, material, machine and warehouse planning for semi- and finished products.
  • Ensuring timely and demand-oriented production according to the orders of sales and trade.
  • Promoting optimization measures and continuous improvement processes as well as taking over the deputy production management in the absence of production management
  • Responsibility for compliance with quality standards, hygiene guidelines, occupational safety requirements and legal provisions.
  • Promoting a safe working environment through the consistent implementation of safety measures and accident prevention.
  • Leadership, motivation and further development of the production team as well as strengthening a successful team culture.
  • organisation of personnel deployments, shift and break planning and control of absences.
  • Monitoring production processes and plants, detecting and reporting disruptions as well as active support in day-to-day operations.
  • Responsibility for order, organization and economic inventory management of production warehouses as well as the preparation of key figures and inventory support. Requirements that you bring
  • Completed training in the food sector, as a baker, confectioner or in a comparable production environment
  • Several years of professional experience in production, ideally in the food industry
  • Initial management experience and pleasure in accompanying and developing employees
  • Experience in production, personnel and resource planning
  • Good knowledge of hygiene, quality and safety regulations
  • Technical understanding and interest in production facilities and processes
  • Structured, organized and solution-oriented working method
  • Secure handling of MS Office and ideally experience with ERP or production planning systems
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing as well as a responsible hands-on mentality
  • Versatile and exciting working environment
  • Attractive discounts in the Valora formats
  • Direct exchange and an open du-culture
  • Daily fresh pretzels and good Italian caffè
  • Fair employment conditions
  • Joint company events
