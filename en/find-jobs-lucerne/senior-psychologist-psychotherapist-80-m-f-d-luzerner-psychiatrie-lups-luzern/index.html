Full description

Lucerne Psychiatry AG is a teaching hospital of the University of Lucerne and is represented with its offers at various locations in the Canton of Lucerne and in Sarnen, Canton of Obwalden. More than 1,600 employees from various professional groups and departments are committed to the human and competent welfare of patients. The offers are aimed at children, adolescents and adults up to old age. Senior psychologist/psychotherapist 80% (m/f/d)

Their tasks

  • Professional and human leadership of the psychological team of the Consiliary and Liaison Psychiatric Service
  • Responsibility for the further development of psychological and psychosomatic care at the Children's Hospital Central Switzerland
  • Conduct of consiliar psychiatric investigations, crisis interventions as well as psychological diagnosis and treatment
  • Psychotherapeutic support of children, adolescents and their families for psychosomatic, chronic and acute diseases (including eating disorders, functional disorders, chronic pain, mental crises)
  • Supervision, professional support and promotion of psychologists in further education and employees in training
  • Close interprofessional cooperation with paediatric clinics, care, therapy services and child and adolescent psychiatry
  • Collaboration in the development of clinical standards, treatment guidelines and quality projects
  • Engagement in teaching, further education as well as scientific projects and innovations are desired

Your profile

  • Completed psychology studies with federally recognized further education in psychotherapy
  • Several years of clinical experience in child and adolescent psychiatry, pediatric psychology or consultant and liaison psychiatry
  • Sound knowledge in the diagnosis and treatment of psychosomatic diseases in childhood and adolescence
  • Leadership experience or proven interest in a leadership function
  • High social and communication skills and enjoyment of interprofessional cooperation
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working method
  • Resilient, reflective and inclusive personality with a high willingness to take responsibility
  • Interest in the further development of innovative care models as well as in teaching and research

Benefits

  • A responsible leadership function with a great deal of room for manoeuvre
  • Cooperation in the further expansion of an innovative and highly specialized consultancy and liaison psychiatric service
  • Participation in scientific projects as well as in national and international cooperation
  • Attractive employment conditions according to cantonal guidelines and family-friendly working conditions
  • Benefitful social benefits (acceptance of the KTG premium and 2/3 of the PK premium)
