Full description

The Lucerne Canton Hospital with its Lucerne, Sursee and Wolhusen sites is a company of the LUKS Group and is one of Switzerland's leading hospital groups. The more than 8500 employees are there with cordiality, competence and commitment around the clock for the well-being of patients. The clinics and institutes of the LUKS Group offer medical services of the highest quality. For our team we are looking for reinforcement on October 1, 2026 or by agreement. Tasks:

  • Their varied tasks include: reception and dismissal of patients, simple medical examinations, organising diagnostic and therapeutic measures, medical regulations in delegation, carrying out visits, documentation and coordination work
  • As part of the medical team, you take care of stationary patients and work in an interdisciplinary manner (with nursing, diagnostic and therapeutic services)
  • Based on treatment paths and under medical supervision, you take care of medical care in stable patient situations and support in complex treatment procedures Requirements:
  • They have completed training as a qualified nursing professional HF/FH with a few years of professional experience in the acupsomatic field, preferably in visceral surgery
  • You will preferably have a further training CAS Clinical specialist/clinic specialist, a nursing bachelor/master degree or NDS further education (e.g. anaesthesia, emergency or intensive care)
  • They like to work in the team, but are also used to independent and structured work
  • A quick understanding and a high sense of responsibility complete your profile We offer:
  • You have the opportunity to continuously develop your skills and participate in the design of the job profile in the clinic
  • An exciting and challenging task area awaits you in a collegial team culture. Through interdisciplinary work, you can continuously expand your network
  • You benefit from a lot of existing know-how from the largest employer in central Switzerland
  • We offer attractive employment conditions, numerous interesting employee offers and regulated working hours (day service from Monday to Friday). You can find more information on this at www.luks.ch
