Full description

The Children's Hospital Central Switzerland of the Lucerne Cantonal Hospital (LUKS) is one of the largest children's hospitals in Switzerland and serves the entire region of Central Switzerland at the highest level. Our employees work around the clock to ensure that children, young people and their relatives are measurably better off. LUKS, with its locations in Lucerne, Sursee and Wolhusen, is a company of the LUKS Group and thus belongs to one of the leading hospital groups in Switzerland. >For our team we are looking for reinforcement as of 1 February 2027 or by agreement.

Tasks

  • They independently carry out developmental and neuropsychological assessments in children and adolescents and derive individual support and treatment measures from this.
  • Her versatile tasks include neuropsychological examinations in children and adolescents with neurological diseases and disorders such as epilepsy, brain trauma, brain tumors or cerebral palsy.
  • In addition, you take on developmental diagnostic examinations in formerly prematurely born children as well as in at-risk preschool-age children
  • Close interdisciplinary and interinstitutional cooperation enables you to make a valuable contribution to the holistic care of children and young people.

Requirements

  • You have a completed psychology degree (M.Sc.) with a focus on developmental psychology, cognitive psychology or neuropsychology as well as sound knowledge in diagnostics. Ideally, bring a specialist title in Neuropsychology
  • Your experience in the developmental and neuropsychological diagnosis of children and adolescents as well as your knowledge of current diagnostic procedures distinguish you
  • An independent, structured and responsible way of working is a matter of course for you. Administrative tasks such as scheduling, reporting and performance recording are also performed reliably
  • Enrich with your communicative and empathetic personality as well as your enjoyment of interdisciplinary cooperation You join our team and accompany children and young people with a lot of commitment

We offer

  • As the largest employer in Central Switzerland, LUKS offers numerous interesting internal and external training opportunities.
  • We offer you the opportunity to participate in an exciting field of activity with a high level of personal responsibility
  • They benefit from very good social benefits and attractive employment conditions. You will find this and more information at the following link: www.luks.ch/employment conditions
