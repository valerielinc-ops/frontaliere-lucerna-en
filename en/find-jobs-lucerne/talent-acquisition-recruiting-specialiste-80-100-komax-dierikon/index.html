Talent Acquisition & Recruiting Specialist (80-100%) — Komax

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

We want to win people who fit Komax and our culture and are ready to shape our future. For this, we are looking for a personality who combines operational recruitment strength with strategic talent acquisition thinking: close to the line, secure in the process, active in the market and with pleasure in driving forward developments.

Your responsibilities

  • Responsibility for the end-to-end recruitment of specialists and key roles, from hiring meetings to successful recruitment
  • Conducting hiring meetings with Line Management and HRBP to clarify the profile, story and target group-specific search strategy
  • Designing a professional candidate experience – binding, fast, transparent and suitable for the Komax culture
  • Further development of recruiting standards, in particular interview structure, evaluation logic and assessments, as well as ensuring value & culture fit and recruiting training for line management
  • Consistent use of SAP SuccessFactors Recruiting, active participation in its further development and the introduction of new technologies in recruiting in close cooperation with Group HR and the Global Recruiting Community
  • Analysis of recruiting KPIs such as time-to-hire, quality and process bottlenecks and derivation of concrete improvements
  • Participation in employer branding, the career site and social media recruiting as well as contributing the recruiting perspective to the further development of our employer positioning

Your profile

  • Experience in end-to-end recruiting or talent acquisition, ideally in an industrial-technical environment
  • Good understanding of the Swiss labour market as well as a sense of employer positioning and modern recruiting channels
  • Strong consulting expertise and secure stakeholder management in dealing with Line Management, HRBP and external partners
  • Safe handling of recruitment systems
  • Experience with SAP SuccessFactors is a plus
  • Enjoy data, KPIs and continuous improvement of processes, quality and candidate experience
  • Winning, proactive and implementation-strong personality with strong discussion and consulting skills

Your benefits

Your new place of work in Dierikon is equipped with the latest technology and work equipment. Your workplace is located in one of the modern open-plan offices, where there are also inspiring meeting areas and a large terrace for various employee events. Komax AG offers its employees flexible working time models and home office. The further training of employees is important to Komax AG, which means, for example, that they benefit from free internal English courses or access to selected attractive learning platforms. At the Dierikon location, there are modern break rooms as well as the possibility of a discounted lunch in a neighbouring partner restaurant.
