Global Finance Transformation Manager (m/f/d) 100%

Ready to shape the future of Finance on a global scale?

Join us in driving the transformation of our global Finance landscape. As Global Finance Transformation Manager, you will play a key role in our SAP S/4HANA journey and lead strategic initiatives across Finance, data, reporting, and technology involving Power BI and CCH Tagetik.

As part of Global Finance Reporting Solutions, you will collaborate with stakeholders worldwide to deliver scalable solutions, drive reporting excellence and finance processes, and contribute to the future of Finance in an international environment.

Your Impact • Shape and drive Schindler’s global finance SAP S/4 HANA transformation agenda, with a focus on Financial Steering, Universal Journal, Margin Analysis, Material Ledger, and Product Costing. • Lead cross-functional transformation programs in a complex international environment, bringing together Finance, IT, and business teams to achieve sustainable change. • Act as a trusted partner to senior business and Finance stakeholders, aligning global priorities and translating strategic objectives into actional transformation initiatives • Partner with Global Finance stakeholders, Process Owners, IT teams, and external implementation partners to ensure successful project delivery. • Analyze Finance operating models, processes, reporting, and data structures, identifying opportunities for simplification, standardization, and continuous improvement

Who you are

You combine strong analytical and conceptual thinking with excellent stakeholder management skills. You can connect finance, data, reporting, and technology to develop practical solutions and communicate complex topics clearly.

Your Profile • University degree in Finance, Accounting, Controlling, Business Administration, Business Information Systems, or a related field • Minimum 8 years of relevant professional experience in Finance, Finance Transformation, SAP projects, or business process management, ideally gained in an industrial multinational company. • Hands-on experience with SAP ERP and/or SAP S/4HANA transformation projects and a strong understanding of end-to-end Finance and reporting processes. • Knowledge of SAP Financial Steering, management reporting, Universal Journal, Margin Analysis, Material Ledger, Product Costing, or similar Finance domains. • Strong ability to engage, influence, and communicate with senior stakeholders, including Finance leadership, business executives, and C-level management. • Ability to bridge business and technology teams, translating strategic objectives into practical solutions. • Fluent English and the ability to communicate effectively with senior stakeholders
