Full description

The activity includes controlling production facilities and ensuring trouble-free operation. The company offers a structured shift work with attractive social benefits. You benefit from comprehensive training opportunities and a modern working environment. For our client we are looking for a Activities

  • Ensure smooth operation of the allocated production facilities
  • Management of the respective machine group in compliance with the specified objectives
  • Responsible for compliance with production management and quality requirements
  • Execution of maintenance, service, repair work including preventive maintenance work
  • Assistance in projects in the engine room as well as in the development of solutions for improving machine performance

Requirements

  • Professional experience in production engineering and/or maintenance

  • Good German skills in word and writing, oral English skills are advantageous
  • Readiness to work in shift operation is required (early shift rotating from 05.30 a.m. to 2 p.m., late shift from 14.00 a.m. to 22.30 a.m. as well as night shift from 22.30 a.m. to 5.30 p.m.)
