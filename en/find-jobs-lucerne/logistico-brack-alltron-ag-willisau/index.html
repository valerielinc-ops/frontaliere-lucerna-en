Tasks

  • Ensuring timely and qualitatively correct order processing
  • Control of articles, quantities and quality and professional and transport-safe packaging of consignments

  • Possibility to introduce process optimizations, actively participate and gradually take on more responsibility
  • A varied working day in two shifts (07:00 – 16:15 and 12:45 – 22:00)
  • A modern working environment with clear processes, short decision paths and development opportunities Requirements
  • Completed training as a logistician:in EFZ
  • Flowing knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Experience in handling industrial trucks as well as good computer skills (MS Office and warehouse management systems)
  • Readiness for use in two-shift operation and good repute
  • A reliable, careful and responsible way of working
  • Physical fitness and robustness for the varied working day
  • Team spirit and joy in achieving goals together We offer:
  • Perks: Attractive perks in the Brack online shop as well as lunch perks and staff restaurants in Mägenwil and Willisau.

  • Development: Internal training in the own Academy as well as financial participation in external further education.
  • Family: 18 weeks of paid maternity leave with extension and 4 weeks of paid paternity leave.
  • Insurance: Supercompulsory – 100% continued pay and full financing of the sickness allowance insurance by the employer.
  • Mobility: free parking, financial participation in public transport and pool vehicles for business meetings.
