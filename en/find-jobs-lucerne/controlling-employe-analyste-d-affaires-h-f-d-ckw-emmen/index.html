Full description

Pensum: 80-100%, limited to 9-12 months

You can design it.

  • You identify optimization potential and develop processes with a focus on digitalization, automation and efficiency.
  • You analyze data, generate evaluations and support the development of practical automation solutions.
  • You support the team in the day-to-day business of controlling and get insights into budgeting, forecasts and reporting.
  • You work in regulatory accounting and support with analyses and reports for internal and external stakeholders.
  • You take on partial tasks in ongoing projects and actively bring your ideas to the team.
  • You work closely with different interfaces and help make data-based decisions possible.

This is what makes you

  • You have a business education with a focus on finance, controlling, business informatics or data analytics.
  • Ideally, you have already gained experience in controlling, business analysis or in a comparable environment.
  • You have a high IT and AI affinity and enjoy questioning, simplifying and automating processes.
  • You work safely with Excel and have experience with data analytics and digital tools.
  • You communicate with the addressee, work solution-oriented and actively participate in the team.
  • You are stylish in German and can communicate well in English.

The job is initially limited to 9-12 months. In the event of mutual interest and corresponding need, there is the possibility of an extension or a longer-term perspective within the company.
