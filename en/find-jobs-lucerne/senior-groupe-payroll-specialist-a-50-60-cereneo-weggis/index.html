Full description

As one of the world’s leading clinics, we develop an individualized treatment program with the aim of enabling our patients to enjoy the highest possible degree of independence and freedom of life, thereby optimizing their quality of life. We are aimed at both patients from the Swiss cash system and international patients. cereneo offers highly specialized rehabilitation programs for people with neurological diseases and injuries, including stroke, brain injury, neurodegenerative diseases such as Parkinson’s, multiple sclerosis and many other neurological disorders.

Your new role

  • Payroll responsibility for the entire NRG Group:

cereneo Schweiz AG + NRG: 2 wage runs per month EAV: 1x hour wage + 1x monthly wage approximately 170 MA (CH) cereneo Prevention AG: 1 wage run L-GAV approx. 35 MA (CH) cereneo Global Services AG: 1 wage run approximately 19 MA (International)

  • Administrative responsibility for master data collection and updating
  • Self-employed responsibility of social insurance (KTG, UVG, AHV) withholding taxes as well as pension and insurance issues in close cooperation with external bodies
  • Processing of daily sickness and accident allowances, maternity insurance and EO bills
  • Implementation of end-of-year processes, wage reporting and legal declarations
  • Active participation in the optimization and digitalization of payroll processes and systems
  • Competent contact person for employees and managers in all payroll issues
  • Place of work Hertenstein, HO on agreement, time flexibility if there are no wage runs

This is what you bring with you

  • Basic commercial training with higher education in the payroll sector (e.g. HR, payroll specialist or similar)
  • Several years of professional experience as a payroll specialist in a Swiss company (advantage in the healthcare sector)
  • Very good Swiss labour law knowledge (of advantage in health care)
  • Verified with EAV and L-GAV
  • Precise, structured and independent working methods, combined with high service orientation and discretion
  • Very good German and good English skills in word and writing

We offer you

We offer you a modern and professional working environment, attractive employment conditions and a wide range of opportunities to develop personally and professionally.

  • Attractive place of work: Modern infrastructure in a unique location with a view of Vierwaldstättersee, Pilatus and Rigi.
  • Further education and development: Individual opportunities for professional and personal education and development.
  • Attractive social benefits: takeover of the NBU premium and an employer’s share of 60% of pension fund contributions.
  • Mobility: Free parking and e-charging stations and a contribution to public transport. In addition, employees benefit from the AVIA fuel card of 5 centimeters discount per litre of fuel and a free roadside service throughout Europe.
  • Food: Lunch check with 50% employer participation and free coffee, tea, water and fruit.
  • Family friendliness: discounted conditions at our connected daycare center.
  • Health and Fitness: Free sharing of existing fitness equipment.
  • Leisure and culture: Various employee benefits as well as attractive leisure offers, including free Pilate tickets, entrance to the Tierpark Goldau and the Swiss Museum Pass with access to over 500 museums in Switzerland.

Our culture – that connects us Excellence, warmth, motivation, teamwork and innovation are more than just values for us. We combine the latest scientific findings with first-class service, meet our patients and each other with warmth and develop new ways in neurological rehabilitation together. If you are an open and committed person, would like to work interdisciplinary and continuously develop, we look forward to getting to know you. Become part of our team and shape the future of neurological rehabilitation with us.
