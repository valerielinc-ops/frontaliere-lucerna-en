Full description

Become part of something bigger. Work with more than 2000 specialists from more than 100 professions and enable paraplegic people to live a self-determined life. On the modern campus in Nottwil, an inspiring atmosphere and interdisciplinary cooperation at eye level awaits you.

In the role of graphic designer with professional responsibility, you actively shape the visual appearance of the Swiss Paraplegic Group (SPG). You design, design and realize high-quality communication tools with a focus on print and ensure a consistent visual appearance across different communication channels. As a professional contact person, you work operationally in day-to-day business, contribute your graphical expertise and assume the professional responsibility within the graphics team. Tasks: • Art Direction and design of our patron magazine "Paraplegia" and the employee magazine • Implementation of graphic projects from the idea to the finished product • Responsibility in Team Graphics • Design, realisation and production of print and communication media • Ensuring a uniform visual appearance according to corporate design • Further development and maintenance of graphic standards and templates • Quality assurance of all visual means of communication • Advising internal clients on graphic issues • Planning, coordination and support of print production • Collaboration with internal departments as well as external partners and printers • Close cooperation with the photo, video, audio and marketing & communication teams within the newsroom • Cooperation in the further development of efficient graphics and production processes • Participation in the daily newsroom session with regular updates from Graphics & Design Requirements • Completed basic training as a graphic designer EFZ or comparable training • Further education in graphic or communication design (HF/FH) is advantageous • Several years of professional experience in the design and production of communication media • High affinity for print media and their production • Very good knowledge of Adobe Creative Cloud, especially InDesign, Photoshop and Illustrator • Strong communication, creative and initiative personality • Structured, independent and forward-looking working method • Enjoy independent design and operational implementation of projects • Strong quality awareness and high design standards • High consulting competence and service orientation • Very good knowledge of German as well as language skills in French and English are advantageous We offer: You not only expect attractive employment conditions, but also numerous other advantages. Look forward to meaningful work in a modern working environment, flexible working hours and a wide range of further education opportunities. Come and experience our unique spirit. More information on parplegie.ch/benefits. Founded in 1975 by Guido A. Zäch, the Swiss Paraplegic Foundation is today one of the largest works of solidarity in Switzerland. With its 2 million members of the Gönner-Vereinigung, it is broadly anchored in the population. The membership fees together with donations form the financial basis for a worldwide unique service network for people with paraplegia. The Swiss Paraplegic Foundation is the umbrella organisation of the Swiss Paraplegic Group and its group companies.
