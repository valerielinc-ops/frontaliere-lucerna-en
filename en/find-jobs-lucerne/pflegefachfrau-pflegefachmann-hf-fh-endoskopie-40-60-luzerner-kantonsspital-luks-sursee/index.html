Full description

The Lucerne Cantonal Hospital with locations in Lucerne, Sursee and Wolhusen is a company of the LUKS Group and is therefore one of the leading hospital groups in Switzerland. The more than 8,500 employees are there around the clock for the well-being of patients with warmth, competence and commitment. The clinics and institutes of the LUKS Group offer medical services of the highest quality. We are looking for reinforcements for our team on September 1, 2026 or by arrangement. Tasks:

  • You accompany exciting procedures in gastroenterology, provide sedation and look after our patients with empathy and professional competence before, during and after the examinations
  • You ensure a smooth daily routine and ensure the highest standards of hygiene with your eye for detail
  • As part of an interdisciplinary team, you support the team and the medical profession with diagnostic and interventional endoscopies (including Gastroscopy, colonoscopy, ERCP, PEG systems, etc.) and contribute to an efficient process
  • Thanks to your technical understanding, you work safely and enthusiastically with our devices and actively contribute your ideas Requirements:
  • You have completed training as a qualified HF/FH nurse and ideally have experience in the field of gastroenterology and sedation
  • You are characterized by your empathy in dealing with patients as well as your openness and joy in learning
  • Your technical skills Understanding and your practical experience in dealing with devices enable you to use our medical technology safely and efficiently. We offer:
  • You can look forward to a small, well-coordinated team with appreciative cooperation, a modern workplace and good accessibility by public transport
  • You can expect regular working hours on day shift - without any night or weekend shifts - as well as an interesting, varied and independent job
  • As the largest employer in Central Switzerland, you benefit from numerous benefits - including internal training opportunities and attractive social benefits that go beyond the standard
