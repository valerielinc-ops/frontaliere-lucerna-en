Full description

For <b>Raiffeisenbank Lucerne</b>, a successful and locally oriented bank with offices in Lucerne, Ebikon, Root and Littau, we are looking for a personality as a customer consultant.

Tasks

  • Comprehensive, needs-oriented advice to our customers on all credit issues in the mortgage sector as well as on the subject of investments.
  • Advising our customers on pensions, insurance, retirement and taxes
  • Recognizing and exploiting the customer potential through active and continuous support of the personal customer portfolio
  • Development of needs-based solutions taking into account the individual needs, wishes, goals and living conditions of the customers as well as within the segment-specific product range
  • Expansion of the existing customer base through targeted acquisition of new customers
  • Cooperation in market development measures and performance of representative tasks

Requirements

  • Completed banking training or similar training
  • Experience in credit advice (mortgage) and investment advice
  • Banking and finance training
  • Enjoy customer contact and communication with our customers on all channels
  • Strong customer- and service-oriented thinking and acting
  • Self-employed and responsible working practices
  • You are proactive, open, flexible and humorous
