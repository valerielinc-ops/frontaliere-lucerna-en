Full description

Every day, around 3,000 employees of RUAG and RUAG Real Estate make a significant contribution to the security of Switzerland. They ensure that the Swiss Army and other operational and security organisations can perform their tasks comprehensively at any time.

You can move that

  • Leadership, organization and further development of the regional Safety & Security Organization East with several locations and assigned Safety & Security Officers
  • Ensuring uniform implementation of Group-wide specifications, standards and processes in the areas of safety, security, environment, crisis management and business continuity management
  • Responsibility for operational management and continuous optimisation of the security organisation and regional processes
  • Perform security inspections, risk analyses, audits and vulnerability analyses as well as follow-up of the defined measures
  • Responsibility for emergency, crisis and incident management including planning and conducting exercises and coordinating events
  • Ensuring compliance with legal, regulatory and internal requirements in the areas of safety, security and environment
  • Advising and supporting managers and locations in all matters of security, risk, environmental and crisis management
  • Cooperation with authorities, blue light organisations, customers and other internal and external stakeholders
  • Integration of safety, environmental and business continuity requirements into projects, operational processes and change projects as well as support for sustainability initiatives
  • Planning and awareness-raising activities and exercises to promote a sustainable safety and risk culture

You bring that with you

  • Higher education (HF/FH/uni) or equivalent qualification in the fields of security, risk management, environment, facility management or business administration
  • Several years of management and professional experience (minimum 5 years) in corporate security, safety, crisis management or a comparable safety organization
  • In-depth experience in managing teams and managing decentralized organizational units
  • Very good knowledge of physical safety, occupational safety, risk management, emergency and crisis management
  • Practical experience in audits, risk and vulnerability analyses as well as incident and emergency management
  • Sovereign treatment of authorities, blue light organisations and internal and external stakeholders
  • Strong leadership, communication and organizational competence as well as a structured, solution-oriented working method
  • High resilience, decision-making strength, integrity and sense of responsibility even in crisis situations
  • Confident German, good knowledge of English (min. B2) and good knowledge of Italian for successful cooperation in the East region

Over the area

The "Safety & Security" division ensures Group-wide security and environmental management in order to effectively protect employees, infrastructures, business processes and entrusted information and materials of the federal government that are worth protecting. The division is responsible for the protection and physical integrity of employees as well as the resilience of business processes to security-related risks. Through uniform standards and processes, it ensures that legal requirements and Group-internal safety and sustainability goals are met.
