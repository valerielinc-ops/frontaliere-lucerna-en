Full description

JOB SUMMARY Marriott International, Inc. has entered a joint venture with the Leali family, founders of Lefay, to bring the highly regarded luxury wellness hospitality brand into Marriott's global portfolio. The joint venture, which is equally owned by Marriott and the Leali Family, owns the brand and intellectual property assets, and is focused on growing the Lefay around the world. This Manager Entity Reporting & Tax - JV is a key member of the Lefay Joint Venture. This position reports directly to the Managing Director of the Lefay Joint Venture with dotted-line reporting to Marriott International’s Director Policy & Governance – EMEA on the Marriott EMEA Finance & Tax team. This position supports the timely preparation and filing of statutory accounts for the Italian and Swiss Joint Venture legal entities, coordinates with Marriott and external auditors, and supports all aspects of local income tax compliance for the Joint Venture. Responsibilities include assisting with financial statement preparation, coordination, preparation and filing of tax returns for entities incorporated and/or tax resident in the Continent including estimated payments, informative returns and withholding tax filing, VAT returns and payments. The Manager Entity Reporting & Tax works with independence, but within established guidelines, producing and analysing financial information as well as legal contracts to support the accounting and tax for subsidiaries. In addition, he/she will serve as a contact for entity questions/issues for stakeholders across the organisation as it pertains to countries across EMEA. SCOPE/BUSINESS CONTEXT/EXPECTED CONTRIBUTIONS

  • Preparation and delivery of information required for the filing of the annual statutory accounts for varying entities within EMEA, including the financial statements and the excel files containing the current years activity
  • Work with the company auditors and tax advisors to deliver the information required to perform the audits and submission of tax returns in a timely manner
  • Prepare financial analysis to the JV accounting team at HQ
  • Work with Research and Planning team for any tax audits within the JV’s
  • Recording journal entries as necessary
  • Reconcile Local accounting systems to Marriott’s Peoplesoft system, and ensure Local and Marriott ledgers are in balance
  • Use relevant computer programs to verify details of recorded transactions; conduct detailed examinations of cash receipts and disbursement vouchers, payroll records, requisitions, delivery notes, receiving reports and other accounting and operating documents to ascertain whether transactions are properly supported and are recorded correctly
  • Review, verify, and update withholding tax rates in the owner billing database. Research and resolve owner billing disputes in relation to withholding tax.
  • Recommend ways to bring programs and operations into compliance with goals and objectives
  • Partner with Tax, Treasury Accounting Services, Corporate & Statutory Accounting, Treasury, Legal and all other interested parties
  • Assist with the quarterly Balance Sheet Reconciliation certification process (known as MIP66) for EMEA entities
  • Work with MI HQ to fulfil the continents income tax accounting responsibilities including providing support for the reconciliation of the current tax liability, preparation of complex deferred tax analyses, preparation or review of local tax provisions and the timely and review of OneSource
  • Partner with hotel Directors of Finance as appropriate
  • Maintain accurate and detailed working papers in the agreed standard format
  • Perform any other tasks as assigned by the Lefay Joint Venture Managing Director or the Director Policy & Governance - EMEA
