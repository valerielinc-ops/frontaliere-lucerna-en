Full description

In the field of neuro-urology, computer-aided urodynamics, digitized X-ray, and medical technology are used. In addition, minimally invasive endoscopic procedures are performed.

Tasks

  • Independent planning, preparation and implementation of prescribed neuro-urological diagnostics, therapies and measures
  • Carrying out the computer-controlled urodynamic examinations and ultrasonic residual urine measurements
  • X-ray representation of the lower and upper urinary tract with contrast medium
  • Independent switching of suprapubic bladder fistula catheters
  • Assistance in medical procedures

Requirements

  • You have a degree as a dipl. Nurse, Med. Praxisassistent*in, or health professional
  • You are a professionally competent, team-capable and resilient personality
  • You appreciate the interdisciplinary cooperation
  • You are interested in specializing in neuro-urology

The Swiss Paraplegic Centre in Nottwil is a private, nationally and internationally recognized specialist clinic for cross-sectional, back and respiratory medicine. As an acute and rehabilitation clinic, the centre offers its patients comprehensive care and supports them throughout their lives. The clinic is part of the Swiss Paraplegic Group, which provides a globally unique service network for the holistic rehabilitation of people with paraplegia.
