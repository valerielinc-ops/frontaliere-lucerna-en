Full description

Become part of something bigger. Work with more than 2000 specialists from more than 100 professions and enable paraplegic people to live a self-determined life. On the modern campus in Nottwil, an inspiring atmosphere and interdisciplinary cooperation at eye level awaits you.

The neurological department forms an important part of the Swiss Paraplegic Centre. Approximately 1500 patients are treated inpatiently or outpatiently every year. Neurology is growing. Therefore, we would like to expand our team immediately or by agreement with a responsible and team-capable medical-technical assistance (MTA). Tasks: • Assistance and implementation of routine examinations (EEG, ENG, EMG and evoked potentials) in the ambulatory and intraoperative area • Assistance in lumbar punctures and patient monitoring • Ensure preparation, functionality and suitability of medical devices and consumables • Assistance in the introduction of new department-internal structures and processes Requirements • Completed basic training as a medical-technical assistant (MTA) or degree in neuro- / health sciences • Open and communicative nature as well as independent, focused and customer-oriented working methods • Computer user knowledge and good technical understanding • Interested in working in an interdisciplinary team • Fluent knowledge of German, English, Italian and French are advantageous We offer: You not only expect attractive employment conditions, but also numerous other advantages. Look forward to meaningful work in a modern working environment, flexible working hours and a wide range of further education opportunities. Come and experience our unique spirit. More information on parplegie.ch/benefits. The Swiss Paraplegic Centre in Nottwil is a private, nationally and internationally recognized specialist clinic for cross-sectional, back and respiratory medicine. As an acute and rehabilitation clinic, the centre offers its patients comprehensive care and supports them throughout their lives. The clinic is part of the Swiss Paraplegic Group, which provides a globally unique service network for the holistic rehabilitation of people with paraplegia.
