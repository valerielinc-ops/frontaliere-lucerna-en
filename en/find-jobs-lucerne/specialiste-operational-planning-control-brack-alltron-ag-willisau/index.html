Specialist Operational Planning & Control - Logistikleitstand — Brack.Alltron AG

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Tasks

  • Monitoring and controlling ongoing logistics processes with the aim of ensuring day-to-day capability
  • Coordination of orders along defined cut-off times and control of special orders such as JIT or floor deliveries
  • Preparation and implementation of daily meetings and brush-ups
  • Ensuring a smooth flow of information within the team and relevant departments and interfaces
  • Responsibility for the coordination of information flows between the Mägenwil and Willisau sites
  • Participation in shift transfers and structured sharing of all relevant information
  • Recognizing, reporting and resolving minor disruptions in the responsible area
  • Participation in the review and further development of processes and standards
  • Ensuring compliance with regulations, occupational safety, health and order and cleanliness
  • Support for group management in special tasks and operational challenges

Requirements

  • Several years of practical experience in logistics and a thorough understanding of different logistical processes and functions
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Good user knowledge in MS Office
  • Strong organisational capacity as well as a good understanding of processes and connections
  • Independent and reliable work with the ability to take responsibility and coordinate tasks in a meaningful way
  • Analytical thinking and the ability to make informed decisions even under time pressure
  • High willingness to learn and openness to changes and new processes
  • Resilience and the ability to maintain an overview even in a dynamic day-to-day business
  • Communication strength as well as a professional handling of different interfaces
  • Professional qualification EFZ, ideally in the field of logistics; further training as a logistics specialist:woman or process specialist:woman is an advantage

We offer

  • Perks: Attractive perks in the Brack online shop as well as lunch perks and staff restaurants in Mägenwil and Willisau.
  • Recreation: At least 5 weeks of vacation, in addition there is the possibility for holiday purchase and unpaid vacation.
  • Development: Internal training in the own Academy as well as financial participation in external further education.
  • Family: 18 weeks of paid maternity leave with extension and 4 weeks of paid paternity leave.
  • Insurance: Super-compulsory – 100% continued pay and full financing of the sickness allowance insurance by the employer.
  • Mobility: free parking, financial participation in public transport and pool vehicles for business appointments.
