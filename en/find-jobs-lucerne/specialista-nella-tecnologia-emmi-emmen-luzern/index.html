Full description

  • With your technical know-how, you ensure that our production and infrastructure facilities run reliably and that downtimes remain as short as possible.

  • Taking on professional responsibility and keeping an eye on several tasks at the same time is one of your strengths.
  • Thanks to your quick comprehension, you will recognize technical connections, set the right priorities and find practical solutions.
  • Transferring knowledge in an understandable way and working together with different specialist areas gives you pleasure.
  • Ideally, you are already familiar with SAP and safely use digital tools.
