Full description

You want to make a difference and use your know-how to create a working environment in which safety and health are a matter of course? Become part of our team! You take on a central role in the further development of our safety culture. With your commitment, you ensure that occupational safety and health protection not only remain guidelines, but are actively lived.

Luogo di lavoro: Cooperative Migros Lucerne, registered office Dierikon, Industriestrasse 2, 6036 Dierikon

Important information

Applications will be collected by the end of September and processed thereafter. We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You take on the professional responsibility for occupational safety and health protection and ensure that guidelines (EKAS and Migros group solution) are effectively implemented in everyday life.
  • You advise the company management and executives competently, understandably and solution-oriented.
  • You will continue to develop our emergency organisation in the role of project management and ensure that we are well prepared in an emergency.
  • You organize security audits, carry them out, carry out risk assessments and conduct targeted measures.
  • You identify risks, analyze security indicators and derive concrete measures for greater security from them.
  • You make safety understandable and strengthen our employees with practical training.
  • You plan measures, accompany their implementation and make sure they have an impact.

Requirements

  • 3 years of professional experience: in occupational safety and health protection (ideally in a large company)
  • Eidg. Professional ID: Specialist for Occupational Safety and Health Protection (ASGS)
  • Experience in project management and in dealing with planning instruments
  • Expertise in the field of fire protection, for example with an additional training fire protection expert (advantageous)
  • Conceptual and analytical thinking, a structured, independent working method and assertiveness
  • Ability to interpret key figures and statistical evaluations and derive effective measures from them
  • Communicative and resilient personality, which brings a safe appearance and can easily adapt to different conversation partners
  • German (very good knowledge)
  • French (advantageous)

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision on progress or withdrawal from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Personal video or telephone conversation: For a large number of applicants, we optionally conduct a personal telephone or video conversation. This gives us the opportunity to get to know more candidates within a short time in order to invite suitable people for the next round. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
  • Expert Discussion: In a specialist discussion, specific knowledge relevant for the execution of the job is requested by the manager and/or another specialist and, if necessary, the team. If possible, there is time afterwards to get to know the workplace and team better in a free conversation. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Getting to know the team: Whether someone fits into the team is an important factor for employees and employers when deciding for or against employment. In a casual framework, we therefore give the opportunity for everyone to get to know each other.
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition (Digital): After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent for digital signature by e-mail. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
