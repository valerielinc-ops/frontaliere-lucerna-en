Full description

Luzerner Psychiatrie AG is a teaching hospital of the University of Lucerne and provides its services at various locations in the Canton of Lucerne and in Sarnen, Canton of Obwalden. More than 1,600 employees from a wide range of professional groups and specialist fields are committed, with humanity and expertise, to the well-being of patients. Its services are aimed at children, adolescents and adults up to an advanced age.

Physician – Outpatient Transition Psychiatry 80-100% (m/f/d)

Your Responsibilities

  • Conduct psychiatric assessments of adolescents and young adults
  • Provide integrated psychiatric and psychotherapeutic treatment to adolescents and their families during the transition to adulthood (transition psychiatry)
  • Participate in case discussions
  • Actively maintain internal interfaces with the Child and Adolescent Psychiatric Service (KJPD), adult psychiatry and the Crisis Intervention Centre (KIZ)

Your Profile

  • Completed medical degree (MEBEKO recognition mandatory)
  • At least 2 years' experience in outpatient and/or inpatient child and adolescent psychiatry and/or adult psychiatry
  • Communicative, flexible and open personality with an independent working style and the ability to work across departments and in an interconnected way.
  • Enjoyment of and curiosity about the social psychiatry spectrum and working with people of adolescent age

Benefits

  • Participation in external group supervision programmes and continuing education
  • At least 5 weeks of vacation, loyalty day from the 3rd year of service
  • Possibility of unpaid leave/sabbatical
  • Favourable social benefits (KTG premium covered and two-thirds of the PK premium)
