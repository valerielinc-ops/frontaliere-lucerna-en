Full description

We make habitats worth living.

For MSR automation

pi-System GmbH is a successful partner in the field of integral building automation based in Oberkirch. Our guiding principle is energy efficiency: economical use of energy and sustainable solutions.

Your tasks

  • You create the electrical plans for MSR automation and clarify technical interfaces independently.
  • You program, install, test and commission automation systems (mainly Beckhoff PLC).
  • You implement operating, alarm and navigation concepts.
  • You support project management in technical clarifications with internal and external partners and are responsible for project initiation, commissioning and testing with customers.
  • You contribute to the creation of technical documentation.
  • In addition, you regularly check the work results to ensure that they meet the specified quality criteria.
  • After your training, you occasionally take on piketting services (about 3 weeks per year) – of course with fair compensation.

You bring that with you

  • You have completed vocational training in the field of automation, electrical installation or electrical engineering/electronics.
  • Further education FH/HF is a plus, but not a must.
  • Preferably, you already have experience with automation programming according to IEC.
  • Your strengths include a structured and independent way of working, and you are adaptable and flexible.
  • Analytical thinking skills as well as proactive thinking and acting distinguish you.
  • Good knowledge of German and secure technical English round off your profile.
  • Category B driver's license you bring.
