HR administration (a) 40% fee We are an initiative, modern and future-oriented transport and logistics company with headquarters in Schachen/LU. Thanks to our motivated staff and a high quality of service, we are on course for success! As of November 1st or by agreement, we are looking for reinforcement (40% penalty) in our HR administration. Task area

  • Personnel administration (entries, exits, mutations, correspondence, etc.) - Maintenance of master data in the HR system as well as management of digital personnel files
  • Processing of accident and illness reports and correspondence with insurance companies and authorities
  • Assistance in wage preparation
  • Management of professional dresses
  • General administrative work

Requirement profile

  • Commercial basic training and further training HR-Assistance / HR-related processing - Several years of profound HR experience in a similar function
  • Good IT knowledge (MS-Office and of advantage Abacus)
  • Mother tongue German, other languages beneficial
  • Enjoyment in proximity to operational business and a service-oriented environment
  • Structured, independent working method and high service orientation
  • Solution-oriented thinking

Offer

  • 40% (full day working days between Wednesday and Friday)
  • Responsible and varied area of responsibility
  • Family corporate structure with flat hierarchies
  • Financial security by intra-corporate pension funds

We offer you a challenging, versatile activity. In addition to a modern infrastructure, ergonomic workstations and free parking spaces are available. Of course, you can look forward to a motivated and competent team that will actively support you in all areas. Did we get your interest? We look forward to your application.
