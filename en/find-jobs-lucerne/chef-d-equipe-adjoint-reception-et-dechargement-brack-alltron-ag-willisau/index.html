Deputy Teamlead Receiving & Unloading — Brack.Alltron AG

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Tasks

  • You relieve the team lead in day-to-day operations and take over his deputy and professional responsibility for around 15 employees
  • You work with around 80% operationally in the incoming goods and ensure efficient unloading, booking in the system (Navision) and storage using a truck
  • Together with your team, you coordinate the incoming goods of around 60 trucks and up to 900 pallets per day and ensure that the ramps run smoothly.
  • You take over the ramp management as well as the predictive deployment and resource planning in day-to-day operations
  • You plan and coordinate container deliveries taking into account storage capacities
  • You work closely with purchasing, logistics planning and quality assurance and ensure a good flow of information
  • You develop your team through coaching on the job, promote collaboration and create a dynamic environment with continuous improvements Requirements
  • You have experience in logistics, ideally with training as a logistician EFZ
  • You have a valid truck ID and work safely with industrial trucks
  • You communicate safely in German (at least B1, ideally B2) in word and writing
  • You like to work operationally and actively tackle daily business
  • You take on responsibility and want to develop professionally towards team leadership
  • You are familiar with IT systems such as MS Office (Outlook) and warehouse management systems
  • You work reliably, structured and have no criminal records
  • You work during the day from 07:00 to 17:00 in a well-established team of around 16 employees in a dynamic logistics environment We offer:
  • Perks: Attractive perks in the Brack online shop as well as lunch perks and staff restaurants in Mägenwil and Willisau.
  • Recreation: At least 5 weeks of vacation, in addition there is the possibility for holiday purchase and unpaid vacation.
  • Development: Internal training in the own Academy as well as financial participation in external further education.
  • Family: 18 weeks of paid maternity leave with extension and 4 weeks of paid paternity leave.
  • Insurance: Supercompulsory – 100% continued pay and full financing of the sickness allowance insurance by the employer.
  • Mobility: free parking, financial participation in public transport and pool vehicles for business meetings.
