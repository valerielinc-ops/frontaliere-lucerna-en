This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Responsibility

  • Monitoring and controlling all relevant safety and alarm systems as well as carrying out inspections, audits and vulnerability analyses
  • Documentation and investigation of safety and environmental incidents incl. Cause analysis and derivation of measures
  • Advising managers on risk assessments, risk analyses and implementation of legal and internal requirements
  • Coordinates the Group-wide collection, consolidation and quality assurance of KPIs, KRIs and management information in the area of safety, environment and generates user-friendly evaluations and reporting
  • Advice on hazard assessments, risk analyses and prevention measures
  • Support for the integration of safety and environment requirements in operational and project processes in accordance with the industry standards ISO 14000 45000;
  • Ensures the coordination and verification of test and control obligations (equipment inspection, site approvals, etc.) and supports the responsible bodies in the documentation, monitoring and timely implementation of measures from tests, audits, assessments and regulatory requirements
  • Creation, maintenance and further development of site-specific work instructions, process instructions, concepts and instructions in the area of safety and environment
  • Support for environmental management (waste, emissions, water protection) and participation in sustainability initiatives
  • Planning, organisation and implementation of training, workshops and evacuation Emergency training
  • Active contribution to the further development of safety, health and environmental culture at the site

Requirements

  • Completed training in the field of safety (e.g. safety specialist (EKAS/SUVA), technical training HF/FH with safety focus
  • Additional fire protection training, ISO 45001/14001 courses, BZS courses
  • Several years of professional experience in the field of occupational safety, health or environment
  • In-depth knowledge of risk management, security audits and internal control systems (ICS) in the security context
  • High communication and team skills at all hierarchical levels
  • Structured and binding working methods as well as a high level of organisational and coordination capacity
  • Very good MS-Office user knowledge (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook);
  • Very good German and English skills
