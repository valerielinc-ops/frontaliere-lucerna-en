Full description

Every day, around 3,000 employees of RUAG and RUAG Real Estate make as significant contribution to the security of Switzerland. They ensure that the Swiss Armed Forces and other emergency and security organizations can comprehensibly carry out their tasks at all times.

You can move it

  • Capture requirements, assess feasibility, and define initial design concepts
  • Lead design, development, integration, testing, and certification of complex avionics projects
  • Ensure compliance with relevant regulations for system integration certification
  • Establish and review system requirement documents, certification reports and safety analyses (FHA/SSA)
  • Manage external suppliers and coordinate technically with customers, procurement, sales, and authorities
  • Support new technology and innovation initiatives
  • Provide technical guidance to production and customer support teams

What you should bring with you

  • University degree in Electronics or Aerospace Engineering
  • 5 years+ practical experience in systems engineering for integrated avionics within aerospace sector or comparable safety critical industry
  • Experience with military aviation or tactical systems is a plus
  • Familiarity with EFIS, FMS, FCS (advantage) and EW/ECM/Weapon Systems (plus)
  • Working knowledge of FAA/EASA/MAA certification requirements
  • Self starter personality with capability to meet tight schedules and collaborate in multicultural teams
  • Business level English (C1); German language skills are a strong advantage

Do you not fully meet all the requirements? Apply anyway. We are always looking for talented and motivated people to join our team. We place great emphasis on diversity and equal opportunities and aim to build a versatile team where everyone can fully utilize their personal strengths. In particular, we encourage all people, regardless of gender, who may have concerns about accepting a position in a military-related environment to apply.

About the field of activity

In this position, you will work in an international working environment, with open-minded, ambitious, and enthusiastic coworkers. You will work on civil and military aircraft (helicopters, fix-wing and UAV) and state of the art mission systems, with the great opportunity to help refine our system development process, getting involved in all phases from conceptual design to lab and flight testing. In this role you will work in our Engineering Center in Emmen.
