Full description

Together with a small, experienced team, you ensure that our mixtures are produced precisely and according to clear specifications. You work with different raw materials, modern systems and clearly defined processes. If you like to work exactly, take responsibility and physically tackle, you are a perfect fit for us. Even lateral entrants: women with motivation and willingness to learn are welcome.

You can do that.

  • According to given recipes, you produce powder mixtures, precisely weigh aromas and components and prepare the required raw materials for production.
  • In addition, you take on assignments on the heater 9 as well as in the mobile tank cleaning and also ensure a safe, clean and reliable process there.
  • All work steps are carefully recorded and documented in our MES/SAP systems.
  • Bottling bags are correctly labeled and marked by you so that further processing works smoothly.
  • You keep a consistent eye on hygiene, quality and safety in your daily work and comply with the corresponding requirements.
  • Together with your team, you ensure a reliable production process in 2- and 3-shift operation from Sunday evening to Friday evening.

Why do you succeed?

  • Physical work does not matter to you and you are ready to regularly lift bags up to 25 kg.
  • Very good knowledge of German helps you to understand work instructions safely and to exchange yourself clearly in the team.
  • You can rely on your careful, accurate and reliable way of working.
  • Working in 2- and 3-shift operation from Sunday evening to Friday evening suits you and you bring the necessary flexibility.
  • When dealing with digital systems, you feel comfortable. Experience with MES or SAP is a plus.
  • Experience in the production or food industry is an advantage. Also as a motivated:r lateral entrant:in with a willingness to learn you are welcome with us.

This is what we offer you

  • 5 weeks of vacation: Enjoy five weeks of vacation per year. From the age of 46, your holiday entitlement gradually increases to up to six weeks.
  • Attractive pension fund with flexible savings plans: Our Emmi pension foundation pays interest on your retirement savings above the legal minimum. Your contribution is based on your annual salary – without coordination deduction. Choose between standard or plus savings plan with 1% higher savings contributions.
  • Voluntary Success Sharing: Depending on the business success, you will receive a voluntary success Sharing. In doing so, we want to acknowledge your valuable contribution to the company’s success.
  • Attractive surcharges & additional services: You receive 20% time allowance from 8 pm, paid warm-up times, a time credit for changing times and a fair compensation for piquetting outside your working hours.
  • Free parking: We offer our employees free parking at the location.
  • Cheap meals in the staff restaurant: For breakfast, lunch and break meals, a staff restaurant with discounted employee prices is available.

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Lucerne's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Lucerne's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
