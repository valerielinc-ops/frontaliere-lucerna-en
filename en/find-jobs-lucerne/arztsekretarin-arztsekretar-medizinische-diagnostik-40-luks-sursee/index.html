The Lucerne Canton Hospital with its locations in Lucerne, Sursee and Wolhusen is a company of the LUKS Group and thus belongs to one of the leading hospital groups in Switzerland. The more than 8,500 employees are there with warmth, competence and commitment around the clock for the well-being of patients. The clinics and institutes of the LUKS Group offer medical services of the highest quality.

For our team we are looking for reinforcement as of 1 October 2026 or by agreement. Tasks: • Together with your teammates, you will coordinate and plan the consultation hours in the fields of gastroenterology, cardiology and infectiology • They receive our patients in a friendly and professional manner, advise them personally and by telephone and take on general administrative tasks in the team • Depending on the workload, you can also support the medical secretariat, open outpatient cases in SAP, create and send reports and process assignments. Requirements • You have completed medical or commercial training with further training as medical secretary • They work organized, keep an overview even in a lively environment and coordinate appointments reliably • They meet patients as well as internal and external contact persons in a friendly, empathetic and service-oriented manner. • Good knowledge of medical terminology and in-depth IT knowledge (MS Office) round off your profile We offer: • You benefit from regular working hours from Monday to Friday, attractive social benefits and a wide range of training opportunities as well as many attractive employee offers. • Our modern clinic information system supports you in managing all patient data and processes digitally and efficiently • A modern leadership culture based on trust, personal responsibility and openness awaits you.
