Full description

Foodie wanted! As a product manager 80-100%, you manage our range of fruits / vegetables from purchase to implementation in the stores. Look forward to a dynamic environment with a lot of creative scope. In this central hub function, you make an important contribution to the success of Migros Lucerne and actively shape developments.

Mansioni

  • Responsibility for assortment management, operational goods business and regional procurement (purchasing and supplier management) of fruits and vegetables
  • Further development of the regional range and implementation of the national category strategy
  • Planning, implementation and controlling of activities, actions and sales support measures
  • Ensuring the flow of information to internal and external contacts
  • Active controlling, market monitoring and recognition of relevant market trends

Requirements

  • Sound experience in product management or purchasing: in the food industry
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): further training in business administration, marketing or purchasing
  • Solution-oriented, flexible and resilient personality with a strong team spirit
  • "maker-type" with independent, well-organized working method
  • Entrepreneurial thinking and action
  • Affinity and enthusiasm for food
  • Strong communication and confident in dealing with various stakeholders
  • German (very good knowledge)
  • French (basic knowledge)
  • Italian (advantageous)
