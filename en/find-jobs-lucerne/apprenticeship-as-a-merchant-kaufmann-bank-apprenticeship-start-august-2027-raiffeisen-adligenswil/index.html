Full description

Are you a well-established and enthusiastic personality who has good manners, an interest in banking and enjoyment of customer contact? Then you may soon be our new team member.

Tasks

During your three-year training, you will gain insight into different departments and will be used in the following work situations:

  • Receiving and forwarding bank customers
  • Banking clients advise
  • Supporting bank customers via digital channels
  • Assistance in advising customers
  • Administrative pre- and post-processing of banking transactions
  • Cooperation in projects and organisation of events such as trial days
  • Insight into marketing

In the various departments, you will be accompanied and supported by competent and experienced practical trainers. You can complete the training with us either as an EFZ (Federal Certificate of Ability) or with a professional degree. Industry education takes place at the CYP in Root and the Vocational School in Lucerne.

This is what we offer you

  • Fun at work in a family environment
  • We not only see each other in the office, but also do something in our free time
  • We participate in your laptop for your education
  • We cover all costs for your schoolbooks
  • We support your language stay financially

Requirements

School

  • Completed secondary school level A with good grades or level B with very good grades
  • Very good behavioral grades (social skills)
  • Very good knowledge of German, oral & writing

Personal

  • Teamwork
  • Contactful and communicative
  • Reliability and exact work
  • Open to new
  • Flexible and responsible
  • Reflective capacity

Interests

  • Delight in everyday banking and customer contact
  • Use of information sources and new technologies

The following documents are required

  • Letter of motivation
  • CVC
  • All upper school certificates
  • Interlocking results
  • References (class instructor)
  • Test gauge assessments (if any)
