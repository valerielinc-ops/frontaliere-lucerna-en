Full description

We focus on proximity and openness: At the city administration we live the du-culture – already in the application process.

Your tasks

  • Information, counselling and therapy of children and adolescents with linguistic abnormalities
  • Therapy and fall management within the framework of integrated special training
  • Advising parents, teachers and specialists in the field of language
  • Development and implementation of prevention projects and early version as well as participation in working groups for the further development of the Speech Pedagogy Service
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation with other areas of school support as well as with various specialist bodies
  • Publicity

Your profile

  • You have a completed and EDK recognized training in speech therapy
  • You appreciate working in a team
  • You like to approach people from different cultural and social circles

We offer

  • diverse and self-responsible activity with many design possibilities
  • appreciative working atmosphere in a committed, technically broad-based team
  • Support and training by mentor
  • a speech therapist as a directly superior department manager
  • Attractive training opportunities
  • Contemporary employment conditions according to cantonal and urban specifications
