Role overview

Therapy Center Meggen is looking for a dedicated personality for the position: Psychological psychotherapist, Psychological psychotherapist (60-100%).

The Meggen Therapy Center (TZM) is a socio-psychiatric therapy station in Meggen on Lake Vierwaldstättersee (Canton of Lucerne).

The institution offers inpatient and outpatient treatments, integrative psychotherapy and milieu therapy for adults.
