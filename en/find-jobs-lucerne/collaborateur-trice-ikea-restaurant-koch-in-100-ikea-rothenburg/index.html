Who you are

In the area of IKEA Food, you always strive to provide customers with a unique shopping experience while offering irresistible food offers in a welcoming atmosphere that gives them a good reason to come back. In addition, you are ready to take the next career step as a leader.

  • You have completed an apprenticeship as a chef or system caterer/in EFZ.
  • You have experience in system catering or are ready to cook for our customer and employee restaurant.
  • You are motivated to work in a dynamic retail environment.
  • You are passionate about ensuring that the food served is always of high quality.
  • You are proud of how you serve our customers and you want to give them a tasty reason to visit us again and again!
  • You have leadership skills and are motivated to grow with IKEA and lead a team in the future.
  • You communicate safely in German, English skills are an advantage.
  • You are ready to work both early (6:30-15:30) and late shifts (12:30-21:30) 5 days a week including Saturdays.

You are responsible for

  • You have a real passion for our range and actively educate yourself about the content, origin and preparation of our food. You pass on your expertise to guests, colleagues.
  • You present the full breadth and depth of the IKEA Food range and inspire the guests.
  • You make an active contribution to our “Safety First” culture.
  • You ensure that efficiency is guaranteed in the company and that your colleagues are familiar with routines and specifications and that they are adhered to in their daily work.
