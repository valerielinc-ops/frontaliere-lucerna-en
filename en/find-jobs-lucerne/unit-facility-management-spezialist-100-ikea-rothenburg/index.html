Full description

Who you are

As a facility employee, you are part of the facility management team, which offers employees, visitors and customers a sustainable, safe, clean and functional environment. Facility services include both technical maintenance and cleaning, waste management, pest control, snow clearance, maintenance of construction and retail equipment, and Energy, Emissions and Water Management.

  • You already have professional experience in a similar position or the technical know-how for most aspects of a retail building and related equipment (HLK, primarily electricity, sanitation, building automation, building structure, etc.).

•You have a well-founded education in the electrical sector (EFZ level or similar) with at least 3 years of professional experience, it is advantageous if you meet the approval requirements according to NIV Art. 13 or Article 15. Further training as a maintenance specialist:frau or Technisches Kaufmann:frau or as a technician:in is an advantage

  • You are able to deal with stressful situations including detect critical events such as fire detection, flooding, etc.
  • You can work independently, act thoughtfully and solution-oriented and solve problems and complex tasks within deadlines and financial constraints.
  • You support the service-oriented approach of the Facility Management team through your social skills and your polite, open and respectful way of dealing with others.
  • You act responsibly, trustingly and calmly with the information available to you.

•You have good written and oral communication skills in the German language, English is an advantage

  • You are familiar with MS Office applications (Word, PowerPoint, Excel) and CAFM systems.

•You are not afraid of occasional work on a lift •You don’t mind working in the Pikettdienst (24/7) every 4-5 weeks, changing working hours and also performing your tasks on Saturday

  • You are in possession of a driver’s license and can be on site in an intervention time of 60 minutes

You are responsible for

FM operation — troubleshooting equipment and infrastructure, ensuring technical safety, compliance and maintenance incl. Overall responsibility for electrical systems (evidence to Esti, contact for electrical topics, possibly minor electrical work)

Service provider management — coordination and quality control of external FM partners, including reporting.

Quality management — conduct internal tests, periodic checks and audits for the infrastructure and in particular for electrical installation.

FM tools & processes — use of CAFM, compliance with standards, creation of relevant reports, processing of work orders generated by CAFM and follow-up control.

Budget & Costs — Participation in planning, cost control and efficient working methods, as well as in invoice processing.

Projects & conversions — Support for medium-sized technical projects and construction projects, independent implementation of smaller projects, maintenance of as‐built documentation.

General: Support of the Facility Manager in the operative business, as well as partial representation in absence after the training phase
