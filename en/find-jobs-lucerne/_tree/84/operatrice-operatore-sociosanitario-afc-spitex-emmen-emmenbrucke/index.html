Full description

With around 65 employees, Spitex Emmen is a modern, versatile and committed employer. We provide high-quality ambulatory health care for the approximately 33,000 inhabitants of the municipality of Emmen and actively promote the nursing profession of tomorrow as a training company. For the expansion of our established team, we are looking for a committed and team-capable personality as Professional Health EFZ We are waiting for you.

  • a lively, varied working environment – no day is like the other
  • Working independently for well-being and maintaining the quality of life of our clients
  • Active participation in the care process
  • progressive employment conditions and generous training and further education offers

Your tasks with us are

  • Ensuring holistic support, support and care of our clients at home
  • Documentation of care-relevant information and forwarding to the case-leading specialist
  • Cooperation with internal contact persons
  • Exchange of information with external bodies such as hospitals, family doctors, pharmacies, etc.

What do you bring with you

  • Completed training as a specialist in health EFZ
  • Joy in a lively environment, sense of responsibility, independence as well as a good portion of humor and warmth
