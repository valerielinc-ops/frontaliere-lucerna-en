Full description

Project manager carpentry 100% We bring together what belongs together! The Anker Group started in April 2021 with two people – now we have already grown to 5 locations. Do you also want to be part of a future-oriented company? From now on or by agreement we are looking for a permanent position a project manager carpentry learned

Your challenge

  • You take precise measurements directly on building objects.
  • You record orders, trigger material orders and ensure a smooth process.
  • You coordinate appointments and maintain close contact with architects, builders and fitters.
  • You monitor ongoing assemblies, accompany them professionally and hold all the strings in your hand.
  • After completion, you take care of the reprocessing and billing of the orders.
  • You are responsible for customer concerns and actively contribute to success with your quality awareness.
  • In addition, you advise architects and builders competently in technical and design issues.

This is what you bring with you

  • You have an education as a carpenter or an equivalent basic technical training.
  • You work independently and like to take responsibility.
  • You keep track even in hectic moments.
  • You enjoy precise work and appreciate the variety between practice and office.
  • With computer-assisted order processing systems, you are safe and efficient.

What we offer you

  • A secure and exciting workplace in an established, innovative Swiss family business.
  • A young, dynamic team that supports each other and achieves great things together.
  • An open corporate culture with flat hierarchies and short decision-making paths.
  • An optimal location in terms of traffic – conveniently accessible and modernly equipped.
  • In addition, many other benefits such as birthday free, company events, company vehicles and and ... are waiting for YOU!
