Full description

Your role with us

IT Business Analyst for Dayforce and other HR applications: Ensuring stable operations, administration and continuous optimization Central IT contact person for HR for system, integration and technical issues Responsibility for roadmap, evaluation, upgrade and release planning together with HR with focus on integrations and technical quality Responsibility for the entire lifecycle of SaaS and on-premise applications: onboarding, changes, upgrades and decommissioning Analysis of HR-

  • Requirements and translation into technical documentation incl. Interfaces, data flows and dependencies to environmental systems

Translation of HR-

  • Requirements in implementable system solutions, including assessment of the impact on configuration, interfaces and systems

Coordination of HR integrations and data flows (including D365) as well as design, documentation and maintenance of interfaces (e.g. file-based, API/REST, SCIM) Testing, adaptation and validation of configuration and data files for system uploads and integrations Hands-on analysis and resolution of incidents incl. root cause analysis

  • Coordination of 3rd-level support with providers, implementation partners and IT teams

Coordination of tests and acceptances for releases, changes, integrations and annual HR processes

This is what they bring with them.

Completed studies in (economic) computer science or a comparable education with corresponding professional experience Several years of experience as an IT Application Manager, IT Business Analyst or in a comparable function in the enterprise environment Strong technical affinity and practical experience with SaaS applications, cloud solutions and system integrations In-depth knowledge of interfaces, APIs and data-based integrations Experience in the analysis of technical problems and cooperation with integration, infrastructure and development teams Knowledge of IT service management processes and IT governance frameworks, ideally according to ITIL Experience in controlling external service providers and implementation partners Knowledge of HR system landscapes, ideally with Dayforce

  • Experience with D365 is an advantage

Strong communication and stakeholder management capabilities at the interface between business and IT Structured, binding working methods, high integrity and discretion in the handling of personal data Very good knowledge of English, good knowledge of German advantageous

What this role is not

No Product Owner: The role requires a sound technical understanding of applications, integrations and identity management. No professional HR role: HR is responsible for processes, content and professional priorities; the role is responsible for the applications and their technical implementation. No software developer: Technical analysis and configuration understanding are required, programming and software development are not in the foreground. No isolated role: The close cooperation with Identity & Access Management, Integration, Development and Security is an integral part of the office.
