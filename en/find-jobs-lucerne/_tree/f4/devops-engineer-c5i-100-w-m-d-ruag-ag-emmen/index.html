Full description

Every day, around 3,000 employees of RUAG and RUAG Real Estate make a significant contribution to the security of Switzerland. They ensure that the Swiss Army and other operational and security organizations can perform their tasks comprehensively at any time.

You can move that

  • Planning, development and optimization of integration solutions for complex ICT platforms with a focus on stable and efficient system landscapes
  • Automation of development and operational processes as well as further development of CI/CD-related deployments and integration processes
  • Development of scripts, interfaces and technical integrations between applications, components and modules
  • Responsibility for API design and management and processing of data formats and data transformations
  • Establishment and support of messaging, event streaming and ETL processes as well as integration of modern IPaaS solutions
  • Ensuring the required development, testing and production systems including networks and container platforms
  • Monitoring the availability, performance, recoverability and resource consumption of applications as well as troubleshooting and monitoring
  • Maintenance and further development of system technical documentation

You bring that with you

  • Degree in computer science or vocational apprenticeship as computer scientist/in EFZ or technically skilled/intern with corresponding further education and professional experience
  • In-depth experience in the field of integrations, interface management and automation of system landscapes
  • Knowledge of API design, data formats, data transformation and messaging and event streaming technologies
  • Experience with GitLab and with automation tools such as CI/CD, Ansible or Terraform
  • Experience with container and virtualization technologies and ideally with Kubernetes, VMware and Nutanix
  • Analytical, independent and proactive working method with a high solution orientation
  • Negotiating knowledge of German
  • Knowledge of English and/or French is advantageous
  • Experience in regulated industries is beneficial
  • Military career as a senior non-commissioned officer or officer of advantage

Over the area

The Business Area C5I of RUAG MRO Holding AG supports the Swiss Army in the realization of ICT projects with a specific focus on the Cyber Command. This is your chance to contribute to highly secure and sovereign IT systems. Over the past eight years, we have grown from a small “start-up” from zero to more than 260 employees and offer a unique, dynamic environment.
