Put to the Point

Are you looking for an exciting challenge as part of the introduction of the new F-35A fighter aircraft and would like to help shape the future of the Swiss Air Force? In this newly created function, you are responsible for the safety and protection of the construction site within the framework of the F-35 new buildings.

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: temporary
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20910
  • Location: Rüeggisingerstrasse 165, 6032 Emmen

You can make this contribution

  • Ensure the safety and protection of F-35 new buildings at Emmen military airfield
  • Supervise construction personnel in the critical areas of the construction site and ensure controls in and access to the various sectors and infrastructures
  • Coordinate and monitor construction work in compliance with applicable safety requirements
  • Continuously update the complete documentation of the various construction phases and construction site opening times
  • Supervise the unloading and storage of sensitive goods, as well as the security checks on goods and construction personnel

This makes them unique.

  • Practical experience in functions associated with the safety of aerodromes, structures or critical infrastructure
  • Telescopic forklift driver's license and knowledge of security control equipment are beneficial
  • Swiss citizen with impeccable reputation
  • Good active knowledge in a second official language as well as English skills are beneficial

Your commitment to security and freedom

The Command Operations (Kdo Op) is responsible for all military operations and operations of the Swiss Army. The Kdo Op includes the Luftwaffe, the Army, the four territorial divisions, the SWISSINT Competence Center, the Special Forces Command and the Military Police. Around 40% of the approximately 2,500 employees are employed by civilians – from engineers to aircraft mechanics to analysts.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.
