Full description

Stay healthy, get healthy or live with an illness. That is what we are here for. For over 1.7 million people in Switzerland. That your commitment makes sense is obvious. As a trusted physician you advise our service specialists in all medical questions. You act as an intermediary between service providers, the CSS and, if applicable, the insured. You will be supported by an assistant team and by your colleagues. Sounds good? When can we meet you?

This is what your everyday life looks like

  • At the heart of it: You assess the obligation to provide services in the mandatory health insurance according to Art. 57 KVG.
  • You can make well-founded recommendations for the CSS.
  • With your medical expertise, you advise in particular performance experts on medical and insurance issues.
  • Between service providers, the CSS and, if necessary, the insured you convey and explain your medical recommendation understandably.
  • In complex cases, you communicate your assessment directly with the responsible service providers.
  • At the interface of the trusted medical service and IT, you actively participate in ongoing implementation projects and advance them with your knowledge of AI and data.

You bring that with you

  • First things first: You are a specialist and have worked for at least five years in a medical practice or in a senior medical position.
  • Leadership experience as well as a broad medical knowledge help you to assess even complex questions safely.
  • Ideally, you have completed the Swiss Society of Confidence Physicians (SGV) certificate and a management training – but neither is a must.
  • You can safely use digital tools, bring in-depth IT skills and be interested in AI, interfaces and large language models (LLMs).
  • Very good knowledge of German is a matter of course for you
  • French, Italian and/or English are beneficial.
  • As a trustworthy, collegial personality, you communicate in an advisory and on an equal footing – and promote cooperation in the team and with our partners.
  • Work Flexible: Adapt your work to your lifestyle annually with part-time options and other flexible models like home office and more.
  • Choose your holiday model: 5 or 6 weeks holiday? Your decision. Accordingly, you work 41 or 42 hours a week.
  • Stay fit: Ergonomic workplaces, promotion of mental well-being and many other offers around your health.
  • Engage Socially: Does Life Mean Good to You? Then let others share your happiness and expand your horizons alone or in a team.
  • Enjoy your family life: Make time for your offspring: 3 weeks for fathers, for mothers up to 20 weeks. There are also generous child and education allowances.
  • Celebrate with us: Focused work and celebrate. We love this combination and maintain it regularly.
