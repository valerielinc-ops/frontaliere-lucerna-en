Full description

As one of the world’s leading clinics, we develop an individualized treatment program with the aim of enabling our patients to have the highest possible degree of independence and freedom of life, thereby optimizing their quality of life. We address both patients from the Swiss cash system and international patients. cereneo offers highly specialized rehabilitation programs for people with neurological diseases and injuries, including stroke, brain injury, neurodegenerative diseases such as Parkinson’s, multiple sclerosis and many other neurological disorders.

Tasks

  • Collaboration in the medical care of patients with neurological diseases (e.g. after stroke, cranial brain trauma, Parkinson's disease, MS)
  • Support for assistant and senior physicians: women in clinical everyday life
  • Participation in visits, interdisciplinary meetings and therapy sessions
  • Documentation and administrative activities in the clinical information system
  • Close cooperation with therapy, care and case management

What should you bring?

  • Advanced medical studies (from 4th year of study) or recently completed studies
  • Interest in Neurology and Rehabilitation
  • Very good knowledge of German (C1), knowledge of English is advantageous
  • High motivation, willingness to learn and teamwork
  • Self-employed, reliable and responsible work

We offer

  • Modern infrastructure: Perfectly located working environment with a view of Lake Vierwaldstättersee, Pilatus and Rigi.
  • Development: Personal training and development opportunities.
  • Social benefits: Good social benefits (NBU, employer share pension fund 60%).
  • Parking & public transport: Free parking and access to e-charging stations, subsidy for public transport.
  • Food: Lunch check with 50% employer participation, free coffee, tea, water & fruit.
  • Kita: Discount for our connected Kita.
  • Discounts: Various discounts for recreational activities.
  • Fitness: Sharing fitness equipment.

Our corporate culture

  • EXCELLENCE: Using the latest research findings and first-class service
  • CHARACTERISTICS: We are always there with all our heart. Of course, this also includes the sincere treatment of our patients: inside, but also between our staff
  • MOTIVATION: Joint goal achievement and support of patients: women to improve their quality of life
  • TEAM WORK: Reliability and mutual support in the team
  • INNOVATION: Research and testing of new therapeutic approaches and devices

If you are an open, committed person who would like to work in an interdisciplinary team and continuously develop, we look forward to your application. Become part of our team and actively shape the future of neurological rehabilitation.
