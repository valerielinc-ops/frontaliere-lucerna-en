Full description

Our heart beats for Swiss gastronomy. We are committed to our customers. In our 31 pick-up markets, by field service and delivery, as well as in the digital world. With a versatile range, attractive private brands, professional specialist advice and innovative services.

Transgourmet Schweiz AG is a member of the international Transgourmet Group. The company is a leader in Swiss wholesale and pick-up wholesale and has around 2,100 employees and 100,000 customers (restaurants, hotels, canteens, homes and hospitals as well as retail).

Tasks

  • Representation of the management and assumption of overall responsibility in the absence of the management
  • Co-responsibility for the organisational, technical, financial and personnel management of the market
  • Management and support of department managers and about 40 employees
  • Ensuring a high level of willingness to sell and smooth daily business
  • Ensuring active customer support and attractive product presentation
  • Ensuring high freshness and quality performance in the market
  • Implementation of efficient operations and compliance with processes and guidelines
  • Bringing entrepreneurial thinking, leadership and customer orientation to the sustainable success of the market

Requirements

  • Basic training in retail trade or gastronomy with further training in retail (at least retail specialists) or hotel schools
  • Experience in a comparable function in the trade or in the gastronomy
  • Entrepreneurial thinking and winning leadership with multi-year management experience and high social skills
  • Excellent customer orientation and joy in the active customer care of our diverse professional customers
  • Enjoying dealing with people, numbers and organisational tasks
  • Conceptual, in-house and networked thinking
  • Ideally resident and anchored in the region
