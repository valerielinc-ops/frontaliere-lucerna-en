Full description

The clinic for sleep medicine (KSM) of ZURZACH Care in Lucerne specializes in the clarification and treatment of sleep disorders. Our interdisciplinary team takes care of patients on site and online. For our clinic administration in Lucerne, we are looking for an employee who likes to work with people and enjoys organizing everyday practice.

Your tasks

  • You receive our patients and are the first point of contact for their concerns on the phone and by e-mail. You are also in contact with assigning agencies and payers.
  • You edit assignments and coordinate initial interviews, investigations and checks. You lead the electronic agendas and coordinate the appointments with the specialist areas.
  • You collect and maintain patient data and keep the electronic dossiers up-to-date. You will process the clinic and patient correspondence and support the specialists in preparing and sending medical reports.
  • You collect outpatient services, control the performance recording and provide invoices to insurance companies and patients. Among other things, you work with TARDOC.
  • You track open pendencies and keep an eye on due therapy controls. Together with specialists from sleep medicine, psychology and diagnostics, you ensure smooth administrative processes.
  • You will do general secretarial work such as postal processing and contribute your ideas to improve our processes.

Your profile

  • You have completed a commercial education or training as a medical practice assistant (MPA). Healthcare experience is beneficial.
  • You work safely with MS Office. Experience with a practice or clinic information system as well as knowledge of ambulatory billing are advantageous.
  • You work independently and carefully. Even if several requests are received at the same time, you set priorities and keep track.
  • You communicate in a friendly and clear way, enjoy working in a team and are interested in sleep medicine.
  • You have good knowledge of German in words and writing. Further language skills are welcome.

This is what we offer you

  • A collegial, appreciative and humorous team that supports each other.
  • A modern working environment and the opportunity to actively shape administrative processes.
  • Above-average social security benefits and a generous share of costs in education and training.
  • Discounts with various partners and access to Benefit@work.
  • Free admission and other benefits in the thermal bath FORTYSEVEN.

We look forward to your application directly via our online application tool.
