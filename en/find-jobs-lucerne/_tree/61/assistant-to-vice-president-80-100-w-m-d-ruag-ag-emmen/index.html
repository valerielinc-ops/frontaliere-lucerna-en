Full description

As an Assistant to Vice President, you are a central hub within the business field. You support the management and the entire management team on organizational, administrative and coordinative topics, ensure an efficient flow of information and keep an eye on appointments, pendencies and important tasks.

You work with different organizational units, managers, employees and external partners. In addition to classic assistance tasks, you take on smaller projects and operational topics and actively participate in the further development of processes.

You can move that

  • You support the leadership and the management team in organizational, administrative and coordination tasks.
  • You coordinate appointments, meetings, workshops and business events and ensure their preparation and follow-up.
  • You organize the mail and calendar management, coordinate pendencies and keep an eye on deadlines.
  • You prepare presentations, decision bases, reports and other management documents and keep minutes.
  • You coordinate information across different organizational units and ensure an efficient flow of information.
  • You take on operational tasks in SAP, such as opening orders and processing business processes.
  • You lead smaller projects and independently take on topics such as the organization of training programs or OE projects.
  • You recognize potential for improvement and support the further development of administrative processes.

You bring that with you

Professional and technical knowledge

  • You have a commercial education or a comparable qualification.
  • You will bring professional experience in an assistant or coordination role, ideally in the ICT environment.
  • Ideally, you have experience with SAP, especially in the processing and tracking of orders, orders, invoices and cost centers.
  • You have good knowledge of Microsoft 365 as well as digital document management and collaboration tools.
  • You are experienced in data preparation, reporting and appointment, task and pending management.

Organisational and methodological competences

  • You are an organizational talent and work independently, structured and priority-oriented.
  • You can combine complex information from different sources and prepare it for the addressee.
  • You organize meetings, workshops and events independently and recognize potential for improvement in administrative processes.
  • You have a quick understanding and safely deal with confidential and strategically relevant information.

Communication

  • You have very good oral and written German skills as well as good English skills
  • Knowledge of French is an advantage.
  • You communicate stylishly at different hierarchical levels and act professionally towards managers, internal specialist positions and external partners.
  • You are characterized by a pronounced service and customer orientation.

Personal competences

  • You are trustworthy, discreet and with integrity.
  • You work independently, take your own initiative and are reliable.
  • You are a structured, solution-oriented and set personality.
