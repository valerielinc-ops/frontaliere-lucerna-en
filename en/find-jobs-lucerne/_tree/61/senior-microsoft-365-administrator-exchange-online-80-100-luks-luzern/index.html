Full description

The Lucerne Canton Hospital with its locations in Lucerne, Sursee and Wolhusen is a company of the LUKS Group and thus belongs to one of the leading hospital groups in Switzerland. The more than 8,500 employees are there with warmth, competence and commitment around the clock for the well-being of patients. The clinics and institutes of the LUKS Group offer medical services of the highest quality.

Exchange is more than just email for you? For us too.
We are not just looking for someone to administer Exchange Online. We are looking for someone who thinks, takes responsibility and fits our team.
Because behind Exchange is a platform that many people rely on every day. News must arrive, accesses must be right and changes must not suddenly turn operations on their heads.
We are a small Microsoft 365 team and are looking for reinforcement immediately or by agreement, who wants to design and develop Exchange Online together with us. Tasks: • Together with an experienced colleague, you take on a central role for Exchange Online. Mailflow, connectors, transport rules, permissions, shared mailboxes and resources are part of your everyday life. Together you ensure that our environment functions reliably and evolves • It usually gets interesting where something does not go according to plan. If messages do not arrive, Microsoft changes something or a new request can not be solved with a few clicks, get to the root of the cause and find a solution that will still work tomorrow • You think about security at Exchange Online right from the start, identify possible risks and develop our e-mail environment together with our specialists for security, compliance and identities • Automate recurring work with PowerShell and consider how to improve existing processes • You will also encounter teams, SharePoint and other Microsoft 365 services as well as joint projects in everyday life. But the clear focus remains Exchange Online Requirements • You have worked with Exchange Online and Microsoft 365 for several years, ideally in a larger environment. Mailflow, permissions, policies, Microsoft Entra ID, PowerShell and email security are familiar topics for you. Very good knowledge of German and good knowledge of English also bring you • If something doesn’t work, don’t just look for the fastest workaround. You want to understand why a problem has arisen, question connections and find solutions that work sustainably • You can also explain complex technical topics in an understandable way, enjoy exchanging ideas with other experts and speak openly if you see something differently or see a better solution. • As a team, you share your knowledge, support others and are just as willing to learn from your colleagues. • Experience with hybrid Exchange environments or Microsoft certificates is welcome, but not a requirement We offer: • They work in a Microsoft 365 environment with around 13,000 user objects. Together with an experienced colleague, you take responsibility for Exchange Online and have the freedom to contribute your own ideas and develop things further. • We are a small team with short distances. We help each other, share our knowledge and talk openly about when something doesn’t work or we can do something better. • They mostly work in the home office. Nevertheless, as a team we are in regular exchange, supporting ourselves with challenges and taking time for joint solutions • With us you do not have to know everything. Much more important to us is that you remain curious, question things and also dare to tackle problems for which there is still no finished guide. • If Exchange Online is your topic and you do not just want to manage, but think and shape, we should get to know each other
