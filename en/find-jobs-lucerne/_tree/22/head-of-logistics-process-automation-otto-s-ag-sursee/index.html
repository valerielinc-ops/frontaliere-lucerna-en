Full description

Do you want to actively shape the future of our logistics? Are you enthusiastic about efficient processes, intelligent logistics systems and innovative automation solutions? You think strategically, but also like to take on yourself? Then you are exactly right with us! In order to strengthen our logistics management, we are looking for an entrepreneurially minded and leadership-strong personality who is responsible for our Logistics Process & Automation division and develops it sustainably.

Area of activity

  • Overall responsibility for the management, control and strategic development of the Logistics Process & Automation division
  • Lead strategic logistics and automation projects, lead and develop the team, and work closely with internal stakeholders and external partners
  • Development and implementation of a future-oriented process, digitalization and automation strategy
  • Analysis, standardization and continuous optimization of logistics and material flow processes as well as automated plants
  • Establishing and further developing effective KPI and process management and promoting an active CIP culture
  • Further development of logistics systems, digital process control and system integration

Benefits

  • Family, owner-managed company
  • Business car incl. Fuel card
  • Attractive shopping perks online and in retail
  • 5 weeks of holiday (6 weeks from age 50)
  • Diverse training opportunities
  • Modern and high-quality employee restaurant
  • Generous loyalty bonuses and legendary staff festival
  • Tickets for various games of FC Lucerne, EV train, HC Ambri-Piotta, SC Bern and Lausanne HC
  • Attractive advantages at myOTTO'S partners
  • Additional remuneration on the old capital of the BVG

Profile

  • Completed higher education (HF/FH/University) in logistics, supply chain management, industrial engineering, engineering or comparable qualification
  • Several years of professional and management experience in a comparable function, ideally in a larger logistics or distribution environment
  • In-depth knowledge in the optimization and automation of complex logistics and material flow processes
  • Experience with warehouse management, ERP and material flow control systems as well as a strong affinity for digitalization and new technologies
  • Demonstrate experience in managing and successfully implementing challenging change and investment projects
