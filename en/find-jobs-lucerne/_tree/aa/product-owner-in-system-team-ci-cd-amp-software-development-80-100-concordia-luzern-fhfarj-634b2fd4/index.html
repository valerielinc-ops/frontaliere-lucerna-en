Full description

Honest collaboration: 100 % healthy Tribschenstrasse 4 6005 Lucerne You do not simply want to further develop an existing product, but build a new technical capability for our entire IT organization? Then this role could be exactly right for you. With your passion for software development, automation, and CI/CD, as the technically skilled Product Owner of the System Team, you will shape our technical foundations, platforms, and standards for software development and modern IT operations. In doing so, you will support our teams in delivering applications and software faster, more securely, and with higher quality.

What you can expect

In an initial phase, your focus will be on three key areas

  • You will be responsible for establishing and further developing standardized CI/CD pipelines for our core technology stacks.
  • Together with the stakeholders, you will define binding minimum standards for code, security, and automation.
  • You will support the teams in using pipelines and standards and ensure that these are understandable, usable, and effectively embedded.

In the next step, you will further develop the System Team into a central enabler for additional parts of the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC):

  • You will drive automation across the entire development and delivery process.
  • You will promote professional software development with clear standards, effective automation, and a high degree of maturity.
  • You will identify high-value areas, prioritize them, and develop them further with the team - for example in the areas of test management, environment management, release management, or development standards.
  • You will lay the foundation for consistent framework conditions, best practices, and reusable solutions.
  • You will ensure that the System Team grows with the organization's requirements and continuously develops and deliberately expands its impact.

What you bring

  • You have an excellent understanding of modern software development, technical platforms, and collaboration across organizational boundaries.
  • You think strategically while also being technically effective in implementation.
  • You understand the requirements of modern software development and bring solid, several years of experience in areas such as CI/CD, automation, testing, release management, integration, and DevOps - ideally from complex organizations.
  • You are confident navigating demanding IT landscapes - including the interplay between standard software, in-house developments, cloud services, and on-premises systems.
  • You can develop a clear product vision and structure and prioritize technical topics, translating them into an actionable roadmap.
  • You act confidently in an environment with various stakeholders and can establish common goals and binding guardrails and embed them effectively within an organization.
  • You think strategically, but remain pragmatic and solution-oriented in implementation. You recognize where standardization makes sense and where teams need the necessary freedom.
  • You have a strong command of the German language and communicate clearly, in an audience-appropriate manner and convincingly.
