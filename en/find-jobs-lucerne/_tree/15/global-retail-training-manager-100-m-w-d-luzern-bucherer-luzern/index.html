Full description

Your role with us

Responsible for planning, coordinating and delivering global retail excellence training to strengthen a consistent customer experience and unified understanding of service across our international retail teams Conception, coordination and further development of product and specialist training as well as international certification programs to promote a sound product understanding and professional customer advice

  • Build and coordinate an international trainer pool and ensure consistent implementation of global retail training programs across all markets
  • Analysis of training needs, quality indicators, customer feedback and relevant business KPIs as well as derivation and implementation of suitable development and training measures
  • Ensuring a coordinated and target-group-oriented retail training offer by coordinating all retail-related training activities across different disciplines
  • Collaboration in international Learning & Development initiatives and retail projects as well as advising internal departments on training and development topics

This is what they bring with them.

Completed studies in business administration, psychology, pedagogy, adult education, human resources or a comparable discipline or equivalent training with relevant professional experience

  • Further education in the area of learning & development, adult education, training or coaching is advantageous
  • Several years of professional experience in the retail, luxury goods, watch, jewellery or premium environment as well as good knowledge in the field of customer consulting, service excellence and customer experience
  • Experience in the conception, further development and implementation of training, development and certification programs as well as coaching-on-the-job measures
  • Strong communication, presentation and moderation capabilities
  • High organisational strength, structured working methods and experience in stakeholder management in international organisations
  • Affinity to luxury goods and interest in watches and jewelry
  • Negotiable German and English, French advantageous
