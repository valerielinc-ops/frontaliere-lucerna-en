Full description

The Lucerne Canton Hospital with its locations in Lucerne, Sursee and Wolhusen is a company of the LUKS Group and thus belongs to one of the leading hospital groups in Switzerland. The more than 8,500 employees are there with warmth, competence and commitment around the clock for the well-being of patients. The clinics and institutes of the LUKS Group offer medical services of the highest quality.

For our team we are looking for 01. September 2026 or by agreement reinforcement. Tasks: • Their main tasks include carrying out various pneumological examinations such as spirometry, body plethysmography and bronchoprovocation tests as well as arterial and venous blood sampling. • They assist in various investigations such as bronchoscopies, pleural punctures, pleural drainages and spiroergometries. After training and qualification, you will also take over the sedation during bronchoscopies under medical supervision. • If necessary, you support the secretariat, take over telephone service and reception, coordinate appointments and organize consultation hours • They perform administrative tasks such as electronic patient documentation, performance recording and various logistical activities Requirements • You have completed training as a medical practice assistant (MPA) or health specialist (FaGe) and ideally already have professional experience • A stylish German rounds off your profile; for foreign-language diplomas, we also require a language level of at least B2 • You are an open-minded, communicative and resilient personality who shows a willingness to learn and enjoys your profession • The well-being of our patients is the focus of your attention, and you always treat people with respect and appreciation. We offer: • You can expect a varied, exciting and dynamic area of responsibility • Grow with us: After a careful familiarization, we offer you further education opportunities. To carry out the sedation, visit two modules of the course FaGe acute at Xund. The costs shall be borne by LUKS; The commitment period shall be one year after completion of the contract. • We offer you the opportunity to work independently and be part of a dedicated team. The co-creation of work processes by our employees is expressly appreciated • You benefit from regular working hours that allow you to optimally reconcile work and private life with a working time regulation from Monday to Friday.
