fenaco is the largest agricultural cooperative in Switzerland. With more than 80 subsidiaries, we operate in Switzerland and Europe. Together, we ensure that valuable Swiss food products make it from the earth to the table – from the earth to the table. We are looking for you to help us shape the agriculture of tomorrow. Are you keen to take on responsibility, are you an ambitious up-and-coming professional and want to get off to a strong start in the Swiss agricultural sector? Then you have come to the right place! After successfully completing the programme, you aspire to a position of responsibility with management duties in the fenaco-LANDI-Gruppe. Your place of work is in Sursee, with assignments in the Central Switzerland region as well as in other parts of the country.

Sandra Anderegg Head of HR Central Switzerland +41 58 434 41 40

These tasks will inspire you

  • Two years, many opportunities: In our on-the-job trainee programme, you will gain a broad insight into various companies and business areas of the fenaco-LANDI-Gruppe (e.g. UFA, AGROLA, LANDI, Anicom, crop production, etc.)
  • Leadership-ready in theory and practice: You will get a taste of sales, marketing, finance, business administration and project work – all with the aim of preparing you for a leadership role
  • You get around: Whether regionally or in other parts of the country – your field of work is varied and offers you exciting prospects
  • Your goal: After successfully completing the programme, you aspire to a position of responsibility with management duties in the fenaco-LANDI-Gruppe

The profile we are looking for

  • You have completed further training as an agro-commercial specialist HF, agrotechnician HF, agronomist FH or ETH – or an equivalent qualification with a proven practical focus on agriculture
  • You want more: take on responsibility, lead teams and shape the future – ideally in one of our companies
  • Communication comes naturally to you: you speak and write German fluently and can communicate well in French
  • You are open-minded, motivated and flexible: you see assignments in different regions of Switzerland and in different teams as an exciting opportunity, not a hurdle

Discover your benefits

  • Training and continuing education: We invest in the development of our employees. A broad range of internal educational opportunities is open to everyone, and we support external continuing education.
  • Family leave: Mothers receive 100 percent of their insured salary during the 18 weeks of maternity leave. The other parent receives 3 weeks of family leave at full pay.
  • Attractive vacation policy: Our vacation policy guarantees all employees at least 5 weeks of vacation. As of 50. Year of age, it is 6 weeks and from the 60. Year of age, even 7 weeks. In addition, it is possible to purchase additional vacation days.
  • Discounts and reductions: Our employees benefit, among other things, from discounts when shopping and refueling, credits on REKA-Geld, as well as discounted mobile subscriptions for themselves and their families.
  • Insurance benefits: We provide our employees with above-average social security and pension benefits and offer special terms on supplementary insurance policies with various health insurers for their entire family.
