Full description

Our parent veterinary parcels are a central component of Bell Schweiz AG’s integrated poultry production. On the parent farm in Herbligen near Thun you play a central role in modern poultry production. Under the guidance of the farm manager, you are actively involved – from the daily care of the fattening parents to the hatching egg production. You monitor and report animal health in accordance with the guidelines, ensure the smooth operation of the technical facilities and carefully record all relevant production data. In this way, you directly contribute to a contemporary, safe and sustainable food production and move in a responsible working environment with clear quality standards.

Tasks
