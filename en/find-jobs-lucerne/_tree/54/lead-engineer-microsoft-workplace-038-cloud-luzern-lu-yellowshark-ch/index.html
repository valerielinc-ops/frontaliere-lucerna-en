02 Your role

  • You design and operate Microsoft environments for clients, from server infrastructure and Microsoft 365 to cloud workloads, in a data center or on the client's premises.
  • You lead technical projects independently, from clarifying requirements and implementation through to handover.
  • For selected clients, you are their trusted technical advisor: you advise them on further development and prioritization, and think beyond day-to-day business.
  • You analyze incidents, eliminate their causes and ensure that problems do not recur.
  • You automate recurring processes and continuously improve existing solutions.
  • You share your knowledge with colleagues and support the professional development of the team.
