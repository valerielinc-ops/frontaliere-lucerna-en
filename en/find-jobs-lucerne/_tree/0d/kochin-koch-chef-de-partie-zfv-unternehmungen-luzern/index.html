Full description

This workplace includes a self-service restaurant, a café bar, the table-service restaurant "Brasserie" with a terrace, and the area for internal and external catering. This meeting place in Lucerne provides an inviting spot for museum visitors and connoisseurs to gather. Our team delights an average of 902 guests here every day with a diverse range of offerings – from high-quality cuisine made with fresh produce from the market to regionally and organically produced dishes, as well as large-scale catering for up to 2,500 people. Are you our new team member who will support us as a chef (Chef de Partie) from 1st December 2026 or by arrangement? You will work flexibly from Monday to Sunday in a year-round operation. You can plan your holidays freely. Working hours vary between 8:00 a.m. and 11:30 p.m., depending on whether you are on the early, middle or late shift. Your list of responsibilities:

  • Versatile work in various roles at the Mercato self-service restaurant and the Brasserie à la carte restaurant
  • Ensuring a professional and high-quality lunch and banqueting service
  • Assisting with banquets, in-house catering, drinks receptions and special events throughout the Swiss Museum of Transport
  • Support with goods receiving and professional warehouse management
  • Actively contributing own ideas to menu design and seasonal menu changes
  • Shared responsibility for the professional support and further development of our assistants and trainees
  • Ensuring cleanliness and order at the assigned workstation
  • Carrying out general tidying and cleaning work
  • Implementation of ZFV quality and service standards
  • Compliance with and implementation of hygiene and occupational safety guidelines Your recipe for success:
  • Completed vocational training as a chef: Swiss Federal Certificate of Proficiency (EFZ)
  • 2–4 years of professional experience in the kitchen
  • A love of cooking and high standards of quality and presentation
  • Team spirit and a reliable, independent way of working
  • Customer focus and willingness to adapt to new situations
  • Openness to new ideas and enjoyment of developing your professional skills further
  • Very good spoken and written German skills What we offer you: We create teams that support each other and in which you will be nurtured. We work together and focus on everything that is beneficial. Among other things, we attach great importance to pay transparency and equal pay. For this position, depending on your experience and qualifications, we offer an annual salary of between CHF 56,000 and CHF 62,400 (incl. 13. Monthly salary). If you would like to share your passion for the catering industry with us, we look forward to receiving your complete online application.
