Full description

With around 65 employees, Spitex Emmen is a modern, versatile and committed employer. We provide high-quality ambulatory health care for the approximately 33,000 inhabitants of the municipality of Emmen and actively promote the nursing profession of tomorrow as a training company. As of August 2027, we offer motivated, dynamic personalities the opportunity to complete a versatile education. We still have a place for you as Apprentice/Expert Health EFZ 100% Vocational training at Spitex Emmen means direct contact with clients from day one. Interesting encounters with people of all ages and different backgrounds make this activity an exciting task. The EFZ training course lasts three years and is completed with the Swiss Federal Certificate of Competence (EFZ). A motivated and well-qualified vocational training team accompanies and supports you throughout your training. After a well-founded introduction, you can work on the following tasks:

  • Care and support of our clients at home
  • Support our clients in the design of everyday life
  • Taking over of medical engineering services
  • Taking over of domestic work
  • Implementation of administrative and logistical tasks

What do you have to bring for this versatile education

  • Joy and interest in meeting people of different ages and origins every day
  • Good psychological conditions so that you can take on responsibility and would like to work independently
  • Willingness to ride the e-bike to our clients
  • Good oral and written knowledge of German
  • Warm and open nature and a good resilience

For questions we are there for you Further information on the training can be obtained from Pia Zimmerli, Head of HR, Tel 041 244 09 10. Further information can be found at www.spitex-emmen.ch. Your application reaches us via bewerbungen@spitex-emmen.ch. On the website www.spitex-emmen you will find more information about us. We look forward to your application!
