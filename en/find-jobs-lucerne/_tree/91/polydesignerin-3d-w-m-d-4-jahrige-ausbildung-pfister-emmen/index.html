Full description

At Pfister, we pursue a vision: We want to bring our products into every Swiss home. To achieve this, we are looking for dedicated support. Help us bring the approximately 40'000 products in our range into the homes of our customers. Whether in one of the 18 branches or at the headquarters in Suhr: you can expect a unique environment, work with appealing products and, last but not least, appreciation and respect for your commitment. Around 1'300 people already work at Pfister today - will you be joining us soon?

For the start of the apprenticeship in 2027, we are looking for a/an

Specialization: Styling - 4-year apprenticeship

Your tasks

  • During the 4-year apprenticeship as a 3D polydesigner, you will learn the craft of three-dimensional design from trained professionals
  • You will learn how inspiring, sales-promoting presentations are implemented, from the design through planning and organization to execution
  • For this purpose, you will have countless accessories, materials and lighting options at your disposal, which you will become thoroughly familiar with during your apprenticeship

What you bring

  • You have a flair for colors and shapes
  • In addition, you enjoy designing harmonious spaces
  • You enjoy craft work and can put your skills to full use when creating and presenting decorative objects
  • You have completed the aptitude test of the professional association Polydesign 3D (please include the test results with your application)
