Full description

Pensum: 80-100 %

You can design it.

  • You win and support B2C and SME customers for CKW Flex Solar – from the first contact to the conclusion of the contract.
  • You develop a structured sales pipeline, process leads promptly and document activities and financial statements reliably in the CRM.
  • You clarify needs, translate them into suitable standard offers and create offers and contracts based on existing pricing models.
  • You coordinate customer onboarding, answer inquiries and, if necessary, coordinate the connection of flexibility systems with internal interfaces.
  • You bring insights from customer discussions, market potential and observations about the competition into product development, pricing and marketing.
  • You participate in digital sales campaigns, webinars and customer events and develop the sales of Flex Solar together with the team.

That makes you

  • You have completed a degree at Bachelor level or higher in a technical, mathematical, business or economic discipline.
  • You bring experience in the distribution of standardized offers, ideally in the B2C or SME environment.
  • You have a good technical understanding, for example for energy systems or photovoltaics, and can convey complex topics in an understandable way.
  • You work customer-oriented, structured and quality-conscious, organize your pipeline independently and act solution-oriented.
  • You enjoy working in an interdisciplinary team, actively bring your perspective and develop effective solutions together with others.
  • Ideally, you will be familiar with decentralized flexibilities, photovoltaics, CRM systems or digital sales and will use AI-supported tools for automation. You communicate negotiably in German, English and French are nice to have.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Lucerne?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Lucerne's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Lucerne's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
