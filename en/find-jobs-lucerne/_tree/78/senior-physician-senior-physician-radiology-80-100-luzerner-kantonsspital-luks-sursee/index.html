Full description

The Lucerne Canton Hospital with its locations in Lucerne, Sursee and Wolhusen is a company of the LUKS Group and thus belongs to one of the leading hospital groups in Switzerland. The more than 8,500 employees are there with warmth, competence and commitment around the clock for the well-being of patients. The clinics and institutes of the LUKS Group offer medical services of the highest quality.

For our team we are looking for 01. February 2027 or by agreement reinforcement. Tasks: • Together with your colleagues, you are responsible for the comprehensive radiological care of patients in one of the largest radiological networks in Switzerland. • At our location in Sursee, you work in a team of three radiologists and independently conduct and assess examinations from the entire spectrum of general radiology, especially in basic and emergency diagnostics. Special examinations are carried out and assessed in close coordination with the organ-specialized sections of the Lucerne Center at the Sursee site. • They are closely involved in the radiological network of the LUKS and maintain a regular professional exchange with the organ-specialised sections at the centre of Lucerne. • They participate in the service operations of the Radiological Network, including weekend and background services. There is also the opportunity to engage in teaching and clinical research Requirements • You have a completed specialist title in radiology, recognized by SIWF or MEBEKO, and have in-depth knowledge in the broad spectrum of diagnostic radiology. Additional organ-specific expertise is welcome • They appreciate the self-responsible work in a manageable team as well as the professional exchange and cooperation within a large radiological network. • A patient-oriented way of working, teamwork and open and appreciative communication with clinical colleagues and external referents distinguish you We offer: • Look forward to a varied and self-responsible radiological activity in a collegial and motivated team. The Sursee location combines the personal working atmosphere of a regional hospital with the technical expertise and possibilities of a large radiological centre. • A modern equipment park with a Siemens dual-source CT, a 3-Tesla-MRI (Philips MR 7000), digital X-ray, mammography with tomosynthesis, transillumination and ultrasound is available on site. • Due to the close connection to the organ-specialized sections in Lucerne, you benefit from regular case discussions, a comprehensive range of further and further training as well as the possibility of rotations between Sursee and the centre of Lucerne. This allows you to maintain existing sub-specialized competencies and further develop them in a targeted manner. • In the long term, our radiological network offers you a wide range of professional and personal development opportunities, including the prospect of taking on additional professional or location-related responsibility.
