You can look forward to that.

You play a central role in ensuring safe and reliable railway operations throughout Switzerland. A small and experienced team works closely together and supports each other. Your training takes place practically in everyday work and you develop continuously. The cooperation with different areas and missions at different locations bring a lot of variety and a broad insight. Benefits such as free GA, discounted travel abroad - also for the family - can be found on our website under Benefits. You can move that.

  • As an automatic technician:in or electrical installer:in the railway stream, you carry out installation, maintenance and maintenance work on switchgear and catenary remote controls and thus contribute to the safety and availability of the infrastructure.
  • Analyse disturbances quickly and repair them safely and efficiently.
  • You work with modern technology and program controls and protection systems.
  • You carry out inspections and functional checks and document the missions completely.
  • Your main field of application is in Central Switzerland, for example in the Lucerne area with operations throughout Switzerland.

You bring that with you.

  • Safety and responsibility are important to you and you act accordingly.
  • You enjoy working in a team and tackle tasks independently and flexibly.
  • Ideally, you have completed a basic electrotechnical training EFZ such as Automatiker:in, Elektroinstallateur:in or in a comparable profession.
  • You have knowledge of German at level C2 and ideally knowledge of a second national language at level A1.
  • You bring a willingness to travel within Central Switzerland and, if necessary, throughout Switzerland.
