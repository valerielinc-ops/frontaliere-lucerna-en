Full description

The Lucerne Canton Hospital with its locations in Lucerne, Sursee and Wolhusen is a company of the LUKS Group and thus belongs to one of the leading hospital groups in Switzerland. The more than 8,500 employees are there with warmth, competence and commitment around the clock for the well-being of patients. The clinics and institutes of the LUKS Group offer medical services of the highest quality.

For our team we are looking for reinforcement as of 1 February 2027 or by agreement. Tasks: • They are responsible for administering intravenous cytostatics and other hematological and oncological therapies and interventions • They perform plasmaphareses and stem cell collections. In this context, you are ready to provide cooking services (weekends about 1x per month, as well as holiday cooking) • They control all care steps of the specialist therapies taking into account the defined quality criteria • Counselling and supporting patients during their therapy are part of your daily work, providing the best possible support with empathy and expertise. Requirements • You have completed basic training as a nurse HF. Professional experience in haematology and/or oncology is beneficial • Knowledge of plasmapheresis and stem cell collection is desirable. If you do not yet have this knowledge, you are ready to acquire it and to continuously train yourself. • They are characterized by a robust personality that is able to keep a clear head even in demanding situations. In addition, you are adaptable, which allows you to quickly adapt to changing requirements and new challenges. • You have a strong social competence that enables you to treat our patients with sensitivity and respect, even in stressful situations We offer: • You benefit from a varied, interesting and lively area of responsibility • We offer you a pleasant working atmosphere in a modern department and a dedicated and very competent team • You benefit from a modern and pleasant infrastructure and the opportunity to participate in the design of the new processes • Monday to Friday operation, no shift service.
