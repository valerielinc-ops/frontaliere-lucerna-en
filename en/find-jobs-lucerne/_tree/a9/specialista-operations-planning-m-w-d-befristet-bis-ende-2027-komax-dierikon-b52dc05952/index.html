Full description

Your tasks

  • You support operational production and production planning during the SAP implementation and the subsequent stabilization phase and plan, process and monitor the corresponding orders.
  • You clear missing parts, material availability and planning deviations and coordinate the necessary measures with the relevant interfaces.
  • You maintain, check and clean material master data, parts lists, work plans and other planning-relevant data.
  • You support data migration from AX to SAP and ensure a reliable database through testing, validation and post-processing.
  • You will process operational faults and errors in the relevant SAP processes and accompany necessary corrections, tests and follow-up work.
  • You are actively involved in stabilizing, standardizing and optimizing S&OP processes as well as in updating process documentation.

Your profile

  • Technical or commercial-technical apprenticeship, ideally in a manufacturing company; further training in the area of processes, production or company processes is advantageous.
  • Professional experience in a manufacturing company, ideally in production or manufacturing planning, AVOR/PPS, supply chain or a comparable operational area.
  • Good understanding of production, manufacturing and material planning processes.
  • Understand the interplay between material master data, parts lists, work plans and production and manufacturing orders.
  • Good ERP and MS Office skills; SAP experience is beneficial.
  • Structured and careful working methods as well as a good understanding of data quality and process contexts.

Your benefits

Your new place of work in Dierikon is newly built and equipped with the latest technology and work equipment. Your workplace is located in one of the modern open-plan offices, where there are also inspiring meeting areas and a large terrace for various employee events. Komax AG offers its employees flexible working time models and home office. The further training of employees is important to Komax AG and offers, for example, free internal English courses or access to selected attractive learning platforms. At the Dierikon location, there are also modern break rooms and the possibility of a discounted lunch in a neighbouring partner restaurant.
