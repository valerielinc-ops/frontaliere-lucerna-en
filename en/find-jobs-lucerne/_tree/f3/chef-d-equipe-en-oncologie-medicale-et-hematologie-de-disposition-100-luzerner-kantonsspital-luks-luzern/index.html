The Lucerne Canton Hospital with its locations in Lucerne, Sursee and Wolhusen is a company of the LUKS Group and thus belongs to one of the leading hospital groups in Switzerland. The more than 8,500 employees are there with warmth, competence and commitment around the clock for the well-being of patients. The clinics and institutes of the LUKS Group offer medical services of the highest quality.

For our team we are looking for reinforcement as of 1 January 2027 or by agreement. As a team leader, you take over the professional and personnel leadership of several employees of different professional groups of the care team. • They look after and care for patients and their relatives from the point of view of patient- and family-centered care and carry out plasmapheresis and stem cell collections. Requirements • You have completed basic training as a nurse HF/FH with some years of professional experience, management experience. Experience already gained in the hematological and/or oncological field is advantageous • You are a resilient, flexible and capable person with high professional and social competence • As an initiative personality, you bring a high level of service and quality awareness as well as good expressive and communication skills • Your networked and pragmatic way of working and the understanding of complex organizations are very suitable for you in the implementation of the task We offer: - A modern infrastructure awaits you as well as the opportunity to develop, shape and implement new processes as a leader.
