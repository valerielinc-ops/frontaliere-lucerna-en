Location: Hirslanden Clinic St. Anna | Lucerne

Occupation by appointment

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 73128

Working at St. Anna Clinic means more than just working. The cornerstone of our trusting and close cooperation is the family coexistence that we maintain together with our patients.

Now we are looking for you for team addition in central sterilization!

YOUR TASKS

  • You are responsible for cleaning and sterilizing all instruments according to the applicable norms and laws
  • You package all instruments of the various disciplines carefully and coordinate the workflows forward-looking. You have a bright computer-assisted workstation with daylight available.
  • You are in close contact with the various interfaces, especially with the OR specialists
  • The cooperation in all shifts (day, late, pikett and weekend shifts) offers you a welcome change.

Your profile

  • You have the training to the techn. Sterilization Assistance Professional Course I completed or even completed training as a medical device technologist
  • In German you can communicate well and express yourself in writing
  • You like to work computer-assisted
  • You are flexible, ready for action and you are happy to take on responsibility

Our offer

  • We offer you an exciting task in a great team in our family clinic
  • You have the option of up to 7 weeks vacation per year
  • Do you like travelling with public transport or Velo? We contribute financially to your work journey
  • We work with work clothes, so you are entitled to up to 4 additional free days of paid changing time
  • Does it all sound good? Further information on employment conditions and additional offers for employees can be found here

You have questions? Contact Milos Jovanovic, Head of AEMP, at T or Merlin Abgottspon, HR recruitment partner, at merlin.abgottspon@hirslanden.ch.
