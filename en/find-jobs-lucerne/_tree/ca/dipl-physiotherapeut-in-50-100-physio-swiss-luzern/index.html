We are looking for from 01.02.2027 or by agreement a Dipl. Physiotherapist/50 – 100 % Sonnmatt physiotherapy takes care of the internal spa guests (post-acute), the residents of the residence and the nursing department as well as ambulatory patients. The therapy of our spa guests following a hospital stay is individually planned based on the physiotherapeutic findings. The support and therapy of patients and older people is based on their resources and on standardized post-treatment schemes for promoting mobility, alleviating pain, maintaining the state of health in the aging process and thus ensuring participation. You will perform physiotherapies, work with patients in MTT and provide your expertise in group activities. In single physiotherapy, you apply your knowledge of biomechanical analysis in order to be able to treat pathomechanical conditions in a targeted manner. Furthermore, you perform applications such as respiratory therapy or lymph drainage, depending on your knowledge and focus. Your profile

  • You have a diploma as a physiotherapist (HF/FH or SRK recognition)
  • You are interested in geriatric physiotherapy and its focus
  • Ideally, they have the additional training and professional experience in Concept Sohier and / or manual lymph drainage, MTT or have other adequate additional training or are ready to complete appropriate further training, in which we are happy to support them.
  • You have good oral and written knowledge of German and understand dialect

We offer

  • a great physio team, a good corporate culture and very good social benefits
  • regular internal training and opportunity for intervision/supervision
  • Support for external training
  • for applications from abroad support from SRK Partial decision
  • further advantages and a workplace in the most beautiful location in Lucerne

If you like to accompany people in their illness and recovery process and if you value a dignified treatment of people of all ages, then we look forward to your complete application documents.
