Full description

Every day, around 3,000 employees of RUAG and RUAG Real Estate make a significant contribution to the security of Switzerland. They ensure that the Swiss Army and other operational and security organisations can perform their tasks comprehensively at any time.

You can move that

  • Application and wiring of strain gauges (DMS) and other sensors
  • Preparation of components and production of application tools
  • Matching and calibration of DMS bridges
  • Calibration and maintenance of measurement/electronic systems
  • Manufacture and maintenance of trays
  • Provision of measurement and control systems for wind tunnel tests
  • Commissioning, functional testing and support of measuring chains and documenting of experimental measurement technology

You bring that with you

  • Completed training as a micromechanic, precision mechanic, watchmaker, automatic technician, electronics technician, mechatronics technician or equivalent
  • Several years of professional experience in the application of blank strain gauges (DMS) and DMS-based sensors
  • Pronounced precision mechanical skills and basic electrotechnical knowledge are advantageous
  • Very good German and good English skills in word and writing
  • Precise and quality-conscious work, partly under the microscope
  • Patient and enduring personality with high quality standards of accuracy and documentation

Don't you meet all the requirements one hundred percent? Apply anyway. We are always looking for talented and motivated people who want to enrich our team. We attach great importance to diversity and equal opportunities and our goal is to build a diverse team in which everyone can fully develop their personal strengths. We particularly encourage all individuals, regardless of gender, who may have concerns about accepting a job in an army-related environment to apply.
