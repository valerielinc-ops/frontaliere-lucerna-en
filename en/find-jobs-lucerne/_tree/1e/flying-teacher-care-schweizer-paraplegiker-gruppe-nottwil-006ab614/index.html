Full description

Become part of something bigger. Work with more than 2000 specialists from more than 100 professions and enable paraplegic people to live a self-determined life. On the modern campus in Nottwil, an inspiring atmosphere and interdisciplinary cooperation at eye level awaits you.

Your heart beats for education? Do you want to accompany people on their professional path, promote potential and at the same time participate in the patient bed? Then you are exactly right with us.

As a Flying Teacher Care , you are on the road across stations, shaped the educational quality and accompanied trainees in their development.

Tasks: • You perform educational sequences directly in everyday practice with our trainees (AGS, FaGe, care HF and FH) • You take on joint responsibility for the independent planning and evaluation of the punctual learning units • You promote the theory-practice transfer and support a learning-promoting training culture at the different stations. • You support trainees in problem and crisis situations in cooperation with those responsible for education • You recognize the need for education, contribute ideas, work in projects and are jointly responsible for an innovative and attractive further development of training in nursing SPZ • You work to a fixed part of your work as a care specialist exemplary in the direct care of people with paraplegia Requirements • Diploma as nursing specialist HF/FH • Care professional experience • Enjoyment of educational tasks • Communication strength, flexibility and organisational skills We offer: You not only expect attractive employment conditions, but also numerous other advantages. Look forward to meaningful work in a modern working environment, flexible working hours and a wide range of further education opportunities. Come and experience our unique spirit. More information on parplegie.ch/benefits. The Swiss Paraplegic Centre in Nottwil is a private, nationally and internationally recognized specialist clinic for cross-sectional, back and respiratory medicine. As an acute and rehabilitation clinic, the centre offers its patients comprehensive care and supports them throughout their lives. The clinic is part of the Swiss Paraplegic Group, which provides a globally unique service network for the holistic rehabilitation of people with paraplegia.
