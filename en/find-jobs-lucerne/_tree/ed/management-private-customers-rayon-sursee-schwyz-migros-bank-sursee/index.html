Full description

You can get involved with us, help shape the customer advice of the future and the digitalization of banking. We are looking for you: a personality that thinks ahead. Someone with whom we, as a team, shape the future with fresh ideas, innovations and passion. Come on.

Luogo di lavoro: Migros Bank, Sursee branch, Bahnhofstrasse 6b, 6210 Sursee

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned. An application letter or video is desired for this position.

Mansioni

  • In our offices in Sursee and Schwyz, you are responsible for the personnel management of the teams as well as the management of the two locations.
  • You ensure the sustainable implementation of our overall banking strategy in your area of responsibility.
  • You promote personal responsibility and the continuous development of your team by transferring responsibility, trust and active coaching
  • In important projects & initiatives you actively shape the future of our bank
  • Together with the team, you plan systematic sales measures that lead to success. Through targeted themed events, you will advance the location in the expansion of market positioning
  • Your conviction to put the customer in the center and to advise holistically shape your actions

Requirements

  • At least 3-5 years of leadership experience: Banking & Finance
  • Completed studies (uni/ETH, FH, HF): in the field of banking, finance, sales or leadership
  • For you, leadership means coaching your employees and actively supporting them in realizing their potential. In addition, you promote cross-divisional collaboration.
  • You bring the right personality to live up to your responsibility for an experienced team
  • Your leadership style ensures the balance between team culture, result responsibility and change implementation
  • You manage to keep a cool head even in a challenging environment and make the right decisions
  • With your sales experience, you strengthen the cross-location exchange as part of our regional management team
  • in German (negotiation confident)

Cosa of friamo

  • Coaching: Personal coach for executives
  • Flat hierarchies: Permeable hierarchies and cross-level exchange
  • Du culture: Experienced team spirit with transparent communication
  • Design and decision-making leeway: Optimal possibilities for contributing ideas and know-how
  • Advantages of cadre: One week holidays per year more for cadre members
  • Migros Bank: accounts, mortgages and financial services with preferential terms

Contatto

Application and contact Patrick Käch HR Business Partner / Junior Manager

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision on progress or withdrawal from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Personal video or telephone conversation: For a large number of applicants, we optionally conduct a personal telephone or video conversation. This gives us the opportunity to get to know more candidates within a short time in order to invite suitable people for the next round. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
  • Personality/performance test: Under certain conditions, we use a personality test (BIP, Scales test, etc.) as an additional step to support selection. The results of the test may be discussed personally and form the basis for further selection. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
  • Talks with HR & department: The interview is conducted virtually or on site with the responsible person from HR and/or the specialist department. It is primarily about learning more about the personnel and specialist skills and gives both sides the opportunity to assess the cultural fit into the company and team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Getting to know the team: Whether someone fits into the team is an important factor for employees and employers when deciding for or against employment. In a casual framework, we therefore give the opportunity for everyone to get to know each other.
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition: After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent by post. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks

Grado di occupazione: 70-100 %

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Lucerne?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Lucerne's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Lucerne's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
