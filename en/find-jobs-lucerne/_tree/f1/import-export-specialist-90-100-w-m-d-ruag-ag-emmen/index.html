Full description

Every day, around 3,000 employees of RUAG and RUAG Real Estate make a significant contribution to the security of Switzerland. They ensure that the Swiss Army and other operational and security organizations can perform their tasks comprehensively at any time.

You can move that

  • Implementation of customs clearance for import and export goods taking into account the applicable export control regulations (KMG & GKV)
  • Application of various customs procedures (ZAVV, Carnet ATA, etc.) as well as preparation of origin calculations
  • Issuance of clearance instructions and preparation of transport orders to freight forwarders
  • Organisation and management of international transport (road, air and sea)
  • Preparation and assurance of a compliant shipment including DGR
  • Classification and maintenance of goods under the correct tariff numbers
  • Specific support in the area of export control, e.g. for holiday representatives or increased work volume
  • Cooperation in customer projects as well as other projects and topics in the field of foreign trade

You bring that with you

  • Completed commercial training, ideally with further training in the areas of customs, foreign trade, compliance or forwarding
  • Several years of experience in import and export, preferably in a technology or industrial company
  • Good knowledge of customs processes, customs classification and Incoterms
  • Experience in dealing with customs and logistics service providers is an advantage
  • Sound IT skills (SAP, SAP GTS, MS Office); Experience with customs programs a plus
  • Negotiating knowledge of German and good knowledge of English
  • Customer-oriented, independent, structured and solution-oriented working method as well as teamwork
  • Readiness for selective travel between locations

In connection with this position, certain legal framework conditions have to be taken into account due to international export control regulations. Therefore, in the course of the application process, both in the online application form and in the context of personal interviews, additional information on nationality and export control status may be required.

Do you not meet all the requirements one hundred percent? Apply anyway. We are always looking for talented and motivated people who want to enrich our team. We attach great importance to diversity and equal opportunities and our goal is to build a diverse team in which everyone can fully develop their personal strengths. We particularly encourage all individuals, regardless of gender, who may have concerns about accepting a job in an army-related environment to apply.
