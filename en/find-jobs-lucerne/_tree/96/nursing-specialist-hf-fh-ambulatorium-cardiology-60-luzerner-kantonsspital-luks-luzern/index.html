Full description

The Lucerne Canton Hospital with its locations in Lucerne, Sursee and Wolhusen is a company of the LUKS Group and thus belongs to one of the leading hospital groups in Switzerland. The more than 8,500 employees are there with warmth, competence and commitment around the clock for the well-being of patients. The clinics and institutes of the LUKS Group offer medical services of the highest quality.

For our team we are looking for reinforcement immediately or by agreement. Tasks: • They take over the preparation of patients for consultation hours and examinations and carry out blood samples properly • They assist in various diagnostic examinations, including stress echocardiographies, transesophageal echocardiographies, ergometries, spiroergometrites and tilt table examinations • They perform various cardiological examinations such as writing ECG and applying 24-hour blood pressure equipment • In addition, you will take on a wide range of administrative and logistical tasks that contribute to the smooth running of practice and investigation operations. Requirements • You have completed training as a certified nurse HF/FH/DNII • Your expertise in cardiology and your knowledge in rhythmology complement your profile and enable you to provide sound and professional patient care. • You value work in a team, convince through resilience, flexibility and communication strength and practice your profession with passion and great commitment • They convince through a precise, responsible and goal-oriented working method and always treat the patient with appreciation and empathy. We offer: • Look forward to a versatile, exciting and dynamic area of responsibility that offers you new challenges and development opportunities every day • We offer you the opportunity to work independently in a committed team • Their working hours are clearly structured and take place on day duty from Monday to Friday, ensuring a good work-life balance • You benefit from a comprehensive range of internal and external training opportunities that open up promising future prospects for you.
