Full description

The Children's Hospital Central Switzerland of the Lucerne Cantonal Hospital (LUKS) is one of the largest children's hospitals in Switzerland and serves the entire region of Central Switzerland at the highest level. Our employees work around the clock to ensure that children, young people and their relatives are measurably better off. LUKS, with its locations in Lucerne, Sursee and Wolhusen, is a company of the LUKS Group and thus belongs to one of the leading hospital groups in Switzerland. >For our team we are looking for reinforcement as of 1 January 2027 or by agreement.

Tasks

  • In the Central Medication Room (ZMR) of the Children's Hospital They offer intravenous medications such as infusions, short infusions and permanent infusions as well as parenteral nutrition for our inpatients.
  • Prepare for our small patients in the ambulatorium oncology You carefully prepare the necessary medications and ensure reliable care of the nursing wards
  • Your area of responsibility also includes narcotics control, processing of returns and expiration date control in cooperation with pharmacy.
  • With a high quality and safety awareness, you ensure order and cleanliness in the ZMR, participate in the continuous quality improvement and train students and new employees in the direction of medicines

Requirements

  • You have completed training as a specialist Health EFZ acute, Medical / Practice assistant / in EFZ or Dipl. Care professional HF
  • A very careful, precise and structured working method as well as a high sense of responsibility, safety and quality characterize you
  • Even under time pressure, you work focused and reliable, set the right priorities and organize your tasks independently and forward-looking
  • Thanks to your good communication and team skills, you will succeed in interprofessional cooperation with nursing care, medical profession and pharmacy.
