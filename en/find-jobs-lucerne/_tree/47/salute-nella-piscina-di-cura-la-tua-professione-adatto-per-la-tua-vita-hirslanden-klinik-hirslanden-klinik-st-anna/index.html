Do you want to optimally combine work, family, study or leisure time with your work?

With us, you determine when and how you work. As part of our pool, you can flexibly design your assignments according to your possibilities and support where your skills are required. Whether early, late or selected night services: You choose a working model that fits your life.

You can expect varied assignments on different bed stations, exciting insights into different specialist areas as well as the opportunity to use and develop your expertise in a variety of ways.

YOUR TASKS

  • You accompany our trainees with commitment and joy

Your profile
