Full description

Location: Lucerne Contract type: Perpetual workload: Full-time help shape our reputation, build sustainable relationships and implement our corporate affairs agenda in Switzerland. In this role, you combine strategic consulting with operational implementation in the areas of communication, public affairs, crisis management and sustainability. You report directly to the DACH Corporate Affairs Director and work closely with the Swiss Country Manager. You will link the priorities of Swiss business with the overarching corporate affairs agenda at DACH and European level. Strategy & Business Consulting: Develop and implement the Swiss corporate affairs strategy in line with local business priorities and the DACH agenda. Advise the country manager and coordinate closely with colleagues at DACH and European level. Communication & Reputation: Strength and protect the reputation of HEINEKEN Switzerland through internal, external and change communication. Responsible for media work, assume the role of media spokesperson and steer the corporate identity as well as the brand PR. Public Affairs & Stakeholder Dialogue: Shape the Swiss public affairs agenda, build relationships with stakeholders from politics and business and represent the company externally, including in the Swiss Brewery Association. Secure the connection to the European public affairs agenda and use stakeholder surveys to target the dialogue. Crisis & Issues Management: Lead crisis communication in Switzerland, participate in the DACH crisis team and deal with reputation-relevant topics. Strengthen crisis preparedness through planning and training. Sustainability: Coordinate the Swiss sustainability agenda within the DACH framework, including reporting and partnerships with non-governmental and non-profit organisations. Leadership & Resources: Lead the communications professional and lead the visitor service activities. Control external agencies and service providers and are responsible for a budget of around CHF 450,000. Relevant expertise: A completed degree in communication, public affairs, political science, economics or a related field or equivalent practical experience, combined with comprehensive experience in corporate affairs, corporate communications or public affairs. Knowledge of the Swiss market: An in-depth understanding of the Swiss political, media and stakeholder landscape as well as the ability to build trusting relationships and represent corporate interests credibly. Communication competence: Strong skills in Texdesign, storytelling and as a media spokesperson as well as experience in translating complex topics into clear messages for employees, media and external stakeholders. Judgment under pressure: experience in issue and crisis communication, secure judgment on sensitive issues and the necessary sovereignty to advise leaders at the highest level. Strategic View and Operational Implementation Strength: The ability to translate business priorities into concrete plans, impact effectively in a matrix organization, and collaborate effectively with local, regional, and European teams. Sustainability knowledge: Experience in the coordination of sustainability initiatives, in reporting or in external partnerships – alternatively relevant transferable skills and a strong interest in building this expertise. Leadership and resource management: Experience in managing employees or cross-functional teams, managing agencies and budgets, and weighing competing priorities. Languages: Excellent knowledge of German and English

  • Knowledge of French is an advantage.
  • What we offer you Flexibility & Work-Life Balance: Flexible working hours and hybrid work. Learning & Development: Internal and external training opportunities, for example language courses and our Beer Academy. Team spirit & culture: Regular employee events, monthly company apos and joint celebrations. Discounts & Extras: Attractive discounts, for example in our beverage shop and selected leisure and cultural offers. Events & Experiences: Regular ticket raffles for festivals, concerts and other events. Health & well-being: health and support services for different life situations.
