Full description

Do you want to work agile, drive innovation and provide more safety and health in Switzerland with digital solutions? Then you are right with us. What you do

  • You are part of the Innovation department and take on the role of contact in an agile team: was for the stakeholders.
  • You ensure the optimization of claims management processes with the help of digital solutions. Your focus is on further optimizing the Syrius platform.
  • Together with our customers and specialists, you will analyze processes, identify action priorities and develop solutions.
  • Together with technical experts, you will examine the use of innovative technologies and design the implementation of ICT solutions.
  • You benefit from the possibility of flexibly dividing working time and location. What you bring
  • You have a degree in Business Informatics (FH, Uni) or a comparable education.
  • You have already gained experience in business analysis, ideally in an agile development environment (e.g. Scrum, SAFe).
  • In-depth knowledge of damage management UVG is an advantage in this task.
  • As a reliable and effective personality, you bring an independent and structured way of working.
  • In addition to your pronounced analytical-conceptual ability, you have impeccable knowledge of German. French is beneficial.
