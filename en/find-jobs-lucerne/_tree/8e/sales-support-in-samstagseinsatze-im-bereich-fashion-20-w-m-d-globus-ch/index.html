Women’s Fashion Sales Advisor (m/f/d)

At Globus, our employees are the key to our success, especially those who leave the final impression on our customers. You are the final, radiant smile of our brand and ensure that every customer leaves our store with a positive feeling and wants to come back. For our Luzern store, we are looking for exactly you—an engaged individual who, with passion and dedication, offers our customers a first-class experience.

THIS IS WHERE YOU MAKE THE DIFFERENCE

  • As a host, you make sure our customers feel welcome and well looked after as guests.
  • Thanks to your excellent service, your fascination with fashion and your individual advice, you ensure unique shopping experiences.
  • You support visual merchandising, inspiring our guests to purchase our exclusive brands.
  • With your positive presence and passion for trends, you make a decisive contribution to the company’s success.

THIS IS WHAT SETS YOU APART

  • You are service-oriented and have a strong passion for fashion.
  • You love and embody the role of a host and have a strong customer focus.
  • You have impeccable manners and a well-groomed appearance.
  • You are very reliable and take initiative.
  • Working Saturdays is a given for you.

BENEFITS FOR YOU

Together with around 1,700 other detail-oriented people, you are an important part of our fascinating premium and luxury world. You experience genuine appreciation, work in a great team, and we give you the trust you need to make use of your freedom and make a difference with your skills. If you know your craft and bring a great deal of passion, many career doors are open to you. We are happy to support you with internal and external education and further training.

You can also look forward to

  • an exceptional working environment with flat hierarchies in the heart of Luzern.
  • performance-based bonuses and attractive social benefits.
  • up to 50% off products from the world of Globus for you and your family.
  • much more...
