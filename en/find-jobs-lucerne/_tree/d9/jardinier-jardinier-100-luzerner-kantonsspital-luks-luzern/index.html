Full description

The Lucerne Canton Hospital with its locations in Lucerne, Sursee and Wolhusen is a company of the LUKS Group and thus belongs to one of the leading hospital groups in Switzerland. The more than 8,500 employees are there with warmth, competence and commitment around the clock for the well-being of patients. The clinics and institutes of the LUKS Group offer medical services of the highest quality.

For our team we are looking for reinforcement immediately or by agreement. Tasks: • Together with the team, you will take care of the maintenance, maintenance and further development of the interior greenery as well as the 15-hectare green and outdoor facilities on the hospital area. • They take over the maintenance and care of our plants indoors, carry out plantings and contribute to an attractive and well-kept environment • Minor repair, service and maintenance work as well as the safe handling of machines and equipment are just as much a part of your versatile working day as the supportive waste disposal in the event of bottlenecks • They accompany and support our learners in their training and lead and coordinate assistants in their activities. They provide for the winter piquetting service and support the team in snow clearance and ice smoothing operations Requirements • You have completed vocational training as a gardener/EFZ and have in-depth knowledge of green area care and/or plant production • Ideally, you have practical experience in indoor greening and enjoy developing in this specialty area. • The safe handling of small machines is part of your everyday work. Bring the category B driving licence, the category BE is an advantage • Team spirit, initiative and a reliable and responsible way of working distinguish you. You are also ready to provide winter piquetting service We offer: • Look forward to a varied activity with a lot of personal responsibility and scope for design in a versatile working environment • Bring your expertise to a future-oriented and process-oriented team and continuously develop together with us • Benefit from a collegial working environment where quality, cooperation and mutual support are lived. • Actively shape the environment and interior greening of the largest employer in Central Switzerland and make a visible contribution to a well-maintained hospital area every day
