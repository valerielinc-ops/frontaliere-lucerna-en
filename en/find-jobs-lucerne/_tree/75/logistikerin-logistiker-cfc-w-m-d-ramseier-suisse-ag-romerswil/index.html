These tasks will inspire you

  • Picking and processing customer orders
  • Posting goods receipts and goods issues in SAP
  • Independent loading and unloading of trucks using a counterbalance forklift
  • Efficient operation and administration of the warehouse (raw materials, auxiliary materials and finished products) in block-stacked and high-bay warehouses
  • Implementation and assurance of quality and occupational safety standards
  • Maintenance and updating of process and QM documentation in SharePoint in collaboration with the Head of Freight Forwarding

This is the profile we are looking for

  • Completed training as a logistics specialist EFZ
  • Several years of professional experience in warehousing and freight forwarding
  • SUVA-recognized forklift license for counterbalance forklifts
  • Very good knowledge of SAP and MS Office
  • Independent, structured and efficient way of working
  • Technical understanding and proficient handling of counterbalance forklifts (e.g. Linde E20/E30)
  • Strong command of German, both spoken and written (min. B2)
  • Enjoyment of contact with chauffeurs and internal contacts
  • Sense of responsibility, team spirit and willingness to take on deputy duties
  • Flexibility and willingness to help with additional tasks that arise
