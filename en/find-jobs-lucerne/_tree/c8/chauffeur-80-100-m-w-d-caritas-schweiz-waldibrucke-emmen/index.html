Full description

Doing the right thing. In Switzerland and in around 20 countries on four continents. Engage with us for the vision of a solidarity society, as Every year, the dress centre collects and sorts around 800 tons of used clothes and shoes for meaningful and targeted reuse. An important part of the collected clothes goes on sale in Switzerland. The operation includes the sorting plant, the warehouses, various sales channels and administration. Their tasks

  • Collection of clothes/footwear from clothing containers
  • First triage of the collected goods
  • Storage support
  • Support for forwarding

Our requirements

  • Completed apprenticeship or equivalent education
  • Cat C driving licence of advantage, Cat B mandatory
  • Professional experience as a chauffeur
  • Good knowledge of German (B1)
  • Teamplayer

The place of work is Waldibrücke, Emmen. Start of work: immediately or by agreement Caritas offers attractive employment conditions and an open working environment at the pulse of social issues. For the transformation towards role-based work, we count on employees with a digital and agile mindset who like to participate in change processes and take responsibility. Information on this point can be provided by Mr. Beat Heinz, Head of Clothing Centre, bheinz@caritas.ch. We thank you for your interest and look forward to your application via the online portal.
