Full description

Your responsibilities

  • They further develop the product vision and the strategic roadmap and translate market, customer and company requirements into clear product priorities.
  • They create and evaluate business cases, help shape the pricing strategy and create sound decision-making bases for investments as well as new products and services.
  • They represent the needs of global customers and markets and work closely with internal interfaces in Dierikon and Tianjin (China).
  • You control the product backlog at a higher level, prioritize features and initiatives according to business value and ensure effective coordination within the agile project organization (Agile Release Train / ART).
  • They are actively involved in quarterly planning as well as in agile coordination and improvement formats to promote innovation and represent the interests of ART TW vis-à-vis management and portfolio.
  • You develop the marketing mix of the product group TW together with the product management.

Your profile

  • They have a technical or business education or training at FH, university or comparable qualification.
  • You have several years of experience in product management or in a comparable strategic product responsibility in the industrial environment.
  • You have experience with P&L, budgets or business cases and combine a strong market and customer understanding with entrepreneurial thinking.
  • They work strategically and structured, make clear decisions and set effective priorities even in the event of incomplete information.
  • They communicate convincingly with different stakeholders and ideally bring experience in agile organizations, especially with SAFe.
  • They have a good technical understanding and a very good knowledge of German and English
  • Knowledge of cable processing is advantageous.

Your Benefits

You take on a role with real creative leeway and direct influence on business success. You can expect a well-established team with product management, architecture, RTE and teams to relieve the operational burden. You can also look forward to a structured onboarding with handover. Your new place of work in Dierikon is equipped with modern technology and contemporary work equipment. They benefit from flexible working time models and home office opportunities as well as from a wide range of internal and external training opportunities. This includes, for example, free internal English courses and access to selected learning platforms. Modern meeting and break zones, a large terrace and discounted food options in a neighbouring partner restaurant complement the offer.
