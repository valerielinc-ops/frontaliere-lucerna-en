Full description

Head of Transport (a) We are an initiative, modern and future-oriented transport and logistics company with headquarters in Schachen/LU. Thanks to our approximately 340 motivated employees and a high quality of service, we are on course for success! Since the long-term job owner will retire next summer, we are looking for a passionate head of transport (a) to succeed him. In this versatile and demanding management function, you assume overall responsibility for the transport sector. Together with your management team, you ensure an efficient, economical and customer-oriented handling of the transport processes and continuously develop the processes. Task area

  • Overall responsibility for operations in the Transport, Disposition and Platform Night departments
  • Management and assurance of administration, controlling and billing
  • Leadership of three team leaders and around 130 employees
  • Optimal management of resources such as personnel, vehicles and equipment
  • Management, supervision and implementation of projects
  • Recruitment, leadership, coaching, training and promotion of employees
  • Co-development and implementation of corporate strategy in the field of transport
  • Planning and project management when integrating new customers
  • Enforce and ensure compliance with various standards (ISO, FSSC, etc.), guidelines and instructions

Requirement profile

  • Completed commercial, logistical or technical basic training, ideally in the transport industry
  • Further training in business administration and/or management
  • Swiss Federal Diploma as Manager of Transport and Logistics
  • Several years of management experience in a comparable function
  • Sound industry knowledge in road transport
  • Technical understanding of commercial vehicles and their use
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Very good IT user knowledge (MS Office)

You have strong interpersonal and communicative skills, you are an empathetic maker with a structured and solution-oriented way of working as well as a high level of service and team orientation. In addition, you have strong leadership skills combined with assertiveness and high social skills. Offer This job offers you a versatile and challenging job in an exciting environment. You get a lot of design freedom and have a direct influence on the performance, processes and further development of the transport area. We trust in your expertise and give you the necessary freedom to plan and lead projects independently. Short decision paths and flat hierarchies allow you to effectively fill your role, contribute ideas and further develop processes. Have we aroused your interest in taking on this responsible key position in a modern work environment? For this position, we only consider direct applications. We look forward to your application! Christian Kempter-Imbach, Managing Director, telephone: +41 41 499 70 10.
