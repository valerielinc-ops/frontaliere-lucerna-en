Full description

80-100%

You can design it.

  • You take on the professional responsibility for the end-to-end supplier processes in energy data management (EDM) at CKW and are the central contact person for internal and external participants.
  • You ensure the BGM preparatory work and a high quality of measured data – from SDAT-compliant aggregate formation to status and completeness checks to plausibility checks and coordination with the distribution system operators (DNBs).
  • You clear missing or implausible measured values, coordinate corrections and subsequent deliveries and monitor the timely sending of the SDAT data.
  • You coordinate the data transfer as well as necessary follow-up work and queries in the billing process.
  • You will continuously develop processes, controls and interfaces and promote reliable cooperation within CKW and with our partners.
  • You bring the EDM specialist perspective to various strategic projects and significantly shape the system environment for the EDM supplier.

That makes you

  • You have at least five years of professional experience in the energy industry, in energy or raw materials trading, metrology, energy data management or a comparable environment and know the practice in EDM, market communication or supplier processes.
  • You have first practical experience with SAP; Knowledge of Robotron is a plus.
  • You will bring experience in the processing and quality assurance of large amounts of data (ideal energy or measurement data) and coordinate deviations in a solution-oriented manner across divisional and company boundaries.
  • You understand energy market processes and data flows. Knowledge of the Swiss supplier processes, MC-CH, SDAT and BGM are welcome – they can also be built up with us.
  • You like to work with large amounts of data and bring knowledge in Python and SQL to detect abnormalities and derive improvements. An interest or previous experience in data science round off your profile.
  • You collect professional requirements structured, work carefully and independently and communicate clearly with all parties involved. Even if you do not meet all requirements, we look forward to your application.
