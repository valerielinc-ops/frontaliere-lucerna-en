Senior Finance Business Partner 100 (f/m/d) — RUAG AG

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Approximately 3,000 employees of RUAG and RUAG Real Estate make a significant contribution to the security of Switzerland every day. They ensure that the Swiss Armed Forces and other operational and security organizations can fully perform their duties at all times.

What you can achieve

  • You will become part of a dynamic finance team operating in the growing business area SCG (Sensors, Communication & Ground)
  • In the role of Finance Business Partner, you are jointly responsible for the financial steering and transparency of various business segments, each of which is responsible for its own P&L. In this capacity, you act as a sparring partner to the Business Managers
  • You support the further development of Business Area Controlling and are also involved in conceptual work
  • You work on high-quality business cases and ensure Financial Governance during the bidding phase
  • Together with the project managers, you ensure transparent and targeted cost center controlling
  • Together with the Business Managers, you analyze and comment on results, and prepare the forecast and financial planning
  • You ensure high-quality financial closing within your area of responsibility

What you bring

  • Higher business education (University of Applied Sciences/University) and/or a specialized further education, such as Certified Financial and Accounting Specialist with federal professional certificate
  • Several years of experience in controlling as well as in a complex and diversified corporate and customer environment
  • Very good knowledge of SAP FI/CO (other modules are an advantage) and MS-Office (especially Excel)
  • Sense of responsibility and economic way of thinking and acting
  • Communicative, resilient, and motivated personality with a high degree of self-responsibility, as well as very good analytical, conceptual, and communication skills
  • Very good German and English skills in writing and speaking (Level B2)
  • Travel to other RUAG locations in Switzerland

Do you not meet all the requirements one hundred percent? Apply anyway. We are always looking for talented and motivated individuals who want to enrich our team. We place great value on diversity and equal opportunity, and our goal is to build a diverse team where everyone can fully develop their personal strengths. In particular, we encourage all individuals, regardless of their gender, who may have concerns about accepting a position in an environment close to the military, to apply.

Work location

RUAG AG, Allmendstrasse 86, 3602 Thun

About the area

For our Thun location (Business Area Sensors, Communication & Ground), we are looking for a Finance Business Partner who ensures the financial governance of the team and provides transparent reporting. You have the opportunity to develop within the company, contribute your own ideas, and implement them independently. With your professional experience, you make a significant contribution to the security of Switzerland. Become part of our committed team and actively shape the future of the SCG location.

Your benefits

Your well-being is important to us. Therefore, you have many benefits with us. These include, for example:

  • Salary and fringe benefits: We offer you a performance-oriented and market-appropriate salary, 13 monthly salaries, and generous premiums and allowances. Together with other fringe benefits, this results in an attractive total package.
  • Flexible working: We place great value on our employees finding a healthy balance between work and private life. Therefore, we offer, among other things, flexible working hours and home office options.
  • Discounts: At RUAG, you benefit from discounts in the areas of mobility and catering. In addition, you benefit from attractive offers on our benefit portal and have fleet discounts with various renowned car brands.
  • Career and further education opportunities: It is important to us to promote and develop the potential of our employees. Through training, courses, and further education, we support them in continuously expanding their skills to achieve their professional goals.
  • Leisure activities: Our employees are important to us. Therefore, we give them a day off on their birthday, offer a diverse sports program, and the possibility to take additional days off with salary deduction in addition to the annual vacation entitlement.
