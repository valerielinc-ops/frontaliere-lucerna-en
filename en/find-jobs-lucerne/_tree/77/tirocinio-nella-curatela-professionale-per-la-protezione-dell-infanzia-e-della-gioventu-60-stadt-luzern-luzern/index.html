Full description

We focus on proximity and openness: At the city administration we live the du-culture – already in the application process. Are you interested in child and youth protection? With us, you have the opportunity to gain an exciting insight into this versatile professional field during an internship and to actively participate.

Your tasks

  • You will get an insight into the management of child and youth protection assistance and will be accompanied by experienced support staff.
  • You advise, support and empower affected persons and initiate change processes.
  • You prepare accountability reports, opinions and requests to the child and adult protection authority.

Your profile

  • You are studying Social Work at HSLU.
  • Working in child and youth protection interests you.
  • You are an empathetic and well-organized personality with pleasure in advising and accompanying children and young adults.
  • You are sociable and ready to face complex topics and difficult life stories.
