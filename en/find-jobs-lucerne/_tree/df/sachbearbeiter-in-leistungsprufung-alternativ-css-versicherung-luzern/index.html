Full description

We at CSS are a health partner for around 1.7 million insured people and maybe you will soon be one of them? Because with us you can make a difference! With your medical expertise, you’ll help us keep performance costs low. Are you very interested in paramedicine and are you also fascinated by complementary medicine? We have some strong offers for you: great working hours, home office and excellent development opportunities. And a large team of medical professionals - there is always something going on with us. When do we meet?

This is what your everyday life looks like

  • You have your service billing under control – because our customers are happy about correctly and quickly billed service assumptions.
  • You juggle skillfully between service providers, insured persons and internal stakeholders, clarify open questions and are available for written or oral questions.
  • Your skilful preparation of inquiries to the trusted doctor or other specialists ensures a smooth process.
  • Here you shine with your medical knowledge: You exchange ideas with your colleagues in order to handle your complex cases in the best possible way.

You bring that with you

  • Your basic medical or commercial education with suitable further education forms a solid basis – there are open.
  • Do you also have experience in the performance test of a health insurance? That would definitely be a plus!
  • You express yourself very well in German (verbal and written) and are sure to negotiate in conversation with our customers. Maybe you even know French, English or Italian?
  • With the notebook you know and have it under control.
  • Structured work, endurance and a motivated nature make you stand out.
  • Work Flexible: Adapt your work to your lifestyle annually with part-time options and other flexible models like home office and more.
  • Enjoy your wallet: Benefits such as bonus, Reka, discounted health insurance premium and others that quickly make up a few thousands a year.
  • Choose your holiday model: 5 or 6 weeks holiday? Your decision. Accordingly, you work 41 or 42 hours a week.
  • Improve yourself further: We like to see it if you educate yourself internally or externally and support generously with time and money.
  • Stay fit: Ergonomic workplaces, promotion of mental well-being and many other offers around your health.
  • Engage Socially: Does Life Mean Good to You? Then let others share your happiness and expand your horizons alone or in a team.
