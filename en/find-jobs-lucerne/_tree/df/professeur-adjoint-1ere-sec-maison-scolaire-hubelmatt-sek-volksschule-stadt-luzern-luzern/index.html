We focus on proximity and openness: At the city administration we live the du-culture – already in the application process.

You are an initiative person who

  • has completed training as a secondary teacher or is currently in training at a higher education institution.
  • technically sound, motivating and differentiated teaching.
  • is safe in dealing with heterogeneous learning groups and uses suitable methods as well as didactic approaches.
  • Learning processes are structured, accompanied reliably and learning progress made transparent.
  • cooperates constructively with the College and contributes to a high quality of teaching.
  • convinced by reliability, commitment and a professional attitude.
  • Young people meet openly, appreciatively and at eye level.

We offer

  • An established and dynamic team.
  • a constructive and solution-oriented working atmosphere.
  • Support from colleagues and school management.
  • targeted teaching and school development.
  • contemporary employment conditions according to cantonal specifications.

Hubelmatt secondary school – your new place of work Our school is located above the Lucerne Allmend, surrounded by forest and sports facilities. Thanks to public transport and cycling routes, you are quickly with us. We are a committed, dynamic team that values collaboration and exchange. Self-organized learning is a central part of our inclusive secondary school model. The focus is on the individual support and promotion of our learners – respectful, appreciative and at eye level. Participation is important to us. Our students are actively involved. As a “school with a special profile”, we live co-determination and develop together as a learning organization. In addition, we are a partner school of the PH Lucerne and actively participate in the training of teachers. We are looking for a dedicated class teacher for teaching on the 1st secondary class as part of a leave representation. You teach the subjects Mathematics, Media and Computer Science, Space Times Societies (RZG), Life Science and SOL (Self-organized learning) and accompany the students in their professional, personal and social development. Together with a supportive team, you will design modern, varied and life-like teaching as well as a successful start to secondary school.
