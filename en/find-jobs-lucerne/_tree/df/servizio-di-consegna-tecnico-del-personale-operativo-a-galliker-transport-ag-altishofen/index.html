Full description

Operational staff technical delivery service (a) Galliker Service AG prepares the new cars from various car manufacturers for the Swiss market. In addition, it performs order-specific series, conversion and various workshop work on behalf of importers, corporate customers, rental car and leasing companies as well as dealers. For the car brands in question, Galliker Service AG is also responsible for delivering and taking back the rental cars and leasing vehicles and carries out all kinds of workshop, car bodywork and painting work in the service of the customers. Task area

  • Performing delivery service to customer vehicles
  • Cooperation in the fitting of commercial vehicles
  • Miscellaneous simple workshop work
  • Operating the washing system, indoor washing of semi-trailers, vehicles descriptive
  • Vehicle displacement of customer vehicles on the site

Requirement profile

  • Completed apprenticeship (EBA,EFZ) in a technical profession and craftsmanship
  • Independent, reliable, capable of teamwork, dutiful and flexible
  • Good knowledge of German
  • Driving licence Cat. B

Offer

  • Varied tasks
  • Personal development opportunities in a versatile company
  • More hours can be compensated
  • At least 5 weeks of vacation
