Full description

Do you want to use your medical expertise and at the same time build up new competencies in the inpatient performance test? In our team, you will get the opportunity to familiarize yourself step by step with medical coding - even without completed coding training. You can build up a new professional perspective in a practical manner and make an important contribution to a correct and fair performance test. Sounds exciting? Then we look forward to meeting you!

This is what your everyday life looks like

  • You will gradually familiarize yourself with the medical coding according to ICD and CHOP as well as the relevant tariff and regulatory principles. You test selected inpatient performance cases and use your medical expertise in a targeted manner.
  • Using modern systems, you will analyze medical documentation, identify discrepancies and clarify open questions.
  • You formulate comprehensible and concise statements on erroneous codings.
  • You support experienced encoders in the assessment of more complex cases and continuously expand your skills.
  • With increasing experience, you independently take on more challenging tasks in the medical coding exam.
  • You start with a structured introduction to medical coding. With appropriate development and mutual interest, we support you on your way to the Medical Coder.

You bring that with you

  • First things first: You have completed basic medical or nursing training, at least at the level of nursing specialist HF or in a comparable training.
  • You are required to bring professional experience from the acute hospital. Your experience with insurance or performance testing is a plus.
  • Further training in medical coding is not required. Crucial is your broad experience in inpatient acute medicine, your interest in coding, your willingness to learn and your motivation to acquire this field step by step.
  • You can understand, classify and accurately document medical issues. You question existing processes in the exam, bring your own ideas and enjoy working in a team.
  • Careful, independent and structured work is a matter of course for you. Even with recurring tasks, you keep an overview and pay attention to a high quality.
  • You have good knowledge of German in words and writing. Knowledge of French or Italian is an advantage.
  • Work Flexible: Adapt your work to your lifestyle annually with part-time options and other flexible models like home office and more.
  • Choose your holiday model: 5 or 6 weeks holiday? Your decision. Accordingly, you work 41 or 42 hours a week.
  • Stay fit: Ergonomic workplaces, promotion of mental well-being and many other offers around your health.
  • Engage Socially: Does Life Mean Good to You? Then let others share your happiness and expand your horizons alone or in a team.
  • Enjoy your family life: Make time for your offspring: 3 weeks for fathers, for mothers up to 20 weeks. There are also generous child and education allowances.
  • Celebrate with us: Focused work and celebrate. We love this combination and maintain it regularly.
