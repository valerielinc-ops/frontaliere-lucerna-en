Homepage Head of Internal Computer Science Rothenburg, Switzerland Do you want to ensure that our internal IT systems function reliably and that more than 200 employees can work optimally? You would like to take on responsibility and actively develop our future IT landscape? Then apply now! Your everyday life Responsible in this key position Our internal IT infrastructure, information security and IT services. You lead the internal IT team and ensure a secure, modern and efficient work environment. You combine management responsibility with technical proximity to daily business. You set priorities, develop structures further and also take on operational tasks yourself. Your multifaceted tasks:

  • Professional and personnel management of the internal IT team
  • Implementation of IT strategy, roadmap, standards and governance issues
  • Responsibility for secure and stable IT operations including information security, backup, disaster recovery and business continuity
  • Further development of our cloud, workplace and identity architecture
  • Optimization and automation of IT service, support and operating processes
  • Processing, coordination and escalation of incidents of the technical campus infrastructure
  • Management of IT projects, technological innovations and new platforms
  • Responsibility for budget, licenses, contracts and external service providers
  • Close cooperation with software development, Opacc Cloud Center, management and departments
  • Active participation in day-to-day operations
  • Assuming responsibility as a vocational trainer for computer scientists EFZ, field of platform development

Your background You have several years of experience in IT infrastructure, IT operations or IT management and already have technical and human leadership experience. Ideally, You have the following knowledge and experience with:

  • Deep experience with modern cloud, workplace and identity environments
  • Good knowledge of information security, network and infrastructure
  • Experience with endpoint management, client lifecycle and IT service and support processes
  • Experience with backup, disaster recovery and business continuity
  • Experience in managing external service providers as well as in budget, license and contract management
  • Good understanding of modern software development environments
  • Knowledge of Microsoft 365, Entra ID, Virtualization, Linux, Containers and Infrastructure as Code

What sets you apart You think not only in technology, but in services, risks and business benefits. You explain complex technical topics in an understandable way, set clear priorities and keep an overview even in critical situations. You work in a structured, pragmatic and solution-oriented way and understand internal computer science as a partner for employees and software development. Our Benefits

And of course we offer you

  • flexible working hours, annual working hours and sabbaticals.
  • A top modern workplace.
  • valuable training offers and equivalent models for professional and management careers.

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Lucerne's migration office after the contract is signed.
