This is what awaits you

  • You develop production-ready AI applications and integrate them into our existing IT landscape.
  • Keep an eye on architectural best practices and ensure our AI applications are scalable, secure and maintainable.

You bring that with you

  • You have several years of experience in software development and good Python knowledge.
  • You are able to develop and containerize production-ready applications. Common technologies and best practices such as FastAPI, Docker, Kubernetes, Infrastructure as Code and CI/CD are familiar to you.
  • You bring experience in developing LLM-based applications. Experience with classic machine learning models is a plus.
  • You are familiar with common cloud platforms. Experience with Azure and Azure AI Services is a big advantage.
  • Ideally, you will already be responsible for AI solutions from conception to implementation and will have experience in the architecture and integration of AI systems.
  • You enjoy working with stakeholders in IT and industry. You can also communicate your ideas clearly in German.

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Lucerne's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Lucerne's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
