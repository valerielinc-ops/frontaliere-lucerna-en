Full description

Pensum: 80-100 %

You can design it.

  • You accompany learners together with the vocational trainees from recruitment to successful apprenticeship.
  • Together with vocational and practical trainees, you ensure practical training in accordance with legal requirements.
  • You advise and empower professional and practical trainees in their role and supervise offices throughout Switzerland. For this, you are on the road about 40% of your working time.
  • You support learners with challenges and develop sustainable solutions together with them.
  • You organize vocational training events and training courses and implement measures in apprenticeship marketing.

That makes you

  • You have completed a technical or craft vocational training, ideally in the electrical sector and have a further education at the level of HF or a vocational examination.
  • You have experience in training learners and have completed at least one vocational trainer course.
  • You use digital tools like Microsoft 365, Teams, SharePoint and learning platforms safely. You use AI tools such as our internal AxpoGPT to make your work efficient and future-oriented.
  • You have very good knowledge of German
  • Knowledge of English is an advantage.

Even if you do not fully meet all requirements, we look forward to your application. Diverse experiences and perspectives strengthen our team.
