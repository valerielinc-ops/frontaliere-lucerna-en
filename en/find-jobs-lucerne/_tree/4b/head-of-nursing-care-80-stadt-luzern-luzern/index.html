Full description

We focus on proximity and openness: At the city administration we live the du-culture – already in the application process. Actively shape the care of the city of Lucerne!

Your tasks

  • You carry the overall responsibility for the area of care and manage it professionally, personnel and organizationally. This includes in particular the financing of care and the supervision and approval of Spitex organizations.
  • Together with your team, you determine the annual care rates of the institutions of long-term care based in the city of Lucerne as well as for other municipalities on their behalf and lead the collective bargaining discussions.
  • You will continuously develop the processes and instruments of care. This includes in particular approval procedures, performance and collective agreements as well as cost controlling.
  • You ensure the correct handling and analysis of the assumption of care residual costs. This includes the examination of municipal jurisdiction and the tariff as well as statistical evaluations.
  • You ensure compliance with the legal requirements for the supervision and approval of Spitex organizations.
  • You work in projects and represent the area to external stakeholders.

Your profile

  • You have a bachelor’s degree in health or social care or an equivalent education. Further training at MAS level is advantageous.
  • You bring practical leadership experience, have a good feeling for the development of employees and are ready to further develop your leadership skills.
  • You have several years of experience in the healthcare sector, know its structures and are well connected.
  • You have in-depth business knowledge, are above average in MS Excel and can quickly assess complex relationships.
  • You are trustworthy, resilient and confident in negotiation and act solution-oriented even in demanding situations.
  • You communicate stylishly in German and convey complex issues in an understandable and addressable manner.
