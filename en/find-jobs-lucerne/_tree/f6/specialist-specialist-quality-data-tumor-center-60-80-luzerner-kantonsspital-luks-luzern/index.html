Full description

For the office of the Tumor Center at the Lucerne Canton Hospital we are looking for reinforcements. You take over the quality management in the areas assigned to you, work with treatment data and organize interdisciplinary case meetings.

For our team we are looking for reinforcement as of 1 February 2027 or by agreement. Tasks: • You are responsible for the organizational and administrative coordination as well as the quality management of the areas assigned to you and act as the central contact person for the specialist areas involved. • On the basis of patient records, you document diagnoses and treatment courses, examine quality indicators and clarify inconsistencies together with the competent specialists • They organize the interdisciplinary tumor boards, take over their preparation and follow-up and are responsible for recording the participations and the performance billing • The coordination and preparation of certification audits, the compilation of relevant evidence and the monitoring of the agreed measures round off your versatile area of responsibility. Requirements • They have completed vocational training or studies in the medical, nursing, commercial or administrative field. We are also open to lateral entrants with an equivalent degree. • Ideally, you will have knowledge of oncological terminology or experience in medical documentation or quality management. • Your analytical, structured and autonomous way of working allows you to merge information from different sources, identify discrepancies, prioritize relevant information and document results in a comprehensible way. • You convince with your organizational talent, your address-oriented communication and your well-founded Microsoft 365 user knowledge. Experience with a clinic information system, ideally Epic, is an advantage We offer: • A step-by-step familiarization with the processes and systems of the tumor center enables you to increasingly take responsibility for your areas • She expects a versatile area of responsibility at the interface between quality management, medical documentation and interdisciplinary cooperation. • Together with specialists from the medical profession, nursing and administration, you have the opportunity to actively shape and further develop existing processes. • If you are interested and receive appropriate training, we offer you the opportunity to deepen your competences in quality management and independently carry out internal audits.
