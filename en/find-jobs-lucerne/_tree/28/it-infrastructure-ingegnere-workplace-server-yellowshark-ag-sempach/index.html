01 Future employer

A systems integrator in Central Switzerland, customers from the regional economy, and a wide variety of environments: This is exactly where you learn more in a short time than at any single company. We are looking for a System Engineer with solid training and initial practical experience who is ready to take on their own projects and customers soon.

Why making the move is worthwhile

  • Varied projects instead of working in the same environment all the time
  • A team that shares knowledge, and time for further training
  • A comprehensive package with fair terms

This listing is intended for professionals who work or have worked at an IT service provider, systems integrator, or MSP.

02 Your role

  • You set up, optimize, and maintain server, client, and cloud environments for customers.
  • You deliver projects from planning through handover and serve as the technical point of contact on site or remotely.
  • You support customer environments in day-to-day operations, resolve 2nd- and 3rd-level incidents, and ensure that customers’ employees can work without disruption.
  • You work on virtualization, data backup, and networking topics, and build your expertise in these areas.
  • You support colleagues when projects or incidents require it, and share your know-how with the team.
  • After a short onboarding period, you take on tasks independently and plan your work largely on your own.

03 Benefits

  • Contributions toward external training
  • Bistro or cafeteria
  • Independent organization of day-to-day work
  • Flat hierarchies
  • Flexible annual and trust-based working hours
  • Progressive employment conditions
  • Time off for volunteer work
  • Guaranteed hybrid or home-based work
  • Career and advancement opportunities
  • At least 5 weeks of vacation
  • Modern workplace
  • Regular team-building activities and company events
  • Structured onboarding with support
  • Responsibility

04 Your profile

  • You have completed basic IT training (e.g., IT specialist EFZ, systems engineering) and have initial professional experience at an IT service provider.
  • You have a good knowledge of Windows Server and clients, Active Directory, and messaging solutions, and experience with virtualization and backup solutions.
  • You have solid networking fundamentals (switching, routing, WLAN). Experience with network access control is a plus, as is an aptitude for IT security.
  • You have experience in 2nd- and 3rd-level support.
  • You enjoy communicating with customers, stay calm in hectic situations, and find practical solutions.

05 Good to know

Application: Please apply only if you currently work or have worked at an IT service provider, and include a complete application dossier (CV, employment references, diplomas, certificates).

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Lucerne?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Lucerne's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Lucerne's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
