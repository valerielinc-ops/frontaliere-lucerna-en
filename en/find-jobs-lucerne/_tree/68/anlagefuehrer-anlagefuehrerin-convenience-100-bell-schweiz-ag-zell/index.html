Full description

Convenience is responsible for the timely and impeccable quality production and packaging of our products ready for enjoyment. The aim is to ensure smooth operation on the production lines. The main tasks include the operation and monitoring of the plants as well as the control of quality, hygiene and production processes. You like to work in a team, are reliable and keep an overview even in hectic situations? Then you are the right person to join our convenience team.

  • Contract type: Perpetual
  • Pensum: 100%
  • Working time model: 2-shift operation
  • Job: from now on
  • Language: German
  • Location: Bell Schweiz AG, Zelgmatte 1 6144 Zell

Tasks

  • Operation and monitoring of production and packaging facilities
  • Control of product quality, packaging, labelling and dating
  • Ensuring hygiene, safety and compliance with CCP requirements
  • Supporting the team on the production lines and introducing ideas for improvement
  • Correct completion of the proof documents and cooperation in 2-shift operation

Requirements

  • Technical flair and pleasure in dealing with modern production facilities and food
  • Quality awareness, reliability and a precise working method
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
  • PC skills, ideally MS Office
  • Teamwork and independent working methods
  • Readiness for 2-shift operation and occasional weekend work
  • Mobility for commuting
