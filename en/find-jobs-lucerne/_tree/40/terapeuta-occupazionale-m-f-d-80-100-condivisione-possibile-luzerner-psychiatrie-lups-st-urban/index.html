Full description

Lucerne Psychiatry AG is a teaching hospital of the University of Lucerne and is represented with its offers at various locations in the Canton of Lucerne and in Sarnen, Canton of Obwalden. More than 1,600 employees from a wide range of professional groups and departments are humanely and competently committed to the well-being of patients. The offers are aimed at children, adolescents and adults up to old age. Occupational therapist (m/f/d) 80 - 100% (Job-sharing possible)

Their tasks

  • Ergotherapeutic treatment of patients at the two specialist stations with a wide range of disorders
  • Close cooperation in an interdisciplinary team
  • Independent planning and implementation of client-centered individual therapies in individual setting as well as management of artisanal design groups
  • Promotion of everyday skills, independence, participation and personal resources of hospitalized persons
  • Participation in the further development of the specialist therapeutic offer on the station

Your profile

  • You have a recognised diploma as an occupational therapist or equivalent training
  • If possible, experience with psychiatric patients
  • Independent, structured and reliable working method
  • Resilience and flexibility in the dynamic psychiatric work environment
  • They have high social skills, a positive basic attitude and maintain an empathetic approach

Benefits

  • Varied and versatile work in an exciting psychiatric area
  • Opportunity for Open Dialog further education
  • Opportunity for ACT training
  • Collegial and committed interdisciplinary team
  • Mind. 5 weeks holidays, loyalty day and possibility of unpaid leave
  • Special social allowances for families
  • Benefitful social benefits (acquisition of KTG premium and 2/3 of PK premium)
