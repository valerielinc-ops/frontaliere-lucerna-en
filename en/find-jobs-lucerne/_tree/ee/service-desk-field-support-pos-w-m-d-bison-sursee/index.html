Full description

In order for everything to run smoothly in everyday life at LANDI, TopShop and Volg, you need not only functioning POS systems, but also people who are there when the technology does not play along. As an IT partner, Bison supports our customers in ensuring that their systems work reliably.And this is where you come in! As a Service Desk & Field Support PoS, you are just as in demand on the phone as you are on site. You analyze disruptions, find solutions, install and replace hardware and ensure that our customers can continue working quickly.You have a “can-do” mentality, like to take part and enjoy helping customers easily and solution-oriented? Even if it gets hectic, you keep track and look for a solution instead of a problem?Then you fit exactly to our bison flock. We look forward to meeting you!

You do that.

  • You provide the 1st level support for our PoS and POS systems and support our customers by phone for technical questions and problems
  • You accept customer inquiries, analyze the problem and find the right solution independently or together with your team
  • You take over field support operations directly at our customers on site and resolve technical problems independently
  • You carry out installations, commissionings and the exchange of PoS components and IT hardware on site
  • You document disruptions and solutions and, if necessary, ensure the transfer to the 2nd Level Support or other specialist departments
  • You participate in the Pikettdienst and together with your team ensure that our customers can count on reliable support outside of regular support hours.
  • As an established, communicative and team-oriented personality, you quickly gain the trust of our customers and your new team
  • With your dedicated work, you contribute to solving disruptions in a sustainable manner and continuously improving the quality of service for our customers.

You bring that to me.

  • A completed vocational apprenticeship in the technical, computer science or comparable field
  • Ideally, first experience in IT support, service desk, field support or technical customer service
  • Enjoying direct customer contact as well as a pronounced service and solution orientation
  • Technical flair and the willingness to familiarize yourself with PoS systems, hardware and various IT components
  • You like to work independently and also practically
  • With your structured and solution-oriented way of working, you also keep track of several customer inquiries
  • Readiness for regular assignments with our customers on site and for participation in the pikett service
  • Possession of category B driving licence
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Knowledge of French is beneficial

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Lucerne?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Lucerne's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Lucerne's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
