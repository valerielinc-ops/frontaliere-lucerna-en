Full description

Luzerner Psychiatrie AG is a teaching hospital of the University of Lucerne and is represented by its services at various locations in the Canton of Lucerne and in Sarnen, Canton of Obwalden. More than 1,600 employees from a wide range of professional groups and specialist fields are committed, with humanity and expertise, to the well-being of patients. The services are aimed at children, adolescents and adults up to an advanced age.

APN Nursing Expert (f/m/d) 80%

Your responsibilities

  • Your area of responsibility covers three specialist wards (depression and stress-related disorders, rehabilitation, residential psychiatry)
  • Work in direct clinical practice in collaboration with social networks
  • Consulting and coaching of treatment teams
  • Further development of interdisciplinary treatment concepts involving the target groups
  • Design, delivery and evaluation of workshops
  • Management of and participation in internal and regional projects
  • Professional leadership and collaboration with the nursing specialists and within the nursing expert team
  • Involvement in specialist networks and participation in conferences
  • Co-supervision of student placements (Bachelor's/Master's)

Your profile

  • Completed master's degree in nursing science or willingness to complete one
  • Professional experience as a nursing professional in a psychiatric setting
  • Strong initiative and self-organization
  • Willingness to innovate and enthusiasm for developing your own APN role and driving change processes forward
  • Experience in project management
  • Trialogical approach to thinking and working
  • Interest in Open Dialogue

Benefits

  • A high degree of scope to shape your work and independent planning of working hours
  • Mutual learning within the nursing expert team
  • Continuing-education contract for a master's degree
  • Thorough induction into the diverse field of work
  • Opportunity to attend internal Open Dialogue continuing education
