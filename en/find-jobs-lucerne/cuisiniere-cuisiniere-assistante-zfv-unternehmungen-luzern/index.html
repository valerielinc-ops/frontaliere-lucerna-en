The gastronomy at the Swiss Transport House, in Lucerne, includes a self-service restaurant and a café bar. In addition, internal and external catering for up to 2500 people is carried out. True to the ZFV philosophy, freshly cooked locally with regional, ecologically well-produced and fair trade food. Are you our new team member who supports us as cook / chef: (aid) immediately or by arrangement?
