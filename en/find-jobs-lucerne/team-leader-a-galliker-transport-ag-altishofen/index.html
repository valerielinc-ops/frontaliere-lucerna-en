Full description

Team leader (a) As a nationally and internationally oriented transport and logistics company, Galliker Transport AG concentrates on its core areas of car, cargo and food logistics. The Altishofen LU Logistics Park is the hub of the national and international Galliker network. We offer comprehensive storage, picking and packaging services for our customers. We are looking for you – immediately or by agreement – as a team leader. Task area

  • Leading and motivating a team of 15 -25 MA, incl. Resource planning
  • Conducting Quarterly and Qualification Talks
  • Ensuring Trans-Fair knowledge within the team
  • Monitoring the day-to-day logistics business of well-known warehouse customers
  • Optimizing and managing the warehouse, according to logistical principles
  • Documentation of customer processes
  • Preparation, control and release of performance data for warehouse accounting (key user)
  • Maintaining our high standards of order and cleanliness

Requirement profile

  • completed vocational apprenticeship as a logistician
  • Further education as a logistics or process specialist of advantage
  • Flair for commercial work and a very good business understanding
  • Designated leadership experience
  • Experience in GDP/GMP and HACCP processes
  • very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Skilled computer user
  • Reliable, flexible, resilient, independent, capable of teamwork, flexible and quality-conscious

Offer

  • A cool, motivated and collaborative team
  • Good career prospects
  • High degree of participation in sub-projects
  • beautiful, modern, air-conditioned warehouses
  • a diverse, varied and interesting range of orders
  • Internal and external training opportunities

Did we get your interest? We look forward to your application. Elisha Affentranger, Human Resources, telephone: +41 62 748 80 70.
