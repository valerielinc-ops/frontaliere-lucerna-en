Role overview

ParaWork also accompanies people with paraplegia or mobility restrictions after inpatient rehabilitation when they return to school, vocational training or work and the world of work.

The implementation of the work is closely aligned with the principles of Supported Employment (SE) and Supported Education (SEd) Switzerland.

The job coaching team works in the office in Nottwil and in the home office, enabling flexible, location-independent and efficient support processes.
