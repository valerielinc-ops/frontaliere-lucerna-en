Full description

Nothing works without you. In the spare parts warehouse, you ensure that our technology is always supplied with the right spare parts. With your organizational talent, your technical understanding and your attention to detail, you can reliably keep our production facilities running.

You can do that.

  • You coordinate the acceptance of goods in the technical spare parts warehouse, receive internal and external packages, record them via SAP and forward them to the right places.
  • You also support internal customers in the issuance of technical consumables.
  • With your structured and precise working method, you ensure a smooth national and international outflow of goods.
  • You set up and control orders carefully, compile all necessary documents and keep a clean documentation and filing.
  • The recording of new materials and disposal of old materials in the system as well as various works in the magazine round off this exciting field of activity.

You bring that with you

  • You have completed technical training with experience in warehouse management.
  • Your system affinity helps you operate the MS Office and SAP programs.
  • You master German in word and writing.
  • You have a structured, independent and reliable way of working.
  • Technical understanding and enjoyment of working with different specialist areas.

Talks will not take place until the 14th. September 2026.

This is what we offer you

  • 5 weeks of vacation: Enjoy five weeks of vacation per year. From the age of 46, your holiday entitlement gradually increases to up to six weeks.
  • Attractive pension fund with flexible savings plans: Our Emmi pension foundation pays interest on your retirement savings above the legal minimum. Your contribution is based on your annual salary – without coordination deduction. Choose between standard or plus savings plan with 1% higher savings contributions.
  • Voluntary Success Sharing: Depending on the business success, you will receive a voluntary success Sharing. In doing so, we want to acknowledge your valuable contribution to the company’s success.
  • Attractive surcharges & additional services: You will receive 20% time allowance from 8 pm, paid warm-up times, a time credit for changing times and a fair compensation for piquetting outside your working hours.
  • Cheap meals in the staff restaurant: For breakfast, lunch and break meals, a staff restaurant with discounted employee prices is available.
  • Discounts: Benefit from up to 20% discount on Emmi products, attractive leisure and travel discounts, special offers at top brands and fleet discounts when buying a car.
