Full description

The Lucerne Canton Hospital with its locations in Lucerne, Sursee and Wolhusen is a company of the LUKS Group and thus belongs to one of the leading hospital groups in Switzerland. The more than 8,500 employees are there with warmth, competence and commitment around the clock for the well-being of patients. The clinics and institutes of the LUKS Group offer medical services of the highest quality.

Strengthen our team at 1. January 2027 or by appointment in Sursee! She expects an exciting environment in which you accompany people in challenging life situations and help shape sustainable solutions. Tasks: • They accompany patients of all age and life situations as well as their environment with expertise and empathy through challenging phases. • You create orientation by advising on social, personal, financial and legal issues in the context of hospital stay and exit planning • You develop sustainable connection solutions, provide targeted assistance if necessary and network with the right specialists • You are part of an interdisciplinary team and actively contribute to ensuring that transitions are seamless and patient-centered. Requirements • You have a degree in social work or are about to graduate • You think networked, recognize connections and keep an eye on processes • You work independently, solution-oriented and take responsibility • With your positive, open attitude, you contribute to a humorous team where cohesion and mutual support are felt every day. We offer: • Meaningful work with responsibility and a lot of creative scope • Exchange and further development through specialist exchange, intervision, supervision and interprofessional cooperation • Modern, paperless working method with a continuous clinic information system • Attractive employment conditions, training support and diverse benefits
