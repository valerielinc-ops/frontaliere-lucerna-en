Full description

As a leading Swiss construction and real estate service provider, Implenia develops, realizes and manages living spaces, working environments and infrastructure for future generations in Switzerland and Germany. Implenia also offers tunnelling and related infrastructure projects in other markets. The company combines the know-how of highly qualified consulting, development, planning and execution units under one roof into an integrated, multinationally leading construction and real estate service provider. Headquartered in Opfikon near Zurich, Implenia employs more than 8,000 people (FTE) across Europe and achieved a turnover of CHF 3.5 billion in 2025. More information at implenia.com

For our Baumeister location in Gisikon (Lucerne office) we are looking for you as project manager: in Baumeister (m/f/d). Your field of application extends across Central Switzerland, where you are responsible for challenging building construction projects from the start of construction to the handover.

At the Gisikon site, Implenia offers the entire portfolio of services in building construction: from AVOR to execution and completion of complex new construction and modernization projects. In doing so, we consistently focus on digital processes: calculation, extent and supplementary offers run via BauBit pro, BIM models and plans are shared with all project participants via Trimble Connect, and delivery notes are handled paperlessly via Comstruct. You will work in an experienced, interdisciplinary team that aims to efficiently implement challenging construction projects with modern tools.

You move that

  • Management and implementation of demanding buildings
  • Commercial and technical management of the project
  • Leading the builders: inside
  • Independent preparation and enforcement of supplementary offers
  • Resource planning and monitoring of contract management

This is why you succeed

  • Completion as a dipl. Civil engineer FH, dipl. Baumeister or dipl. Construction manager TS
  • Several years of experience in project management in a construction company
  • Experience with Swiss standards and in building contracts
  • Entrepreneurial thinking and action
  • Commitment and enthusiasm for building
  • Negotiable knowledge of German in word and writing

This is what we offer you

  • Exciting major projects
  • A multicultural working environment with the chance to shape a future worth living
  • State-of-the-art technologies and a contemporary workplace at a sustainable company
  • A company that promotes talent and the training of its employees
  • The security of a financially healthy, broad-based group with strong roots in Switzerland
  • An exciting selection of benefits:
  • 5 weeks holidays and 9 paid bridge days
  • an in-house pension fund with very good coverage and 3 contribution levels
  • numerous discounts on well-known suppliers of products and services
