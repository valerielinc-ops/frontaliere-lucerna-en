Full description

The Lucerne Canton Hospital with its locations in Lucerne, Sursee and Wolhusen is a company of the LUKS Group and thus belongs to one of the leading hospital groups in Switzerland. The more than 8,500 employees are there with warmth, competence and commitment around the clock for the well-being of patients. The clinics and institutes of the LUKS Group offer medical services of the highest quality.

For our team we are looking for reinforcement as of 1 November 2026 or by agreement. Tasks: • As a member of a dedicated and innovative team, you will process and coordinate inquiries from social and supplementary insurance companies (VVG) on outpatient and inpatient benefits cases. • In close cooperation with the medical management, you prepare medical opinions on cost languages, medicines, emergency service missions, therapies and other performance tests. • Through your hospital-wide networked cooperation, you contribute significantly to an efficient and timely answer to insurer inquiries Requirements • This versatile area of responsibility is aimed in particular at specialists from the fields of care, health insurance, medical secretariat, pharmacy or paramedicine. • They have in-depth knowledge of medical terminology and are adept at handling modern IT applications. Knowledge of the Swiss KVG performance test as well as experience in the health insurance environment are advantageous • Their way of working is characterized by accuracy, independence and efficiency. In addition, you convince with a quick understanding and stylish written expression • As a resilient, team-oriented personality, you appreciate varied tasks and demanding administrative tasks in a dynamic hospital environment. We offer: • Enjoy a collegial, appreciative working environment in one of the largest and most modern hospitals in Central Switzerland • It awaits you a responsible activity with a lot of independence and scope for design • Benefit from flexible working time models, attractive home office opportunities and progressive social benefits
