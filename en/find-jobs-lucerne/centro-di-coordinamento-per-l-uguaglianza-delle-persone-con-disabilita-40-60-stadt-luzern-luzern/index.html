Full description

We focus on proximity and openness: At the city administration we live the du-culture – already in the application process. Engage yourself with your project experience and know-how for the city of Lucerne and work for the inclusion of people with disabilities.

Your tasks

  • You advise and sensitize the service departments of the city administration on issues of inclusion and equality.
  • As a point of contact, you take on concerns of people with disabilities.
  • You work in urban planning, projects and decrees.
  • In your role, you work closely with the Confederation, the Canton and private institutions and associations.
  • You ensure the systematic involvement of people with disabilities.
  • With targeted awareness and outreach, you increase awareness of equality, diversity and inclusion.

Your profile

  • You have a (specialised) university degree, preferably in social sciences, humanities or law, architecture, urban or spatial planning or a related discipline.
  • You bring (professional) experience in equality and discrimination issues.
  • You have several years of professional and project management experience and are used to advancing projects independently.
  • A strong will to shape and a high degree of personal responsibility distinguish you.
  • You are interested in working at the interface of administration and politics
  • Experience in public administration is beneficial.

Within the Board of Directors of the President of the City of Lucerne, the City of Lucerne is rebuilding a specialised office for gender equality for people with disabilities. It will become the City of Lucerne’s competence centre for the legal and actual equality of people with disabilities. The specialist centre advises and supports the municipal service departments on issues of equality of people with disabilities and is the municipal contact and triage centre for the concerns of people with disabilities and their relatives.
