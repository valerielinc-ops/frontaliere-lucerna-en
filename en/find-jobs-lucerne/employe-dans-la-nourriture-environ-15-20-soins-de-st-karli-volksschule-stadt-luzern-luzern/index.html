Full description

Would you like to contribute your experience in the field of catering in the primary school of St. Karli? Support our team four to five days a week in the preparation of the food rooms, the delivery of meals and the necessary cleaning work – and together with us contribute to the welfare of children and adults. Tasks:

  • Together with the care team, you prepare the food room for the children and provide an inviting atmosphere during lunchtime.
  • You set up meals in the Bain-Maries and accompany the food service for up to 30 children in 3rd to 6th grade. You contribute to a pleasant and appreciative food culture.
  • You support the children during lunchtime and are a friendly, reliable contact person in the dining room.
  • You take on independently incurred domestic work in the kitchen and dining sector. After eating, you clean tables, chairs, work surfaces and the premises and ensure the proper cleaning of the Bain-Maries.
  • You ensure compliance with the cantonal hygiene guidelines and the hygiene concept of the elementary school in Lucerne and work responsibly according to the applicable standards.
  • You work mainly during the school weeks, an average of 9 to 11 hours per week, from 11.45 to about 14.00 / 2.30 pm. Requirements
  • Ideally, you will bring practical experience in the kitchen or housekeeping sector or enjoy working in this field.
  • You enjoy fresh meals, a well-groomed environment and contact with children of primary school age.
  • Order, cleanliness and hygiene are a matter of course for you and you work reliably and carefully.
  • You are a responsible, open personality and appreciate working together in a team.
  • You work in a structured, independent way and keep an overview even in lively situations.
  • Flexibility, resilience and reliability set you apart.
  • You like to take on board and take on domestic work in the kitchen and in the dining room with commitment.
