Full description

Tasks

  • They ensure a secure, timely and economical supply of medicines and take over the acceptance of goods, warehousing, warehousing and management of medicines and materials with SAP
  • With care and responsibility, you pick and provide internal and external orders, process returns, supervise the counter service and ensure a smooth delivery of medicines and narcotics
  • In your day-to-day work, you consistently pay attention to compliance with legal and qualitative requirements, in particular in the handling of cold chain, highly expensive and risky medicines, and participate in inventory, expiration date and quality processes.
  • In defined departments, you contribute to a hygienically correct and optimized drug management, maintain Kanban systems, avoid supply gaps and – depending on the location – support additional tasks such as drug transport, the operation of the ROWA Vmax or the location-based order organization.

Requirements

  • You have completed training in healthcare, medical technology or logistics EFZ, ideally have professional experience in dealing with medicines and are familiar with SAP MM as well as the MS Office applications or are ready to acquire them quickly
  • With a high willingness to learn, a quick apprehension and openness to change, you will also find yourself safe in new processes, legal requirements and digital workflows.
  • Their working method is characterized by service orientation, great attention to detail and a pronounced awareness of quality, especially in the careful handling of medicines taking into account the duty of care and confidentiality according to the Therapeutic Products Act
  • As a reliable, helpful and team-oriented personality, you also work calmly, accurately and constructively with different internal stakeholders in demanding situations.

We offer

  • Look forward to a responsible task in a central care area where you make a direct contribution to secure patient care with your work
  • A versatile and well-established environment awaits you with clear processes, modern infrastructure and exciting interfaces to pharmacy, care, logistics and other areas of the hospital
  • We offer you the opportunity to continuously deepen your specialist knowledge in a demanding regulated field of work and to further develop on-the-job and through targeted training.
  • With us, you work in a culture that is characterized by warmth, competence and networking and offers you an appreciative cooperation and attractive employment conditions. Further information can be found at www.luks.ch/employment conditions
