Full description

cereneo – Centre for Neurology and Rehabilitation is a world-leading centre in the field of neurological rehabilitation. Our location on the beautiful Lake Vierwaldstättersee offers not only a breathtaking view of Pilatus and Rigi, but also a highly modern, accessible infrastructure. We focus on excellence, warmth, motivation, teamwork and innovation – values that determine our daily actions and ensure the quality of our care.

What awaits you with us

  • Responsibility for the implementation of professional care as part of the care process and reference care.
  • Interprofessional cooperation for the best possible, individual implementation of the care and therapy concept.
  • Documentation and performance recording of the care process.
  • Individual relationships with patients and relatives from different cultures.
  • Continuous improvement of care quality through active participation in improvement processes.

Your duties and responsibilities

  • Management and implementation of the care process within the framework of reference care
  • Cooperative cooperation and competent delegation to caregivers
  • shift work in all shifts (early, late and night)
  • Participation in interprofessional and team meetings
  • Ensuring patient safety and compliance with quality and hygiene standards
  • Economical and careful use of resources
  • Support and training of apprentices and new employees
  • Drug management according to medical prescription

What should you bring?

  • Qualification as a Dipl. Care professional HF/FH
  • Empathy, role distance and trustworthiness
  • Positive attitude to working with people with neurological limitations of all ages
  • Professional interaction with external partners and customers
  • Interested in working in an interdisciplinary team
  • Readiness for continuous training
  • Flexibility, initiative, communication and teamwork
  • Criticism and conflict capability
  • Good knowledge of German and/or English, other languages beneficial

What we offer

  • Modern infrastructure: Perfectly located working environment with a view of Lake Vierwaldstättersee, Pilatus and Rigi.
  • Development: Personal training and development opportunities.
  • Social benefits: Good social benefits (NBU, employer share pension fund 60%).
  • Parking & public transport: Free parking and access to e-charging stations, subsidy for public transport.
  • Food: Lunch check with 50% employer participation, free coffee, tea, water & fruit.
  • Kita: Discount for our connected Kita.
  • Discounts: Various discounts for leisure activities.
  • Fitness: Sharing fitness equipment.

Our corporate culture

  • EXCELLENCE: Using the latest research findings and first-class service
  • HEARTH: We are always there with all our heart. Of course, this also includes the sincere treatment of our patients: inside, but also between our staff
  • MOTIVATION: Joint goal achievement and support of patients: women to improve their quality of life
  • TEAM WORK: Reliability and mutual support in the team
  • INNOVATION: Research and testing of new therapeutic approaches and devices

If you are an open, committed person who would like to work in an interdisciplinary team and continuously develop, we look forward to your application. Become part of our team and actively shape the future of neurological rehabilitation.
