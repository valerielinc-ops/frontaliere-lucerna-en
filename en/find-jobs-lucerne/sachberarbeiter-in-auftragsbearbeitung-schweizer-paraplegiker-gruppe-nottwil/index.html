Full description

Become part of something bigger. Work with more than 2000 specialists from more than 100 professions and enable paraplegic people to live a self-determined life. On the modern campus in Nottwil, an inspiring atmosphere and interdisciplinary cooperation at eye level awaits you.

Tasks

  • You are responsible for the administrative processing of supplies of rehabilitation technology and orthopaedic technology (offering and mailing of offers, follow-up with payers, delivery note creation, reporter creation, master data maintenance, etc.).
  • You are responsible for scheduling for service & repairs, new and sports equipment, special construction SOB and aid consulting, including possible transport planning and creation of reports.
  • You will maintain telephone, written or personal contact at the reception with patients/clients, insurance companies and the other departments of the clinic (physical and occupational therapy, foundation, social service, patient administration and technical service).
  • You process requests for rental equipment including the associated administration and the submission of the equipment.
  • You are responsible for the management and organization of the new equipment, exhibition, occupation and rental pool.
  • You support the employees of the rehabilitation technology department (everyday assistance, service & repairs, new and sports equipment, special construction SOB), orthopaedic technology and supply chain management with your foreign language skills.

Requirements

  • For this versatile commercial activity you have a completed commercial training and ideally bring industry knowledge.
  • For this function, good language skills in French and/or Italian are desired.
  • Knowledge of scheduling and scheduling is an advantage.
  • If you appreciate the cooperation with other departments and have a strong quality awareness, we look forward to meeting you.

We offer

You not only expect attractive employment conditions, but also numerous other advantages. Look forward to meaningful work in a modern working environment, flexible working hours and a wide range of further education opportunities. Come and experience our unique spirit. More information on parplegie.ch/bonus.

Orthotec promotes the #free movement of people with paralysis and similar limitations through tools and services. Around 100 employees are committed to this mission in the areas of vehicle conversion, rehabilitation technology, orthopaedic technology, continence and everyday assistance as well as wheelchair sports. Orthotec is part of the Swiss Paraplegic Group in Nottwil and is the official supplier of Swiss Paralympic.
