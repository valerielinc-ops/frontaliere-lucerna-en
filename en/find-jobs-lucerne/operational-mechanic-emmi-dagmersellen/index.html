Full description

You enjoy food and have long wanted to know how it is made. Especially the dairy products fascinate you and you have a flair for technology. Then you can use this at our many production facilities. Mozzarella, cream cheese or cottage cheese are produced every day in the highest quality. And you are part of it!

You can do that.

  • With your commitment, you ensure that our modern production facilities run reliably at all times: You carry out control, maintenance and maintenance work independently and responsibly and thus make a direct contribution to a smooth production process.
  • Thanks to your technical know-how and your analytical working methods, you can identify disruptions at an early stage and repair them efficiently and sustainably. This ensures high plant availability and keeps our production moving.
  • You actively contribute new ideas, identify potential for improvement and lead optimization projects from the first idea to successful implementation. Your creative will makes our processes a bit better every day.
  • With your commitment, you make a significant contribution to ensuring that our products remain safe and of high quality – and that our processes function economically and efficiently.
  • Collaboration with colleagues from production, technology and other specialist areas makes you happy. You value an environment where you communicate openly, find solutions together and continual improvement is part of the culture.

You bring that with you

  • You bring a completed training in a mechanical or comparable technical profession – and thus exactly the expertise that is crucial in the industrial environment. Your experience forms the basis for high-quality work in daily operations.
  • Flexibility and a sense of responsibility characterize you: During 3–5 weeks per year, you reliably take care of early and piketting service (without shift operation) and ensure that our facilities remain operational at all times.
  • Your high willingness to learn as well as your structured and independent working method allow you to quickly familiarize yourself with new technologies and tasks. You tackle, quickly become confident in new topics and develop continuously
  • As an open, communicative and goal-oriented personality, you enjoy working in a team – and at the same time take responsibility for your own tasks. In this way, you actively contribute to a constructive and solution-oriented cooperation.
  • Very good knowledge of German and a category B driver’s license round off your profile and make you flexible – whether in daily operations or on-site assignments.

This is what we offer you

  • 5 weeks of vacation: Enjoy five weeks of vacation per year. From the age of 46, your holiday entitlement gradually increases to up to six weeks.
  • Attractive pension fund with flexible savings plans: Our Emmi pension foundation pays interest on your retirement balance above the legal minimum. Your contribution is based on your annual salary – without coordination deduction. Choose between standard or plus savings plan with 1% higher savings contributions.
  • Voluntary Success Sharing: Depending on the business success, you will receive a voluntary success Sharing. In doing so, we want to acknowledge your valuable contribution to the company’s success.
  • Attractive surcharges & additional services: You receive 20% time allowance from 8 pm, paid warm-up times, a time credit for changing times and a fair compensation for piquetting outside your working hours.
  • Individual development plan: With tailor-made development plans, we specifically promote your individual skills and support your long-term growth – a central component of our sustainability strategy. Learn more .
  • Focus on your health: We offer occupational health management, preventive measures and free social advice from our partner Proitera for professional and private concerns.
