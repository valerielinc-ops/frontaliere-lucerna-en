The outpatient surgery center of the St. Anna Clinic offers patients a state-of-the-art and efficient care. Here you can use your expertise in a targeted and structured work routine. Do you want to work in a professional and appreciative environment where your expertise is appreciated and you can move something every day? Then you are exactly right with us!

YOUR TASKS

  • You will be able to advise on all procedures in our outpatient surgery centre at the station
  • From the professional preparation of the operating room including the storage of patients and patients to support during the operation, you will ensure the highest standards and safety
  • your expertise and commitment will make the difference for our patients and our team
  • After the procedures you will take care of complete follow-up and documentation
  • You will ensure compliance with all hygiene and safety regulations

YOUR PROFILE

  • You have completed your training as a graduate in business management
  • You like to work in a structured and precise way, even in a dynamic environment
  • Your team spirit and your resilience help you keep a cool head even in stressful situations
  • You appreciate your own responsibility and are ready to contribute to a valuable team
  • You enjoy working at all our three locations at Lucerne (Klinik St. Anna im Bahnhof, Klinik St. Anna in Meggen and Klinik St. Anna)

OUR OFFER

  • Structured training in this versatile area of work
  • support in the development of your potential through individual training opportunities
  • possibility of up to 7 weeks of holidays per year
  • Paid change time in the form of up to 4 additional days of free time
