Hubelmatt secondary school – your new place of work Our school is located above the Lucerne Allmend, surrounded by forest and sports facilities. Thanks to public transport and cycling routes, you are quickly with us. We are a committed, dynamic team that values collaboration and exchange. Self-organized learning is a central part of our inclusive secondary school model. The focus is on the individual support and promotion of our learners – respectful, appreciative and at eye level. Participation is important to us. Our students are actively involved. We live codetermination and develop together as a learning organization. In addition, we are a partner school of the PH Lucerne and actively participate in the training of teachers. For the accompaniment of a student with an integrative special school measure in the area of behavior, the support of a student with an integrative special school measure in the area of cognitive development and the cooperation in music lessons, we are looking for a committed class assistance. You accompany young people with different needs in everyday school life, support teachers in the classroom and contribute to a positive learning environment. Joy in working with young people, patience, resilience and a constructive and appreciative approach to challenging behavior are important for this task. Musical knowledge is not required.
