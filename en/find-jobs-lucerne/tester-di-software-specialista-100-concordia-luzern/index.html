Full description

Honest cooperation: 100% healthy Bundesplatz 15, 6002 Lucerne Do you want to play a decisive role in shaping the quality of our customer platform and our performance processes and work closely together with different specialist areas and IT? As a software tester: you work in the specialist test team (five people in the area of Syrius, four in the area of customer platform) and are part of an interdisciplinary Scrum team. Your activity is divided approximately 50% between the customer platform (Internet reward calculator) and 50% between the service area Syrius (project PaC/ad.Claim). Together with specialists: inside, product owners, business analyst: inside, developer: inside and solution architect: inside, you ensure that our web applications and Syrius processes are stable, correct and user-friendly – from the project phase to ongoing development in the company.

This is what awaits you

Your tasks in test and quality assurance

  • You will analyze technical requirements, specifications and error tickets in the areas of customer platform (internet reward calculator) and services / Syrius (project PaC/ad.Claim) and bring your view on quality and testability early.
  • You create and maintain test cases, test preconditions and test data – for manual tests and for automated tests as part of our regression – and ensure that changes are reliably secured.
  • You carry out systematic and exploratory acceptance tests (UAT) as well as integration and system tests – both in the web area and in the Syrius environment – and ensure that the solutions convince professionally and technically.

Internet reward calculator (late project phase)

  • Support for the last test cycles before the go-live.
  • Accompaniment of the go-live and stabilization of the solution.
  • Subsequently, focus on ongoing tests (e.g. release and regression tests for new functions and adjustments).

PaC / ad.Claim (from the start of the project)

  • Analysis of requirements and test case design
  • Test planning and implementation in close cooperation with specialist department, IT and manufacturers (Adcubum); You are there from the beginning and can help shape the solution.

Technical test management and quality assurance

  • As a representative: in the field of test management, you also contribute your know-how to cross-divisional projects (requirement check, test design, test implementation).
  • You report defects in Jira and ALM (Application Quality Management CE 25.1), perform retests and analyze the test results.
  • You use our internally developed test automation framework to support automation.

In-service tests

  • After the go-live of the projects, you take responsibility for quality assurance in the company:

Planning and conducting release and regression tests. Maintenance and further development of test cases and test data. o Close cooperation with the department for technical and technical changes. Active participation in the further development of our test processes and standards.

You bring that with you

  • You have a strong quality awareness and joy in detecting mistakes – you take quality into account from the beginning.
  • Completed vocational training supplemented by an ISTQB certification (or comparable testing training) is required
  • Additional test certificates and many years of test experience are a plus.
  • You bring several years of experience as a software tester – ideally in the specialist area of a health insurance (e.g. finance, services, market or insurance technology) or in a similarly complex application landscape.
  • You are confident in using IT tools:

O Windows o ERP/core systems, of advantage Syrius (Adcubum) o Jira and ALM (Application Quality Management CE 25.1) for test management and defect tracking.

  • You have experience or great interest in test automation, especially in the use of internal test frameworks.
  • Ideally, you are familiar with agile methods (Scrum/Kanban) and enjoy working in an interdisciplinary Scrum team with different roles (PO, specialist: inside, business analyst: inside, developer: inside, solution architect: inside).
  • You convince with quick comprehension, analytical skills and a well-structured, reliable, initiative and independent working method.
  • You are a committed, team-oriented personality with networked thinking skills and enjoy working with different specialist areas.
  • You express yourself very well in German both in writing and orally and can formulate complex technical and technical issues clearly and comprehensibly.
