IT Infrastructure Engineer Workplace & Server

6204 Sempach IT Systems Engineering

  • Permanent position

Ofis Salary band 90'000 - 105,000 CHF Potential (2-3 years) up to 10'000 CHF

01

Future employer

A systems house in Central Switzerland, with customers from the regional business community and many different environments: this is exactly where you learn more in a short time than at any single company. We are looking for a Systems Engineer with a solid education and initial practical experience who is ready to take on their own projects and customers soon.

That's why the move is worthwhile

  • Varied projects instead of always the same environment
  • A team that shares knowledge, and time for professional development
  • An overall package with fair terms

This advertisement is aimed at professionals who work or have worked for an IT service provider, systems house or MSP.

02

Your responsibilities

  • You set up server, client and cloud environments for customers, optimize them and keep them up to date.
  • You implement projects from planning through handover and are the technical point of contact on site or remotely.
  • You support customer environments during ongoing operations, resolve 2nd- and 3rd-level incidents and ensure that customers' employees can work without interruption.
  • You work on virtualization, data backup and networking topics and systematically expand your knowledge in these areas.
  • You support colleagues when projects or incidents require it and share your expertise with the team.
  • After a short onboarding period, you independently take on tasks and largely plan your own work.

  • Days off for volunteer work

  • Career and advancement opportunities
  • At least 5 weeks of vacation
  • Modern workplace
  • Regular team-building activities and company events
  • Structured and supported onboarding
  • Responsibility

04

Your profile

  • You have completed basic IT training (e.g. IT specialist EFZ, systems engineering) and have initial professional experience at an IT service provider.
  • You have good knowledge of Windows Server and clients, Active Directory and messaging solutions, and experience with virtualization and backup solutions.
  • You have solid foundations in networking (switching, routing, WLAN). Experience with network access control is a plus, as is an interest in IT security.
  • You have experience providing 2nd- and 3rd-level support.
  • You enjoy communicating with customers, remain calm in hectic moments and find pragmatic solutions.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Lucerne?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Lucerne's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Lucerne's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
