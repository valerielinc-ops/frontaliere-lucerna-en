Full description

We make habitats worth living.

pi-System GmbH is a successful partner in the field of integral building automation based in Oberkirch. Our guiding principle is energy efficiency: economical use of energy and sustainable solutions.

Your tasks

  • Responsibility for product strategy, vision and roadmap of ZEV, vZEV and LEG solutions
  • Control of the product life cycle from the idea to further development or replacement
  • Analysis of market, competition and regulatory developments as well as identification of new business opportunities
  • Definition, prioritization and management of product requirements, functions and services
  • Survey of customer needs and integration of market and customer feedback
  • Ensuring a convincing customer experience in cooperation with customers and partners
  • Interface to market, sales, development and operation Coordination of internal and external partners as well as support of pilot projects and product launches
  • Ensuring quality, cost-effectiveness, customer benefit and compliance
  • Support sales and marketing in positioning, presentations, tenders and offers
  • Preparation of product and training materials as well as training and knowledge transfer.

You bring that with you

  • Completed basic technical training (electrical, building automation, computer science, energy or equivalent).
  • Further education at FH, HF or university level in the fields of energy, technology, business or computer science.
  • Professional experience in product management, business development, sales or project management.
  • Knowledge of the Swiss energy market is an advantage.
  • Experience in the field of ZEV, vZEV, LEG, energy management, e-mobility or digital energy services is an advantage.
