Full description

Working where others enjoy their free time: The Oberkirch Golf Park is one of the most beautiful golf courses in Switzerland. As a manager, you take responsibility for the further development of the golf park. Together with your team, you provide unforgettable guest experiences and actively shape the future of Migros Golf AG.

Luogo di lavoro: Migros Golf AG, Golfpark Oberkich, Am Hofbach 1, 6208 Oberkirch

Mansioni

  • Golf for everyone - Everything for golf! Inspiring customers is our passion. We implement this as a team at tournaments, courses as well as corporate and private events. Become part of this passion as the new director of Golfpark Oberkirch!
  • Managing a team of around 60 employees (golf teachers, greenkeeping, home service, restaurant and administration)
  • Promotion and marketing of an attractive appearance of the golf park
  • Planning and coordination of all organizational processes of the golf course
  • Representation of the golf park in the management team of Migros Golf resp. Opposite the Golf Club Oberkirch

Requirements

  • Professional and management experience as well as knowledge: in the field of golf
  • Completed initial or continuing education: in the commercial or academic sector
  • Very good knowledge in dealing with IT tools
  • Enjoyment in dealing with people and a well-groomed appearance
  • Flexibility in working days and times (regular work on weekends during golf season)
  • Enjoyment of various activities
  • Joy of shaping the future of Migros Golf
  • German (very good knowledge)
  • English (good knowledge)

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision to advance or withdraw from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Personal video or telephone conversation: For a large number of applicants, we optionally conduct a personal telephone or video conversation. This gives us the opportunity to get to know more candidates within a short time in order to invite suitable people for the next round. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
  • Getting to know the team: Whether someone fits into the team is an important factor for employees and employers when deciding for or against employment. In a casual framework, we therefore give the opportunity for everyone to get to know each other.
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition (Digital): After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent for digital signature by e-mail. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
