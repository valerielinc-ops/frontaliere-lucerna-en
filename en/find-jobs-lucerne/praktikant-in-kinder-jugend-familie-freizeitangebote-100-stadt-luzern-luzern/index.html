Full description

We focus on proximity and openness: At the city administration we live the du-culture – already in the application process. Do you love interacting with people? Do you want to do meaningful work in the field of children, youth and family? Do you enjoy planning events? Then you are right with us!

Your tasks

  • You are responsible for the personal and telephone support of customers at the reception.
  • You help to organize and run events.
  • One of your tasks is the versatile, administrative teamwork for the specialist areas.
  • In addition, you work on evaluations and statistical data collection.
  • You are responsible for the department’s post processing and material management.

Your profile

  • You have successfully completed the secondary level II (Matura, specialised secondary school or similar).
  • You bring stylish knowledge of German, foreign language skills and a safe handling of MS-Office.
  • You are team capable, resilient and communicative.
  • You have a precise and independent working method.
  • If you are also an open-minded, empathetic and appreciative personality, you fit perfectly into our team.
