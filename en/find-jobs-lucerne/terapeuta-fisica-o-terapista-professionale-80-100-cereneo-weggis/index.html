Full description

As one of the world’s leading clinics, we develop an individualized treatment program with the aim of enabling our patients to enjoy the highest possible degree of independence and freedom of life, thereby optimizing their quality of life. We address both patients from the Swiss cash system and international patients. cereneo offers highly specialized rehabilitation programs for people with neurological diseases and injuries, including stroke, brain injury, neurodegenerative diseases such as Parkinson’s, multiple sclerosis and many other neurological disorders.

Your tasks

You bring your professional knowledge and experience to the rehabilitation everyday life and support our patients with individually coordinated therapy concepts on their way to recovery. Where:

  • independently carry out individual and group therapies and design individual therapy and training programs
  • select suitable, needs-based tools and integrate them specifically into the therapy
  • use appropriate therapeutic methods, ADL training and modern technologies, depending on the subject area
  • work closely with other therapeutic disciplines, care and other specialist areas
  • document your services completely and ensure a tariff-compliant performance recording
  • actively contribute to the further development of our therapeutic offerings and internal processes

What should you bring? Our success is only possible with highly qualified and motivated employees at all levels. That is why openness, initiative and fairness are our top priority. Does that reflect your attitude towards work? Then you ideally bring the following:

  • degree in physiotherapy or occupational therapy with SRK recognition or Eligibility for recognition in Switzerland
  • Completed training in the neurological field
  • sound knowledge and professional experience in neurological rehabilitation
  • Knowledge of ADL training
  • Interest and willingness to use new technologies in therapy
  • Interest in professional and personal development in this area
  • Enjoyment of interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Team capable, independent and friendly personality
  • Open and appreciative communication with patients and colleagues
  • Structured and self-responsible working method
  • Very good knowledge of German and English in word and writing

We offer

We offer you a modern and professional working environment, attractive employment conditions and a wide range of opportunities to develop yourself personally and professionally.

  • Attractive place of work: Modern infrastructure in a unique location with a view of Lake Vierwaldstättersee, Pilatus and Rigi.
  • Further education and development: Individual opportunities for professional and personal education and development.
  • Attractive social benefits: takeover of the NBU premium and an employer’s share of 60% of pension fund contributions.
  • Mobility: Free parking and e-charging stations and a contribution to public transport. In addition, employees benefit from the AVIA fuel card of 5 centimeters discount per litre of fuel and a free roadside service throughout Europe.
  • Food: Lunch check with 50% employer participation and free coffee, tea, water and fruit.
  • Family friendliness: discounted conditions at our connected daycare center.
  • Health and Fitness: Free sharing of existing fitness equipment.
  • Leisure and culture: Various employee benefits as well as attractive leisure offers, including free Pilate tickets, entrance to the Tierpark Goldau and the Swiss Museum Pass with access to over 500 museums in Switzerland.

Our culture – that connects us Excellence, warmth, motivation, teamwork and innovation are more than just values for us. We combine the latest scientific findings with first-class service, meet our patients and each other with warmth and develop new ways in neurological rehabilitation together. If you are an open and committed person, would like to work interdisciplinary and continuously develop, we look forward to getting to know you. Become part of our team and shape the future of neurological rehabilitation with us.

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Lucerne's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Lucerne's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
