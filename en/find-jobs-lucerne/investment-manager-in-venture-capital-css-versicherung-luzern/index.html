Full description

Do you want to help shape the healthcare of tomorrow with investments in start-ups? Then you are right with us. With SwissHealth Ventures, our corporate venture capital fund, we are investing in innovative digital health solutions and creating synergies with CSS’s core business. Our portfolio currently comprises 12 start-ups and two venture funds in different phases of development. For our growing investment team, we are looking for an ambitious, entrepreneurial personality. With us, you have the opportunity to develop in venture capital, build a strong network in the HealthTech ecosystem and actively shape the future of healthcare.

This is what your everyday life looks like

  • Deal Sourcing & Networking: You will find promising HealthTech startups, build relationships with founders, venture capital investors and other players in the European HealthTech ecosystem and ensure a continuous, high-quality deal flow.
  • Investment Analysis & Execution: You analyze business models, markets and competitive landscapes, conduct commercial due diligences and participate in the structuring, negotiation and implementation of investments.
  • Investment Recommendations: You develop sound investment theories, assess opportunities and risks and prepare convincing decision-making bases for our Investment Committee.
  • Portfolio Management: You accompany our portfolio companies in strategic issues, financing rounds and cooperation with CSS.
  • Ecosystem & Strategy: You represent SwissHealth Ventures at relevant industry events, maintain and expand our network and contribute your own impulses to the further development of our investment strategy.

You bring that with you

  • First things first: You have at least 3 years of professional experience in venture capital, investment banking, consulting or in a start-up. Experience in venture capital is a plus.
  • Your interest is in start-ups, technology and innovation in healthcare. You want to build a career in venture capital in the long term.
  • You have a university or university degree, ideally in business, finance, medicine or life sciences.
  • You are curious, ambitious and self-initiative. It’s easy for you to reach out to people, build relationships, and identify and drive new investment opportunities.
  • With analytical thinking and a structured way of working, you penetrate complex topics and can convey them appropriately.
  • You communicate effortlessly in German – both in writing and orally – and have a reliable knowledge of English.
  • Work Flexible: Adapt your work to your lifestyle annually with part-time options and other flexible models like home office and more.
  • Choose your holiday model: 5 or 6 weeks holiday? Your decision. Accordingly, you work 41 or 42 hours a week.
  • Improve yourself further: We like to see it if you educate yourself internally or externally and support generously with time and money.
  • Celebrate with us: Focused work and celebrate. We love this combination and maintain it regularly.
  • Engage Socially: Does Life Mean Good to You? Then let others share your happiness and expand your horizons alone or in a team.
  • Enjoy your wallet: Benefits such as bonus, Reka, discounted health insurance premium and others that quickly make up a few thousands a year.
