These tasks inspire you

  • Ensuring optimal capacity utilization and on-time deliveries
  • Responsibility for decisions and escalation management in day-to-day business
  • Technical management of scheduling
  • Personnel and disciplinary responsibility for dispatchers and chauffeurs
  • Targeted coaching as well as support and further development of employees
  • Continuous optimization of processes, structures and interfaces in cooperation with other departments
  • Standardization of workflows and introduction of new working methods
  • Responsibility for resource planning and its ongoing development
  • Control of working hours and ARV requirements as well as responsibility for absence management
  • Ongoing review of operational target achievement and consistent implementation of agreed measures
  • Support for customers and partners with high quality and service standards
  • Joint development of services with customers
  • Ensuring high delivery quality, adherence to deadlines and productivity
  • Ensuring all quality, safety and compliance requirements in the transport sector
  • Daily coordination with envelope and personnel planning
  • Ensuring the flow of information within scheduling
  • Active participation in meetings
  • Ongoing exchange with the plant management and the other operations centres

We are looking forward to this profile

  • Further training in the transport and logistics sector, for example as a federally qualified operations manager for transport and logistics or a comparable qualification

  • Sound knowledge of scheduling, transport control as well as capacity and resource management
