Full description

Lucerne Psychiatry AG is a teaching hospital of the University of Lucerne and is represented with its offers at various locations in the Canton of Lucerne and in Sarnen, Canton of Obwalden. More than 1,600 employees from a wide range of professional groups and departments are humanely and competently committed to the well-being of patients. The offers are aimed at children, adolescents and adults up to old age. Senior physician / Co-director (m/f/d) 80-100 %

Their tasks

  • Professional, medical and personnel leadership together with a specialist psychologist from the interdisciplinary team
  • Co-design and implementation of projects of professional and institutional development
  • Monitoring of departmental objectives
  • Background and consultancy services
  • Networking and exchange with external assignments and specialist bodies
  • If interested, conduct expert opinions on various issues
  • Integrated psychiatric-psychotherapeutic work including case management for selected patients and group therapy as well as ensuring the medical-psychiatric treatment quality for all patients

Your profile

  • Specialist psychiatry
  • Organizational talent, ideally already with leadership experience
  • Strong team orientation and desire to actively deal with your own leadership role
  • Joy of responsibility and further development of the own department
  • Stylistic confident in German in word and writing

Benefits

  • Appreciative and benevolent working environment
  • Collegial, interdisciplinary teams
  • Supportive and supportive leadership culture
  • 5 weeks holidays + 1 week compensation for background service
  • Possibility for unpaid leave and/or sabbaticals
  • Regulated working hours
  • Participation in internal and external group visions and training
  • Interesting mix of interdisciplinary complex treatment for sufferers with serious psychiatric diseases and primary psychiatric care
