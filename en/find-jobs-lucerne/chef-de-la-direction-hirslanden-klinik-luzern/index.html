Shape the future of care

Care means more to you than organization and processes. For you, people are the focus. You want to create a leadership culture that empowers leaders and fosters collaboration. Together with a dedicated team, you will continue to develop the care of tomorrow. Then you are exactly right with us.

In this key position, you assume overall responsibility for the nursing area of the St. Anna Clinic. As a member of the extended management team, you actively shape the further development and create the framework for high-quality care and future-oriented leadership.

YOUR TASKS

You lead the nursing area professionally, personnel, organizationally and economically and develop it continuously together with your management team.

  • Strengthen your leaders in leadership and foster a culture of leadership based on trust, cooperation and mutual support.
  • You design the strategic orientation of the care area and implement it successfully
  • You will continuously develop care quality, processes and innovations
  • You are responsible for budget, resources and the economic achievement of your area
  • You work closely with our partner doctors and interprofessional partners
  • As a member of the extended management team, you actively contribute your perspective to the further development of the clinic

Your profile

You are an inspiring leader who builds trust and provides guidance. You succeed in winning people over to common goals, connecting different interests and making changes sustainable.

You also bring the following

  • Completed training as a Dipl. Nurse HF/FH, further training in management or business administration is an advantage
  • Strong communication, leadership and social skills as well as a hands-on mentality that you use in interdisciplinary and interprofessional exchange
  • A Positive Leadership Mentality in an Acute Hospital
  • Experience in the further development of larger teams and ideally in quality and process management (lean hospital)
  • Entrepreneurial and systemic thinking skills as well as a sense of economic connections

You can look forward to that.

You can expect a responsible leadership function with a lot of room for manoeuvre in one of the leading private clinics in Switzerland. Together with a dedicated leadership team, you actively shape the future of care. Look forward to a corporate culture that is characterized by trust, appreciation and mutual respect, as well as attractive employment conditions and diverse development and further education opportunities. Your place of work is located in Lucerne, one of the most attractive working and leisure regions in Switzerland.

If you have any questions, Dr. med. Beat Sonderegger, Head of Medical System, at beat.sonderegger@hirslanden.ch or David Friesenegger, Head of HR, at david.friesenegger@hirslanden.ch.

Create the future of care with us – we look forward to meeting you.
