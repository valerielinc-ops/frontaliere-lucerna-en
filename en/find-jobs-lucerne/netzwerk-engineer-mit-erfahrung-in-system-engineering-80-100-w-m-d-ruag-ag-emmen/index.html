Full description

Every day, around 3,000 employees of RUAG and RUAG Real Estate make a significant contribution to the security of Switzerland. They ensure that the Swiss Army and other operational and security organizations can perform their tasks comprehensively at any time.

You can move that

  • You design and integrate complex system and network environments: planning, configuration and optimization of Linux-based system solutions as well as modern network technologies such as switching, routing, overlay/underlay and IPv4/IPv6. This also includes the integration of different hardware and software components and ensuring their smooth interaction.
  • You automate and optimize processes: Development and maintenance of automated test cases with Python, modern CI/CD tools and virtualization technologies as well as continuous optimization of processes for system integration, configuration and testing.
  • You will analyze complex technical challenges: systematic investigation, limitation and reproduction of errors in the interaction of network, operating system and software. Together with the participating departments and development teams, you will develop and validate sustainable solutions.
  • You ensure quality and reliability: development of suitable test scenarios, implementation of integration and system tests in realistic environments and technical evaluation of the results in terms of functionality and stability.
  • You ensure transparency and traceability: Structured documentation of system architectures, configurations, technical analyses and test results as the basis for reproducible processes and sustainable knowledge transfer.
  • You work interdisciplinary and solution-oriented: Close cooperation with development, system engineering and other specialist areas as well as support for integration work and error analyses directly on site.

You bring that with you

  • You have a degree from a university or a higher technical school in a technical area, for example in computer science, electrical engineering, telecommunications or a comparable subject area.
  • You have strong knowledge of developing automated test cases with Python. Additional programming skills in C# and/or C++ are beneficial.
  • You have extensive experience with network technologies such as switching, routing, overlay/underlay, IPv4 and IPv6 as well as in-depth know-how in the use of Linux as a test and network platform.
  • You are familiar with modern CI/CD tools and virtualization environments such as GitLab, Git, Jenkins, Ansible and Hypervisor technologies, including building and maintaining pipelines.
  • You have several years of practical experience in system integration, system testing and technical error analysis and have a good sense of quality, processes and technology.
  • You are familiar with modern testing and development methods and ideally bring one or more certifications, such as ISTQB Certified Tester (FL/TA), IREB Foundation Level, ITIL Foundation, HERMES or Scrum Master.
  • You work in a structured, analytical and self-responsible manner and keep an overview even in complex system and test environments.
  • Teamwork, clear communication and a high willingness to learn are of course part of this for you.
  • You bring a regional travel readiness of 60%.
  • You have good knowledge of German in words and writing. Knowledge of English is an advantage.

Do you not meet all the requirements one hundred percent? Apply anyway. We are always looking for talented and motivated people who want to enrich our team. We attach great importance to diversity and equal opportunities and our goal is to build a diverse team in which everyone can fully develop their personal strengths. We particularly encourage all individuals, regardless of gender, who may have concerns about accepting a job in an army-related environment to apply.
