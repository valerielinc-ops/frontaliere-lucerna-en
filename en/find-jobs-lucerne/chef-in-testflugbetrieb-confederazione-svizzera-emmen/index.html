Full description

  • Technical and organisational management of the team Test flight operations for flight tests with civil and military majority non-certified aviation systems (fighter aircraft, helicopters, turboprop and drones)
  • Team test flight operations including ensuring professional qualification and licensing personnel and professional as well as contact person for agreements with the Air Force
  • Collaborate in test flight operations (e.g. in deployment and operation, as a loadmaster, in load flight, in the creation of instructions, processes and content for manuals, etc.)
  • Ensure operational readiness of relevant equipment and vehicles of the test aircraft, installations, installations and systems as well as aircraft hangars in the Centre Aviation Systems Emmen
  • Specifications (EASA Part 145 / Part 66) for our civil aircraft as well as the specifications of the Luftwaffe for military aviation systems
  • Prepare and accompany audits by military and civilian authorities as well as with industry. Complete and release maintenance documents for work carried out by the team
  • Aircraft mechanic or similar 4-year basic technical training as well as many years of experience in flight operations. Training experience and further training in aviation are advantageous
  • EASA Part-66 Aircraft Maintenance Licence (category B1)
  • Precise, responsible, flexible and team-oriented working methods. Enjoyment and motivation in a wide range of tasks related to test flight operations and the leadership of a team
  • Category B (C/E of advantage) and good IT skills (MS Office/SAP)
  • Prior to employment, aero-medical aptitude must be confirmed by the Aviation Medical Institute
  • You have very good knowledge of the German official language and good knowledge of English. Other official languages are beneficial
