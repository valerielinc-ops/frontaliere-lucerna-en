Full description

Become part of something bigger. Work with more than 2000 specialists from more than 100 professions and enable paraplegic people to live a self-determined life. On the modern campus in Nottwil, an inspiring atmosphere and interdisciplinary cooperation at eye level awaits you.

Tasks

  • Events and sponsoring events: Coordination of Orthotec presences at sports, customer and partner events including material, advertising and logistics management.
  • Sponsorship: Administrative support and further development of sponsorship activities as well as securing agreed sponsorship services.
  • Give-aways and advertising materials: Planning, procurement and management of give-aways, advertising materials and event materials.
  • Website: Maintenance, updating and further development of website content in cooperation with the mediamatist.
  • Newsletter: Content support for newsletter marketing measures.
  • Content: Collaboration in the creation of communication content for Orthotec channels.
  • Further development: cooperation in projects and process optimizations in marketing.

Requirements

  • You have successfully completed a basic training and have further training in the field of marketing & communication (e.g. marketing specialist).
  • You have proven experience in organizing and coordinating events and marketing activities.
  • Your distinctive organizational skills as well as a team-oriented and independent working method distinguish you.
  • You have stylish German and a good feeling for target group-oriented communication for the topics of mobility, rehabilitation, health and sports.
  • You appreciate the variety between office life and events and represent Orthotec with the necessary flexibility on site at selected events.
  • A secure use of digital work equipment and common office applications round off your profile.

We offer

You not only expect attractive employment conditions, but also numerous other advantages. Look forward to meaningful work in a modern working environment, flexible working hours and a wide range of further education opportunities. Come and experience our unique spirit. More information on parplegie.ch/benefits.

Orthotec promotes the #free movement of people with paralysis and similar restrictions through tools and services. Around 100 employees are committed to this mission in the areas of vehicle conversion, rehabilitation technology, orthopaedic technology, continence and everyday assistance as well as wheelchair sports. Orthotec is part of the Swiss Paraplegic Group in Nottwil and is the official supplier of Swiss Paralympic.
