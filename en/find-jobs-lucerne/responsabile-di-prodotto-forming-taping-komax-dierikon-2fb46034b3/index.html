Full description

The Komax group is a pioneer and market leader in the field of automated wire processing. As a globally active Swiss company, we develop and manufacture serial production machines, customer-specific systems, quality assurance modules, test systems, networking solutions, and services for various industries. The Komax group has more than 50 companies with 3500 employees across five continents and a worldwide sales and service network.

Your area of responsibility

  • Definition and further development of the product offering over the entire product life cycle
  • Analysis of customer, market and competition requirements
  • Development of product concepts, business cases and pricing strategies
  • Ensuring the implementation of customer requirements and identifying optimization potential in processes and performance
  • Planning and conducting field tests with customers and OEM partners
  • Responsibility for product and production releases up to series maturity
  • Support for market uptake and market positioning
  • Close cooperation with marketing, sales, development and operations

Your profile

  • Basic technical understanding, business education and high IT affinity
  • Experience in product management, project management or a comparable function
  • Entrepreneurial thinking and the ability to combine technical, economic and customer-related requirements
  • Structured working and strong communication and coordination skills
  • Own initiative, enforcement and implementation strength
  • Very good knowledge of German and English

This is what awaits you

  • The opportunity to help shape a new product from the idea to market success
  • High design freedom and responsibility
  • Collaboration with international clients and interdisciplinary teams
  • An innovative environment that promotes initiative and entrepreneurship

Interested? We look forward to your application. Markus Schmidig (markus.schmidig@komaxgroup.com) and Christian Infanger (christian.infanger@komaxgroup.com) are at your disposal for any questions.
