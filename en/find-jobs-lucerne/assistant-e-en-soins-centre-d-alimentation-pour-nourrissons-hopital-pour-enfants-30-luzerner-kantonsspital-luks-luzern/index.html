Full description

The Children's Hospital Central Switzerland of the Lucerne Cantonal Hospital (LUKS) is one of the largest children's hospitals in Switzerland and serves the entire region of Central Switzerland at the highest level. Our employees work around the clock to ensure that children, young people and their relatives are measurably better off. LUKS, with its locations in Lucerne, Sursee and Wolhusen, is a company of the LUKS Group and thus belongs to one of the leading hospital groups in Switzerland. >For our team we are looking for reinforcement as of 1 January 2027 or by agreement.

Tasks

  • As part of the team, you are responsible for housekeeping and cleaning work
  • You will help with the daily provision of food for premature babies, term-borns and infants and toddlers
  • You support the team in various work in the milk nutrition center
  • Your participation in change processes and team development rounds off your field of activity

Requirements

  • This position is also suitable as a return to the profession
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing round off your profile

  • In addition to our attractive employment conditions, you benefit from numerous employee offers
