Full description

For our office in Gisikon in the area of GU/TU we are looking for a flexible and numerical project assistance (m/f/d). In this function, you support our project and construction management in the execution of GU/TU projects in all administrative and organizational tasks. Your place of work and employment is our location in Gisikon.

Tasks

What tasks await you as project assistant GU/TU (m/f/d), Gisikon, 80-100%?

  • As a project assistant, you coordinate project and construction management appointments, complete their correspondence, support project reporting and create presentations, protocols and project-specific reports.
  • You ensure the filing and documentation of the project documents and help to create the construction documentation
  • You support the project and construction management in the creation and sending of tenders
  • In the construction administration program (RIB iTWO 4.0) you also write and update work contracts and supplements,
  • Invoice verification process with and create invoices to customers
  • You are responsible for the registration and support of guarantees (deposit, execution and warranty guarantee)
  • In addition, you are the interface to insurance for damage cases, take over the damage reporting to insurers after the damage has occurred and support the project management in claims regulations

Requirements

What requirements do you bring as project assistant GU/TU (m/f/d), Gisikon, 80-100%?

  • You have completed a commercial education
  • You have an affinity for the construction industry
  • You prefer to bring project experience
  • You are very familiar with MS Office (especially PowerPoint / Excel)
  • You have a very good knowledge of German
  • You are service- and customer-oriented and bring assertiveness
  • In addition, you are innovative, open, far-sighted, numerical and self-confident, have humor and want to change and improve things

About Implenia

As a leading Swiss construction and real estate service provider, Implenia develops, realizes and manages living spaces, working environments and infrastructure for future generations in Switzerland and Germany. Implenia also offers tunnelling and related infrastructure projects in other markets. The company combines the know-how of highly qualified consulting, development, planning and execution units under one roof into an integrated, multinationally leading construction and real estate service provider.
