In our secretarial team of Sports Medicine & Physiotherapy St. Anna in the train station, you will find a versatile working environment with exciting insights into sports medicine and physiotherapy. Our center is recognized as the Swiss Olympic Medical Base and maintains regional and supraregional medical partnerships.

Are you motivated, team-oriented and looking for a new challenge? Then we look forward to meeting you!

YOUR TASKS

  • You receive our patients and customers in a friendly and professional manner and are their first point of contact
  • You coordinate appointments, collect patient data and ensure smooth processes at the reception and on the phone
  • You take on administrative tasks around our therapy services and self-payment offers as well as the processing of post and e-mails
  • You support in the performance recording and billing
  • With your organizational talent, you support the team and contribute to a well-functioning practical day.

Your profile

  • You have a basic commercial education with further training as a doctor secretary
  • You bring experience in a comparable function and know medical terminology
  • You are service-oriented, resilient and work independently, structured and efficient
  • You enjoy contact with people and safely deal with digital systems and practice software

Our offer

  • Do you like longer breaks? With us you have the option to take up to 7 weeks vacation per year
  • Do you like travelling with public transport or Velo? We contribute financially to your work journey
  • We work with work clothes, you are entitled to up to 4 additional free days of paid changing time
