Full description

Every day, around 3,000 employees of RUAG and RUAG Real Estate make a significant contribution to the security of Switzerland. They ensure that the Swiss Army and other operational and security organizations can perform their tasks comprehensively at any time.

You can move that

  • Coordination and administration in the Facility Management Office (Backoffice & PMO Support)
  • First level support as well as processing and coordination of service missions (order management)
  • Control and monitoring of service workflows incl. Completion of completed orders in the CAFM system
  • Ensure correct performance billing and control of order and performance data
  • Processing of orders and material procurement in SAP incl. Entry of goods
  • Auditing and accounting of creditors’ invoices and clarification of discrepancies between order, delivery and invoice
  • Administrative tasks and support for offers, key and bathroom management as well as office and material management
  • Support for the training of learners KV

You bring that with you

  • Basic commercial training
  • Several years of professional experience in a comparable function, preferably in the industrial environment
  • Communicative personality with a high service orientation
  • Flexibility, resilience and solution-oriented thinking and action
  • Good MS Office, CAFM and SAP knowledge
  • Very good knowledge of German, other languages are advantageous

Do you not meet all the requirements one hundred percent? Apply anyway. We are always looking for talented and motivated people who want to enrich our team. We attach great importance to diversity and equal opportunities and our goal is to build a diverse team in which everyone can fully develop their personal strengths. We particularly encourage all individuals, regardless of gender, who may have concerns about accepting a job in an army-related environment to apply.
