Full description

In the exciting and creative profession as a team leader* in the kitchen, you can provide an all-round fine enjoyment with our guests on a daily basis, both on the stove and while serving.

Luogo di lavoro: Migros Golf AG, Golfpark Oberkich, Am Hofbach 1, 6208 Oberkirch

Mansioni

  • Technical leadership of the kitchen team
  • Responsibility for the smooth operation of the kitchen
  • Planning and organisation of daily production processes
  • Ensuring consistently high food quality
  • Conduct staff meetings and accompaniment of new team members during training in consultation with the caterer
  • Operational and holiday planning of the kitchen team in consultation with the caterer
  • Close cooperation with interfaces of restaurant management, service, purchasing and management
  • Cooperation in the design and further development of the food offer
  • Introducing new ideas and trends from gastronomy and cuisine

Requirements

  • Professional experience: in the à la carte and banqueting sector
  • Completed basic or further education: as a cook* cook
  • Communication and team skills
  • Self-employed and precise working method
  • Organisational flair
  • Readiness for assignments on weekends and irregular working hours
  • Driving licence Cat. B
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Occupational pension fund: Migros Pension Fund offers attractive pension fund services with high employee participation
  • Sports and fitness offers: With us you get discounted fitness subscriptions in the Activ Fitness and Migros Fitness Park
  • Leisure and cultural offers: We offer various discounts and free tickets in the area of leisure and culture
  • Healthy work: Benefit from our company health management and attractive insurance benefits
  • Handy subscription: Employees receive highly discounted conditions for the private Handy subscription incl. Family and friends
  • Cumulus points: You collect additional Cumulus points in the Food and Non Food section

Contatto

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision on progress or withdrawal from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Expert Discussion: In a specialist discussion, specific knowledge relevant for the execution of the job is requested by the manager and/or another specialist and, if necessary, the team. If possible, there is time afterwards to get to know the workplace and team better in a free conversation. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Getting to know the team: Whether someone fits into the team is an important factor for employees and employers when deciding for or against employment. In a casual framework, we therefore give the opportunity for everyone to get to know each other.
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition (Digital): After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent for digital signature by e-mail. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
