Your Task

  • Develop, implement and continuously improve Quality Assurance requirements, processes and standards across manufacturing operations

  • Support process qualification and ensure compliance with customer, regulatory and internal requirements
  • Contribute to customer and internal audits as a Quality Assurance representative
  • Support quality-related activities during RFI/RFQ and proposal processes
  • Contribute to Continuous Improvement initiatives, quality training and coaching across the organization
  • Ensure the successful execution of the Quality Assurance Programme in line with project milestones
