Full description

«Nerd» is not an insult, but a status symbol? Do you know more about computers and networks than Bill Gates? Then we want you on our team. In RUAG's IT department, you have the opportunity to cover the entire ICT landscape, from development to maintenance, and contribute to Switzerland's security with your expertise.

What you can accomplish

  • Development and further development of applications in the field of Tactical Communications
  • Participation in the design and implementation of modern software architectures
  • Development of cross-platform applications for Windows and Android
  • Close collaboration with System Engineering and Project Management
  • Planning, implementation and execution of software tests
  • Analysis and resolution of errors as well as continuous improvement of software quality
  • Participation in the creation of technical documentation
  • Active participation in interdisciplinary development projects

What you bring

  • Completed degree (FH/HF/ETH/Uni) in computer science or comparable technical education
  • Good knowledge of C++
  • Experience with Windows and Android platform development
  • Experience with software architectures
  • Experience with modern development tools and development processes (Git, CI/CD, build systems, automated tests)
  • Knowledge of SIP, VoIP or communication protocols is an advantage
  • Experience in developing modern user interfaces with Flutter (Dart) is an advantage
  • Knowledge of Python and Linux is an advantage
  • Analytical, structured and independent way of working
  • Good spoken and written German and English skills

Do you not meet all the requirements one hundred percent? Apply anyway. We are always looking for talented and motivated people who would like to enrich our team. We place great importance on diversity and equal opportunities, and our goal is to build a diverse team in which everyone can fully develop their personal strengths. We particularly encourage all people, regardless of their gender, who may have concerns about accepting a position in a military-affiliated environment, to apply.
