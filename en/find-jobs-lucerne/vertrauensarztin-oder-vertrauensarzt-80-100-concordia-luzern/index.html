Strengthening employees: 100% healthy Hirschmattstrasse 25, 6003 Lucerne Our diversity is our capital: CONCORDIA brings together people from different cultures, professions and areas of responsibility. Will you also enrich our team soon? We are looking for reinforcement for the entire area of insurance medicine (KVG/VVG).

This is what awaits you

  • The focus is on insured persons who need an indicated and recognized treatment / measure. You advise the service department, the insurance technology as well as, if necessary, other areas of health and life insurance in medical matters as well as compensation issues and mediate between health insurance, service providers, insured persons and institutions (e.g. FOPH).
  • You write insurance medical reports in the area of Compulsory Health Insurance (KVG) and Supplementary Insurance (VVG)
  • You are interested in new developments in medicine and can form an opinion on the efficacy, expediency and cost-effectiveness of these developments/applications on the basis of studies / literature / further education.
  • You actively participate in the design and improvement of work processes and cooperation in the VAD

You bring that with you

  • You have a specialist title in internal medicine or with a close connection to internal medicine (e.g. oncology) and preferably bring insurance experience in the field of KVG/VVG or UVG, IVG
  • You have the qualification certificate Trusted physician SGV or would be ready to purchase it in a timely manner
  • You are familiar with the English and French languages and you can have a conversation in French
  • Care, accuracy and high concentration are essential characteristics of your work method
  • You enjoy working in a team consisting of pharmacists, trusted doctors, specialists and assistants
  • In the longer term, a team leadership and/or deputy function would also be conceivable for you
  • You are interested in political trends and the resulting legal and insurance consequences.
