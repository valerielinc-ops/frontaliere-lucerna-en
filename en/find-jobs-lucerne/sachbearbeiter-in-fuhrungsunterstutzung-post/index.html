Full description

Are you looking for a new challenge that requires organizational talent, versatility and teamwork? In this position, you take on a wide range of tasks: from operational planning to process optimization. You work closely with the management team of the distribution area Kriens. Apply now, because with you we create an even more efficient and smoother cooperation in the team.

You can do that.

  • You support the management of the distribution area Kriens and the support team in administrative and personnel tasks.
  • You act as a central interface between employees, managers, HR and case management.
  • You are responsible for the sickness and accidents of the distribution area.
  • The recruitment and support of new employees in the delivery is also in your hands.
  • Maintaining the personnel deployment systems, evaluating personnel data and implementing measures in the area of safety and health protection are also among your tasks.
  • You organize meetings, workshops and training sessions and create valuable exchange opportunities for everyone involved.

You bring that with you

  • You have gained initial experience in HR or employee management or would like to develop further in this area.
  • As a central hub, you meet your colleagues at eye level and contribute to a positive working atmosphere.
  • You are a reliable and trustworthy personality who likes to work both independently and in a team.
  • You have completed training as an HR assistant: or are ready to complete this training in a timely manner.
  • You are extremely PC-affine and knowledge of SAP applications is an advantage, but not a must – we support you with your training.
  • Very good oral and written knowledge of German round off your profile.

With you we are ready for big steps. We appreciate your determination to learn new things. Even if you do not yet meet all the requirements for this position, we are ready to learn and grow together with you.
