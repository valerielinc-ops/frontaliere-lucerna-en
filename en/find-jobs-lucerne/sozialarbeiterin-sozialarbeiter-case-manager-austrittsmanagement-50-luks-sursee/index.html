The more than 8500 employees are there with cordiality, competence and commitment around the clock for the well-being of patients. The clinics and institutes of the LUKS Group offer medical services of the highest quality. Strengthen our team in Sursee! Due to a job increase, we continue to expand our team.   • You contribute with your positive, open attitude to a humorous team in which cohesion and mutual support are perceptible daily We offer:

  • Attractive conditions, training support and diverse benefits
