Full description

In order to strengthen our team in the Mandate Mall of Switzerland, we are looking for a <b>very flexible</b>, service-oriented and committed personality as of August 2026.

Tasks

As an employee: in Infopoint you are the first contact person for our customers. In addition to representative tasks, services in the field of parking and integration are included in the security equipment:

  • Friendly and competent reception of visitors, as well as their allocation and registration
  • Information to be provided
  • Forward malfunction reports, complaints and order reports to the competent bodies
  • Operating intercom system of the car park
  • Administrative work
  • Monitor parking areas and control barriers
  • Active assistance in the security organisation
  • The rental and sale of various articles

The missions take place on Friday from 15:00 - 21:15. In addition, you are ready and flexible to take on more shifts during short-term absences such as illness or during the holidays of your colleagues.

Requirements

You describe yourself as a service provider: from the innermost conviction with an open and cordial manner. Flexibility, reliability, independence and teamwork are also among your character traits.

  • Commercial apprenticeship, hotel school or equivalent education
  • Professional experience at a reception or in the office with customer contact is advantageous
  • Compellingly very good knowledge of German and English in word and writing, French and Italian is advantageous
  • Communicative and fast-thinking personality
  • Adept use of means of communication and electronic aids
  • Maintained appearance

We specialize in holistic facilities management services for every phase of the business real estate and area lifecycle. Buildings and infrastructures are complex systems. Whether technical operation, strategic planning or infrastructural services – we ensure maximum availability, increase operational safety and improve comfort in buildings. In this way, we minimize costs and create optimal conditions so that our customers can concentrate on their core business.

As a company of Equans Switzerland, the leading full-service provider for holistic building and energy solutions in Switzerland, we are also driving the transformation and digitalization of our customers and working together on the energy transition.
