Full description

We focus on proximity and openness: At the city administration we live the du-culture – already in the application process. Would you like to contribute your constructive and open way of working with children, young people and parents? And with your leadership talent make a contribution to the development of the institution? We look forward to your application.

Your tasks

  • You have comprehensive case responsibility for seven adolescents and young adults, including their living and developmental areas
  • You will continue the respective location discussions.
  • You coach your employees in the residential group in parental cooperation with a systemic focus.
  • You lead, support and qualify in personnel processes.
  • You take over the quality assurance within the offer and work in the supervisory board and in specialist groups.
  • You will lead team meetings, participate in supervision and systemic and psychiatric specialist and case counselling.
  • You accompany prospective social pedagogues during training.

Your profile

  • You have a degree in social education/social work (FH/HF) or a comparable education with professional experience in the stationary sector.
  • You already have a leadership education or are interested in completing one.
  • As a trustworthy person, you meet people in an appreciative way and create relationships that are committed and clear.
  • In conflicts, you act calmly and decisively.
  • You work solution-oriented and also tackle challenging situations with a pinch of humor.
  • You enjoy taking responsibility and enjoy working in both leadership and social education.
  • In addition to office days, you also appreciate irregular working hours (evening, some night and few weekend services).
