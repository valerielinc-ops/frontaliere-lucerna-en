Full description

We focus on proximity and openness: At the city administration we live the du-culture – already in the application process. Are you looking for insights into social work activities? And practical experience to make your career choice easier?

Your tasks

  • Contact point Social Info REX (about 40%): You provide information in a personal interview, by phone and e-mail or triagogue to competent specialist offices. For social assistance requests, you check jurisdiction and subsidiarity.
  • You provide assistance to the users of our public Internet stations.
  • Income management (about 40%): You help with the management of client accounts, create budgets, make checks on social insurance, participate in consultations and manage them partly independently.

Your profile

  • You have an educational qualification at secondary level II (FMS, Matura, EFZ).
  • Ideally, you have already gained initial work experience.
  • You are an open person with an interest in people in different situations.
