  • Main Tasks & Responsibilities

  • Facility Management & Infrastructure: Oversee the maintenance of all plant buildings and technical infrastructure to ensure long-term safety, compliance, and asset longevity.
  • Technical Authority & Lifecycle Management: Act as the final technical sign-off for engineering changes and asset lifecycle plans; lead the long-term CAPEX roadmap for infrastructure upgrades.
  • Financial Accountability: Manage the annual operating budget (OPEX) for Utilities and Maintenance and oversee significant CAPEX budgets for site infrastructure.

  • Innovation & Sustainability: Lead the digitalization of maintenance (e-Maintenance, IoT monitoring) and champion sustainable energy projects to reduce the site's carbon footprint.
