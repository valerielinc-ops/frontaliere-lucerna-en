Full description

As a leading Swiss construction and real estate service provider, Implenia develops, realizes and manages living spaces, working environments and infrastructure for future generations in Switzerland and Germany. Implenia also offers tunnelling and related infrastructure projects in other markets. The company combines the know-how of highly qualified consulting, development, planning and execution units under one roof into an integrated, multinationally leading construction and real estate service provider. Headquartered in Opfikon near Zurich, Implenia employs more than 8,000 people (FTE) across Europe and achieved a turnover of CHF 3.5 billion in 2025. More information at implenia.com

We are looking for a project manager: in execution GU/TU for our office in Gisikon. With us, you can expect exciting projects ranging from business and residential buildings to wooden high-rise buildings to industrial buildings and we offer you a modern working environment. In addition, lean principles as well as BIM are not only theory for us, but are implemented comprehensively and consistently in our projects. 20 years after its founding, Implenia is now optimally positioned and ready for a successful future.

You move that

  • As a project manager: in GU/TU you achieve the economic project goals of new construction projects in compliance with the specified quality, deadlines and costs from the tender to the commissioning.
  • You manage your projects operationally and under your own responsibility, taking into account the safety, timing, qualitative and quantitative requirements
  • You work with local subcontractors, suppliers, planners and authorities
  • In your role as project manager: you are the contact person for builders, business partners and tenants
  • You coordinate the specialist planners in your project and ensure the overall coordination of the planning
  • In addition, you are responsible for the further education of the construction managers: inside your team
  • You maintain an open and respectful communication culture as well as a clean documentation and report the current status of the projects to your team leader:

This is why you succeed

  • Completed training as project manager:in, technician:in TS, architect:in or civil engineer:in
  • 2 - 5 years of experience as project manager: in new construction or conversion projects
  • Independent management of construction sites with high complexity
  • Technical guidance of up to five construction managers: inside
  • You have very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Entrepreneurial and purposeful thinking
  • Enforcement and high social skills
  • Familiarity with general contractor environment
