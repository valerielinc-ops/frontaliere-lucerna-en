Put to the Point

Are you enthusiastic about aviation, technology and the challenges of challenging test flight operations? Then you can expect a versatile and exciting job at the interface between test pilots, engineering, maintenance and project teams, right at the pulse of Swiss military aviation.

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-21063
  • Location: Rüeggisingerstrasse 165, 6032 Emmen

You can make this contribution

  • Aircraft mechanic or aircraft avionician for provision and operation in armasuisse test flight operations with military and civilian, mostly non-certified aviation systems (fighter aircraft, helicopters, turboprop and drones)
  • Participation in initial training on the F-35A in the USA, with appropriate preparation and training to achieve the necessary qualifications
  • After completion of training on specialist, test and release activities on combat aircraft, helicopters and turboprop aircraft in the military and civilian sectors (EASA Part-66)
  • Location and repair of mechanical and electromechanical faults and maintenance and repair work on the flight line of armasuisse test flight operations
  • Perform test installations, functional tests and configuration changes on all test aeronautical systems and test systems
  • Practical verification of instructions, checklists and manuals for flight test operations of non-approved aeroplane configurations

This makes you unique

  • Basic technical training EFZ mechanical or electronics
  • Military qualification as an aircraft mechanic or avionician on MT2 or MT3 on combat aircraft or helicopters or Aircraft Maintenance Licence (AML) under EASA Part-66, category B1.x or B2 advantageous
  • Readiness for several months assignments as part of training and test campaigns outside Emmen
  • Strong user knowledge of the M365 range as well as knowledge or learning pleasure of SAP and the electronic business administration GEVER (ActaNova) of the federal government
  • Motivation and willingness to actively participate in test flight operations for many years
  • You have oral and written knowledge of an official language. Good English skills are required
