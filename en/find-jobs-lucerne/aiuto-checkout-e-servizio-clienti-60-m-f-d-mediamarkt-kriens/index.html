Full description

Your tasks

  • You provide close customer support regarding online pickups, returns, changes and repairs
  • You serve the cash register skillfully and efficiently
  • You will find pragmatic solutions for customer complaints
  • You are responsible for selling guarantees
  • You can ensure a flawless customer experience thanks to your in-depth product and service knowledge
  • At customer events, you transform customers into fans and increase customer loyalty
  • You provide with your drive for compliance with guidelines, policies and processes
  • You guarantee in case of difficulties a proper tracking and timely information to the customer
  • You coordinate deliveries to our customers in cooperation with internal and external partners

Your skills and talents

  • You have completed professional training, ideally in the retail trade
  • You already have several years of professional experience in a similar position or in sales
  • You distinguish yourself through your service willingness and solution orientation
  • You are interested in our technical products and related services
  • You can approach people proactively and you meet them at any time with a positive and respectful attitude
  • You bring a feeling for people and you manage to pick them up, calm them down and create a positive experience.

About us

At MediaMarkt, we believe that work and fun go hand in hand. That’s why you can expect a great team spirit, a varied working environment and the latest electronics. We believe that you are best when you can be yourself. We value diversity and therefore welcome all applications - regardless of gender, origin, religion / worldview, disability, age as well as sexual orientation and identity.

In our market, we offer our customers a huge selection of electrical products and a unique shopping experience. Here, at our 44 locations, we contribute every day to making Media Markt what it is: The number one electrical specialist market in Europe.

Ready to grow together? Let's go!
