Full description

Our heart beats for Swiss gastronomy. With a lot of commitment, we are there for our customers. In our 31 pick-up markets, by field service and delivery, as well as in the digital world. With a versatile range, attractive own brands, professional specialist advice and innovative services.

Transgourmet Schweiz AG is part of the international Transgourmet Group. The company is a leader in the Swiss supply and pick-up wholesale trade and has around 2,100 employees and 100,000 customers (restaurants, hotels, canteens, homes and hospitals as well as retailers).

Tasks

  • You record telephone and written customer orders in the ERP system
  • You process quality complaints and clarify them internally and inform customers about the current delivery status
  • You support our demanding customers on the phone or by written medium
  • You will support the webshop customers and advise them on the self-service possibilities of Transgourmet.
  • You support the internal customers according to the service catalogue and processes.
  • You recognize customer needs, capture them and forward them

Requirements

  • You have completed a commercial education, an education in customer dialogue or an equivalent education
  • You bring your first professional experience in a comparable function – ideally in the food and/or catering sector
  • You are well versed in dealing with IT tools as well as digital products and systems (e.g. webshop and apps)
  • German is your mother tongue and you have very good knowledge of Italian and/or French (level C1)
  • You have excellent written as well as oral communication skills and formulate stylish, solution-oriented and error-free
  • You have high social skills and work carefully and accurately
  • You have healthy assertiveness and a strong resilience
  • You are flexible. Our accessibility is from Monday to Friday from 07:00 to 17:30 and on Saturday from 07:00 to 12:00.
