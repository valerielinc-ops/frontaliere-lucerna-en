Full description

Two locations, many encounters and an everyday life that promises variety: In Lucerne and Emmen you are in the middle of it instead of just there. With your work behind the scenes, you create an environment in which our employees can continue to write Emmi history together. You like to get started, like contact with people and appreciate making a difference in different places? Then bring us with you – with your skills, your ideas and your joy of togetherness.

You can do that.

  • With your craftsmanship, you can do minor maintenance and repair work and ensure that buildings, facilities and furniture work reliably.
  • You keep an eye on building and safety facilities, carry out control tours and support in case of alarms or emergencies.
  • You coordinate external maintenance and repair work independently, compare offers and control the professional execution.
  • For a smooth working day, you take care of workplaces, meeting rooms, consumables and the mail inlet and outlet at the Lucerne location.
  • With an eye for cleanliness and order, you support with maintenance and cleaning work and contribute to well-kept interior and exterior areas.
  • In Emmen, you bring additional variety into your everyday life and support the area service with forklift work, maintenance, winter service and as a holiday replacement in the postal courier service.

Why do you succeed?

  • An apprenticeship in company maintenance, in a craft profession or comparable practical experience gives you a good basis for this versatile task.
  • Your technical understanding helps you to operate building facilities, detect irregularities and initiate appropriate measures.
  • Self-planning, working carefully and setting priorities is yours. Even with different orders, you keep an overview and accompany external service providers reliably.
  • With your helpful nature, you respond to concerns and find practical solutions. Collaboration in a team gives you just as much pleasure as contact with employees and external partners.
  • Safety, cleanliness and careful use of materials and resources are important to you. You like to work practically, even outdoors, and bring the readiness for missions in Lucerne and Emmen.
  • Good knowledge of German and a safe use of digital work equipment make everyday life easier for you. A category B driver’s license and a forklift pass ideally complement your profile. You can do the following working hours: In Emmen daily Monday to Friday 05.00 to 09.00 / After deployment in Emmen Drive by car to Lucerne / In Lucerne 3 days Monday / Wednesday / Thursday from. 09.30 a.m. to 14.00

This is what we offer you

  • 5 weeks of vacation: Enjoy five weeks of vacation per year. From the age of 46, your holiday entitlement gradually increases to up to six weeks.
  • Attractive pension fund with flexible savings plans: Our Emmi pension foundation pays interest on your retirement balance above the legal minimum. Your contribution is based on your annual salary – without coordination deduction. Choose between standard or plus savings plan with 1% higher savings contributions.
  • Voluntary Success Sharing: Depending on the business success, you will receive a voluntary success Sharing. In doing so, we want to acknowledge your valuable contribution to the company’s success.
  • Attractive surcharges & additional services: You receive 20% time allowance from 8 pm, paid warm-up times, a time credit for changing times and a fair compensation for piquetting outside your working hours.
  • Cheap meals in the staff restaurant: For breakfast, lunch and break meals, a staff restaurant with discounted employee prices is available.
  • Discounts: Benefit from up to 20% discount on Emmi products, attractive leisure and travel discounts, special offers at top brands and fleet discounts when buying a car.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Lucerne?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Lucerne's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Lucerne's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
