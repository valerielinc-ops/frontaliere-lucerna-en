Full description

Freshness from Emmen – here is heart blood in it 💙 At Emmen, everything revolves around freshness, quality and passion for our products. The fresh produce department produces yoghurts and desserts every day, which provide enjoyment throughout Switzerland. At our state-of-the-art plants, several hundred tons of the finest fresh products are bottled every week – with precision, team spirit and joy in technology. Here you will meet people who tackle, think and are proud to be part of Emmi. If you enjoy state-of-the-art production and like to take on responsibility, you are a perfect fit for us!

You can do that.

  • You prepare the plant for production and ensure a smooth start.
  • During production, you keep an eye on the bottling plant and ensure a uniform process.
  • The further packaging of the products up to the handover to the logistics is your responsibility.
  • In the case of minor disruptions, you intervene yourself and bring in ideas to improve the processes.
  • You pay close attention to our high quality, hygiene and safety standards.
  • As a team, you actively help and help ensure that everything works hand in hand.

You bring that with you

  • You bring an education in the food sector or you have experience as a machine operator:in.
  • You also enjoy modern facilities and a good technical understanding.
  • Your reliable, responsible and careful way of working distinguishes you.
  • Team spirit, openness and willingness to tackle describe you well.
  • You have a high hygiene awareness and sense of quality.
  • With your readiness to work in shift operation (early, late and night shift, latest shift until Saturday morning 5:00 o’clock) you show your flexibility and you are in possession of a driving license and own vehicle.

This is what we offer you

  • 5 weeks of vacation: Enjoy five weeks of vacation per year. From the age of 46, your holiday entitlement gradually increases to up to six weeks.
  • Voluntary Success Sharing: Depending on the business success, you will receive a voluntary success Sharing. In doing so, we want to acknowledge your valuable contribution to the company’s success.
  • Attractive pension fund with flexible savings plans: Our Emmi pension foundation pays interest on your retirement balance above the legal minimum. Your contribution is based on your annual salary – without coordination deduction. Choose between standard or plus savings plan with 1% higher savings contributions.
  • Individual development plan: With tailor-made development plans, we specifically promote your individual skills and support your long-term growth – a central component of our sustainability strategy. Learn more .
  • Focus on your health: We offer occupational health management, preventive measures and free social advice from our partner Proitera for professional and private concerns.
  • Discounts: Benefit from up to 20% discount on Emmi products, attractive leisure and travel discounts, special offers at top brands and fleet discounts when buying a car.
