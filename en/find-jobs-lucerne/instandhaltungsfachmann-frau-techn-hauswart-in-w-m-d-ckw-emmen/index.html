Full description

Pensum: 100%

You ensure that everything runs smoothly at the Rathausen location – both technically and organisationally. At Axpo Services AG, you design a reliable infrastructure for the energy future with us.

Tasks & Responsibilities

  • You will operate and monitor the technical building infrastructure as well as safety-relevant facilities.
  • You carry out maintenance and maintenance work on technical and infrastructural plants and support them.
  • You document maintenance work, tests and controls in the CAFM system (ICFM Campos).
  • You coordinate and monitor external specialist companies for service, maintenance and repair work.
  • You participate in technical projects and structural adjustments at the location.
  • You will be in regular contact with the team manager in-house service and, if necessary, forward more complex topics to the site manager Rathausen.

Profile and Requirements

  • You have a basic technical training in the building sector (e.g. as an electrical installer, heating or sanitary installer) – ideally with a federal contract. Professional certificate as a maintenance specialist or the willingness to train you accordingly.
  • You have experience in the management of administrative and operational buildings as well as with HLKKSE systems.
  • You have a truck test and ideally a connection permit according to type. 15 NIV.
  • You are confident with the common office products and are interested in technical connections, energy and the environment.
  • You work independently, service-oriented and are ready for extraordinary assignments and pikettdienst in rotation.
  • Even if you do not bring all the points – we look forward to your application.
