Application process

  • You are responsible for running and configuring existing medical applications.
  • You cover the support for the solutions you supervise in the 2nd and partially 3rd level.
  • The service concept is your priority and you recognize the added value of these support services.
  • You create and manage the documentation within your task area.
  • When analyzing clinical requirements as well as developing and evaluating solution proposals and concepts, you can use your skills profitably.
  • You implement new requirements independently with the supplier, test them, plan and regularly carry out maintenance windows, changes and release changes.
  • You can imagine taking over the project management in small projects. Requirements
