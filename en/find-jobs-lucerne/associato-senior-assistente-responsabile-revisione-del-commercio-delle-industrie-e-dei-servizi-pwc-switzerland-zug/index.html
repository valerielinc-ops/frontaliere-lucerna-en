Your impact - Your chance to make a difference

  • You audit annual and consolidated financial statements for national and international groups and companies in the industrial, trading and services sectors.
  • You audit business processes and provide support with questions concerning financial reporting under various standards (OR, IFRS, Swiss GAAP FER, US GAAP).
  • Together with the Engagement Manager, you work on the planning, execution and finalisation of various audit engagements and special audits, while being in direct contact with other subject-matter specialists.
  • You are part of the digital revolution in auditing by using our digital audit tools and approaches and helping to improve them.
  • You are interested in gaining in-depth knowledge of a broad range of clients and building and maintaining client relationships.
  • We support your professional and personal development through on- and off-the-job coaching so that you can achieve your long-term career goals.
  • As part of an inspiring, diverse team, you are part of an international network and receive regular feedback from colleagues and supervisors.

Your skill set - What you bring

  • You are completing the training to become a certified auditor or have just completed it.
  • You have at least two years of professional experience in auditing and have gained insight into business audits of companies.
  • You have strong analytical skills, high quality standards for yourself and the team, and want to actively drive digitalisation in auditing.
  • You are motivated and help develop solutions that exceed our clients' expectations.
  • You are ready to take on responsibility from the outset and work in an environment with an active coaching culture.
  • You have very good German and English skills, both written and spoken.
