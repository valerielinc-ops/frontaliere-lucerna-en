Full description

Our rehabilitation clinic Sonnmatt in Lucerne combines medical rehabilitation in the fields of neurology and musculoskeletal with a first-class hotel environment in the most beautiful viewing position. Our patients benefit both inpatient and outpatient from personal, holistic care by an experienced, interdisciplinary team – with maximum comfort and high therapeutic quality.

Your tasks

  • Hospitation (about 30%): You are in the individual therapy areas occupational therapy, physiotherapy, movement therapy, speech therapy (walking and supporting in individual therapies depending on interest)
  • Therapy (about 30%): You carry out therapeutic activities (e.g. application of physical measures, support for self-training, support for therapists in group therapies)
  • Other activities (about 40 %): carrying out domestic and administrative activities (e.g. cleaning materials and equipment, laundry organisation, postal mail)

Your profile

  • Interests: You are interested in therapeutic professions (ideally you are looking for a pre-internship/social internship for the appropriate therapeutic training)
  • Personality: social skills, interest, good self-organization

We offer you

  • Co-creation: Work in a quiet work environment and actively participate
  • Insights: First basics and insights into various therapeutic professions
  • Inspiring and collegial work climate: Work in a supportive and appreciative team that works together for the best possible patient care
  • Above-average social security benefits: Benefit from attractive social security benefits and comprehensive benefits
  • Education and training: generous share of costs in education and training
  • Discounts: Use discounts from various partners, such as wholesalers, pharmacies and our membership of Benefit@work
  • Free admission: Enjoy free admission and other benefits in the FORTYSEVEN thermal bath
