Full description

Become part of something bigger. Work with more than 2000 specialists from more than 100 professions and enable paraplegic people to live a self-determined life. On the modern campus in Nottwil, an inspiring atmosphere and interdisciplinary cooperation at eye level awaits you.

Tasks

The Swiss Paraplegic Center has 3 highly specialized operating rooms equipped with modern technology with a focus on spinal surgery, orthopaedics, neurourology, tetrahand surgery, plastic surgery, visceral surgery and ENT for plegic and nonplegic patients.

  • As a student of anesthesia care NDS HF, you carry out anesthesia independently with the help of your pronounced professional knowledge, your technical skills and your high social competence under the delegation of a specialist anesthesia and are responsible for the intensive, continuous perioperative monitoring of patients within the framework of your competences.

Requirements

  • Degree as a Dipl. Nursing specialist HF, recognised diploma in nursing DN 2 or AKP
  • For foreign nursing diplomas: existing SRC recognition
  • Mastery of the German language in word and writing, level C1
  • Knowledge of French and Italian is an advantage
  • At least 2 years of professional experience in acute care
  • sound nursing and medical basic knowledge
  • Decision and responsiveness in rapidly changing medical situations
  • Resilience, flexibility and a high degree of responsibility
  • High social skills in caring for people in extreme situations
  • High degree of willingness to learn and self-initiative
  • You have the desire to professionalize your knowledge and skills with a postgraduate degree.

We offer

You not only expect attractive employment conditions, but also numerous other advantages. Look forward to meaningful work in a modern working environment, flexible working hours and a wide range of further education opportunities. Come and experience our unique spirit. More information on parplegie.ch/benefits.

The Swiss Paraplegic Centre in Nottwil is a private, nationally and internationally recognized specialist clinic for cross-sectional, back and respiratory medicine. As an acute and rehabilitation clinic, the centre offers its patients comprehensive care and supports them throughout their lives. The clinic is part of the Swiss Paraplegic Group, which provides a globally unique service network for the holistic rehabilitation of people with paraplegia.
