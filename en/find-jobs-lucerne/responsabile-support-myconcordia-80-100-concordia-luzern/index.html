This is what awaits you

You bring that with you

  • You have a commercial training or other professional experience in customer support and have already gained experience in team leadership.
  • We presuppose a stylish oral and written expression in German and French. Knowledge of Italian and English is another plus.
  • The use of digital means is easy for you and makes you fun.
  • A strong service orientation is a matter of course for you.
  • You think in a networked and solution-oriented way and can identify connections between customer concerns, internal processes and technical developments.
  • Teamwork, communication skills and resilience round off your profile.
