Full description

You want to know how millions of liters of milk are used to produce high-quality products every day – and make an important contribution yourself? In this exciting lesson, you will take on responsibility for the most modern production and packaging plants. You ensure that every product ends up on the shelf just as our customers love it – clean, safe and in perfect quality. As part of our team, you will become a professional in dealing with technology. You will learn how to optimally adjust machines and how important hygiene is in food production. A profession with responsibility, variety and future – and with the best fragrance: fresh dairy products made by Emmi

You can do that.

  • You will reliably serve and monitor our state-of-the-art production and packaging facilities.
  • After each product change, you will find the right settings and adjust the system accordingly.
  • During your training, you will acquire comprehensive knowledge of materials science, productivity, quality control, error analysis and hygiene regulations.
  • You analyze disruptions, recognize optimization potential and support improvements in the process.
  • With your commitment, you ensure that our products leave the factory on time, safely and in the best quality.

You bring that with you

  • You have a real or secondary degree.
  • Technology fascinates you and you have a good craftsmanship.
  • Care and cleanliness are a matter of course for you to work – especially with food.
  • You like to work in the team 🧩 and can organize well.
  • With a good feeling for machines and processes, you will quickly find your place in production.
  • Your German language skills are at least level B1.

This is what we offer you

  • Extra wage & voluntary participation in success: Look forward to a 13. Monthly wages and participation in the company’s success.
  • 6 weeks holidays: More time for relaxation, travel and your hobbies.
  • Top future prospects: 80% of our learners receive a fixed employment contract at Emmi after their training.
  • Costs for more focus on your teaching: We cover the costs for schoolbooks, SBB Half-Tax subscription, workwear and support you with CHF 400 for your school laptop.
  • Fun & team spirit: Every year you look forward to an unforgettable trip to the Europapark. In addition, we have a common introduction day and a common camp for learners at the end of the first year of apprenticeship.
  • Free Emmi products: Enjoy our delicious Emmi products during work – and also after work. Thanks to up to 20% discount in our stores, you never have to give up your favorites.
