What you do

  • You will be responsible for the product vision, strategy and roadmap of Suva’s central AI agent platform and develop it further together with the relevant stakeholders.

  • You are interested in modern AI technologies, large language models and agentic AI and want to actively shape their productive use in the corporate environment.
