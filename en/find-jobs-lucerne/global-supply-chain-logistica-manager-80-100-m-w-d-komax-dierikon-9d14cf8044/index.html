Full description

The Komax group is a pioneer and market leader in the field of automated wire processing. As a globally active Swiss company, we develop and manufacture serial production machines, customer-specific systems, quality assurance modules, test systems, networking solutions, and services for various industries. The Komax group has more than 50 companies with 3500 employees across five continents and a worldwide sales and service network.

Your responsibilities

  • You develop and implement the global supply chain strategy and governance framework across the Komax Group.
  • You design and optimize our global logistics, warehousing and distribution network, balancing service levels, lead times, costs, working capital and supply risks.
  • You define Group-wide stocking principles for production and service parts and drive inventory optimization while safeguarding customer availability and on-time delivery.
  • You establish global standards for warehousing, material flows, order fulfilment, packaging, transportation, consignment stock and Vendor Managed Inventory.
  • You create end-to-end supply chain transparency through harmonized KPIs, reliable data and digital solutions, and further develop our global Sales and Operations Planning.
  • You lead strategic supply chain projects and optimize international intercompany flows (e.g., Asia-to-Europe/Global) – ensuring trade-compliant concepts in close collaboration with Operations, Service, Sales, Legal, Finance, Procurement, IT, and our Market Organizations.

Your profile

  • You hold a degree in Supply Chain Management, Logistics, Industrial Engineering, Business Administration or a comparable field.
  • You bring a minimum of 5-10 years of international experience in supply chain management, logistics or operations, ideally within machinery, automation or industrial manufacturing.
  • You have proven expertise in logistics network design, warehousing, inventory optimization and service-parts management across multiple countries or legal entities.
  • You are experienced in Sales and Operations Planning (S&OP), demand planning, intercompany flows, and international trade/customs concepts; formal leadership experience is an advantage.
  • You combine strong analytical capabilities with sound knowledge of supply chain KPIs, ERP and business intelligence solutions and the ability to translate complex data into clear business decisions.
  • You are a strategic, pragmatic, and hands-on personality with strong stakeholder management, intercultural competence, and the ability to drive change across hierarchical levels without direct line authority.
  • You communicate fluently in English (German or other languages are an advantage) and are willing to travel internationally approximately 30%.

Your benefits

You’ll take over a key role with real impact, international exposure and plenty of room to shape Group Supply Chain Management. You will drive strategic initiatives, collaborate across Komax and actively contribute to the development of our global Supply Chain Management. Your new workplace will be either in Dierikon (Switzerland) or another of the mentioned locations, depending on your place of residence and work.
