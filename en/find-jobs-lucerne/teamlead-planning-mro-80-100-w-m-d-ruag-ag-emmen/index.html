Full description

Every day, around 3,000 employees of RUAG and RUAG Real Estate make a significant contribution to the security of Switzerland. They ensure that the Swiss Army and other operational and security organizations can perform their tasks comprehensively at any time.

You can move that

  • You lead and coordinate the AVOR/PPS team and are responsible for production planning and control in the MRO environment.
  • You plan capacities and resources, create and monitor production and maintenance contracts and ensure timely material availability.
  • You coordinate cooperation with purchasing, production, logistics and sales and keep an eye on delivery dates, stocks and lead times.
  • You ensure the quality of master data, parts lists and work plans and continuously optimize planning and production processes.
  • You identify potential for improvement and drive lean management, digitization and automation as well as the further development of ERP/PPS systems.
  • You analyze relevant key figures, create reports and derive suitable measures from them.

You bring that with you

  • You have a basic technical or commercial training with corresponding further training, for example as a process specialist or dipl. Technician/RF.
  • You bring several years of experience in AVOR, PPS or production planning, ideally in the MRO environment, and are familiar with capacity planning, scheduling and maintenance processes.
  • You have experience with ERP/PPS systems, preferably SAP (PM, PP, MM), as well as good knowledge of materials management, master data, parts lists and work plans.
  • You have leadership experience and ideally knowledge of lean management, CIP and optimization. Automation of planning processes.
  • You communicate clearly and solution-oriented, set priorities securely and convince through assertiveness, organizational talent and decision-making.
  • You like to work across departments, take on responsibility and keep an overview even in demanding situations.
  • You have very good knowledge of German
  • Knowledge of English is an advantage.

Do you not meet all the requirements one hundred percent? Apply anyway. We are always looking for talented and motivated people who want to enrich our team. We attach great importance to diversity and equal opportunities and our goal is to build a diverse team in which everyone can fully develop their personal strengths. We particularly encourage all individuals, regardless of gender, who may have concerns about accepting a job in an army-related environment to apply.
