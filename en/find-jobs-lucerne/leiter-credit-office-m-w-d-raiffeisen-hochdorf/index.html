Full description

For <b> Raiffeisenbank Oberseetal,</b> a successful and locally oriented bank with offices in Hochdorf, Eschenbach and Inwil, we are looking for a personality as Head of Credit Office (m/f/d) on the basis of a reorganisation.

Tasks

  • Professional and operational management of credit processing
  • Independent analysis and risk- and solution-oriented assessment of loan applications (private and small and medium-sized corporate customers, real estate or corporate financing)
  • Approval of credit applications in-house or preparation of the basis for the decision and recommendation to the competent authority
  • Intensive cooperation with the customer consultants, in particular advice on credit and system technical as well as regulatory issues
  • Management of credit positions, collection and preparation of property valuations by means of bank-owned valuation tools, data collection for balance sheet analysis, construction credit control and management of mortgage master data (quality management)

Requirements

  • Completed studies, preferably in business administration or finance (FH, university or comparable education)
  • Several years of professional experience in customer consulting, in the credit office and/or in bank audit
  • Designated credit assessment, credit processing and credit monitoring practices
  • Performance-ready, reliable and communicative personality with strong decision-making and teamwork skills
  • Independent, structured working method and networked, solution-oriented action
  • Enjoyment in the real estate sector and willingness for personal and professional development
