Full description

As one of the world’s leading clinics, we develop an individualized treatment program with the aim of providing our patients with the highest possible degree of independence and freedom of life, thereby optimizing their quality of life. We address patients from the Swiss cash system as well as international patients. cereneo and its sister companies support and accompany patients in their various phases after a neurological disease, from inpatient or outpatient treatment to telerehabilitation via digital channels.

Your chance: rethinking rehabilitation together! Are you looking for an exciting challenge in which you can contribute your expertise and at the same time actively contribute to the further development of modern nursing and rehabilitation approaches? At cereneo, you have the opportunity to help shape the future of neurological rehabilitation in an interdisciplinary team of doctors, therapists, researchers and nurses – while sustainably improving the quality of life of our patients.

Your tasks

  • Holistic care: You are responsible for the professional care and care of our inpatients during their neurological rehabilitation.
  • Teamwork: You work closely in a team of two with a dipl. Caregiver and can contribute your individual strengths.
  • Promotion and prevention: You carry out targeted development-promoting and health-preserving measures to actively support the recovery process of our patients.
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation: You actively seek the professional exchange with colleagues and contribute to the optimization of our care and treatment processes through your ideas.

What should you bring? Our success is only possible with highly qualified and motivated employees: women at all levels. Openness, initiative and fairness are therefore at the top of our list.

  • Training as a specialist Health EFZ
  • Positive attitude to working with people with neurological diseases of all ages
  • Willingness & interest in working together in an interprofessional team
  • Readiness for continuous training
  • Good German & English skills, every other language is advantageous
  • Professional interaction with colleagues and external partners & customers
  • Empathy and rolling distance
  • Confidentiality and reliability
  • Initiative, communication pleasure and team spirit
  • Criticism and conflict

We offer

  • Modern infrastructure: Perfectly located working environment with a view of Lake Vierwaldstättersee, Pilatus and Rigi.

Our corporate culture

  • EXCELLENCE: Using the latest research findings and first-class service
  • HEARTH: We are always there with all our heart. Of course, this also includes the sincere treatment of our patients: inside, but also between our staff
  • MOTIVATION: Joint goal achievement and support of patients: women to improve their quality of life
  • TEAM WORK: Reliability and mutual support in the team
  • INNOVATION: Research and testing of new therapeutic approaches and devices

If you are an open, committed person who likes to work in an interdisciplinary team and continuously develop, we look forward to your application. Become part of our team and actively shape the future of neurological rehabilitation.
