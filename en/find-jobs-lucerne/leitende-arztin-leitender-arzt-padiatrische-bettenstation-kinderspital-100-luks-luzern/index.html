Full description

The Children's Hospital Central Switzerland of the Lucerne Cantonal Hospital (LUKS) is one of the largest children's hospitals in Switzerland and serves the entire region of Central Switzerland at the highest level. Our employees work around the clock to ensure that children, young people and their relatives are measurably better off. LUKS, with its locations in Lucerne, Sursee and Wolhusen, is a company of the LUKS Group and thus belongs to one of the leading hospital groups in Switzerland. >For our team we are looking for reinforcement as of 1 January 2027 or by agreement.

Tasks

  • In this responsible position, you take over the professional and organizational management of our pediatric department. We serve patients from the entire spectrum of infectious and non-infectious diseases in childhood and adolescence in the catchment area of Central Switzerland.
  • They are directly involved in patient care on the ward and actively participate in the general pediatric cadre service.
  • They lead the senior physicians of inpatient pediatrics and assume responsibility for the continuous development of inpatient care, especially in the areas of lean hospital and digital transformation.
  • LUKS is a university teaching and research hospital. In this sense, they are actively involved in education, further education and training in pediatrics and teach within the framework of the Joint Medical Master (thematic block pediatrics).

Requirements

  • You have a specialist title in paediatrics and adolescent medicine as well as several years of experience as a senior physician or senior physician of general pediatrics.
  • You are a clinically oriented professional with in-depth professional experience at a center hospital or university clinic.
  • As a leader, you are characterized by high social and communication skills. Assuming responsibility, reliability and integrity are among your strengths.

We offer

  • You can expect a diverse and challenging field of work in the entire field of inpatient pediatrics, embedded in an interdisciplinary and interprofessional environment.
  • Your commitment and ideas actively shape the further development of inpatient paediatrics at the Children's Hospital Central Switzerland (KidZ). In addition, you will shape the future-oriented processes for the new construction of the Children's Hospital and Women's Hospital, which will be opened in autumn 2026.
  • At Kinderspital we cultivate a culture that consciously lives the respectful, passionate and affectionate interaction with each other and with children and parents. If you also live this culture, you are exactly right with us.
  • We offer you attractive employment conditions with a long-term perspective as well as a modern workplace in the new Kinderspital Zentralschweiz.
