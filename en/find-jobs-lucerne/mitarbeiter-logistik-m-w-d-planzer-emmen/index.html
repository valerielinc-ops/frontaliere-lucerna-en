Full description

Whether small or large, simple or complex – we transport and store goods. With motivated employees and modern vehicles, we at Emmen serve not only the region, but also the rearmost corners of Switzerland. Become part of our team and shape the future of logistics!

What you move

  • You pick items accurately and ensure that customer orders are processed on the same day
  • Storage, retrieval, packaging and labelling are done reliably according to specifications
  • Inventory? There you go and take care of the perfect overview
  • You use the storage system and resources safely and efficiently
  • You mainly work on Monday and Tuesday and support us occasionally also on Saturday assignments

Why do you succeed?

  • You have completed vocational training as a logistician EFZ or bring professional experience in logistics
  • You ideally have a forklift card in your pocket – if not, you are motivated to complete it at a later date
  • Oral and written you can certainly express yourself in German
  • Reliability, accuracy and rapid adaptability to new challenges set you apart
  • Even if it gets stressful, you stay cool and keep track.

Your Benefits

  • Attractive holiday regulations: With us you start with 5 weeks holidays and even benefit from 6 weeks from the 5th year of service!
  • Further education and development opportunities: With us you can develop yourself - we promote and challenge talents
  • Central introductory training: You will spend your first working days in the central introduction, where you will be taught the most important basics
  • Free parking: Get ready for parking - with us you can park for free

Who we are For us as a company, the focus is on a supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
