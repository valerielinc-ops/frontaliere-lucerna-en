Description

For our client in Central Switzerland, we are looking for an immediately available or by-agreement trained blinds installer (m/f) or someone with equivalent training, such as a metalworker or mechanic

Your responsibilities

  • Installation and dismantling of louvered blinds and awnings, roller shutters and folding shutters
  • Service work on existing shading systems
  • General installation and repair work throughout Switzerland

Your qualifications

  • Completed vocational apprenticeship as a blinds installer or equivalent training
  • Ability to work independently
  • Good command of German
  • Resilient, flexible and team-oriented
  • Well-groomed and customer-oriented demeanor
  • Category B driver's license
