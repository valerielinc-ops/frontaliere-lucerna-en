Full description

Lucerne Psychiatry AG is a teaching hospital of the University of Lucerne and is represented with its offers at various locations in the Canton of Lucerne and in Sarnen, Canton of Obwalden. More than 1,600 employees from a wide range of professional groups and departments are humanely and competently committed to the well-being of patients. The offers are aimed at children, adolescents and adults up to old age. Care expert APN (m/f/d) 80 %

Their tasks

  • Its field of application comprises five acute care units: 3 general psychiatric units in Lucerne and 2 in Sarnen, with a new treatment concept (integrated care in all settings from stationary to hometreatment with "Weddinger model" and "Open Dialogue").
  • Work in direct clinical practice in collaboration with social networks
  • Counselling and coaching of treatment teams
  • Further development of interdisciplinary treatment concepts involving the target groups
  • Design, implementation and evaluation of workshops
  • Management and cooperation in internal and regional projects
  • Professional leadership and cooperation with the specialist care representatives as well as within the care expert team
  • Participation in specialist networks and participation in congresses
  • Student internships (Bachelor/Master)

Your profile

  • Master's degree in nursing science or willingness to complete it
  • Professional experience as a nurse in the psychiatric environment
  • Readiness to complete the further education "Open Dialogue"
  • Strong initiative and self-organisation
  • Trialogical thinking and approach
  • Willingness to innovate and enjoy developing your own APN role and driving change processes
  • Experience in Project Management

Benefits

  • High design scope
  • Mutual learning in the care team
  • Further education contract for master’s studies and further education Open Dialogue
  • Careful introduction to the versatile field of activity
