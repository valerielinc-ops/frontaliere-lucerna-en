Full description

cereneo is a Swiss clinic specializing in neurorehabilitation, offering innovative and personalized treatments for patients recovering from stroke, living with Parkinson's disease, traumatic brain injuries, and other neurological conditions. At cereneo rehabilitation clinics, we create individualized treatment programs aimed at restoring the highest level of independence and enhancing our patients' quality of life. We are currently seeking a Physiotherapist or Occupational Therapist for our international projects, who is passionate about working in this field. You will begin your journey in Switzerland with training and become part of an interdisciplinary team. After a training period of 6 to 12 months, you will define your path to stay in Switzerland, move to Dubai.

Your new Role

As part of an interdisciplinary team, you will bring your expertise and knowledge into the rehabilitation routine. You will assist the client's recovery through individually tailored therapy concepts. This involves being receptive and unbiased towards the diverse needs of the client and being willing to work in an international interdisciplinary treatment team. Your responsibilities will include independently conducting individual therapies, creating personalized therapy and training programs, and selecting suitable aids according to the patient's needs.

Your Profile

Our success is based on highly qualified and motivated staff at all levels. Therefore, we value openness, initiative, and flexibility. Ideally, you have:

  • A degree in physiotherapy or occupational therapy
  • Professional experience in neurorehabilitation or a related field
  • Interest in interdisciplinary collaboration in an international environment
  • Openness and empathy
  • Willingness to work in Saudi Arabia and adapt to new cultural circumstances
  • Very good English language skills; knowledge of Arabic is advantageous

What we offer

We offer a modern and professional working environment, attractive employment conditions, and a wide range of opportunities for both personal and professional development:

  • A challenging and diverse role in an international setting
  • The opportunity to significantly improve our patients' quality of life
  • Comprehensive support for onboarding and integration on-site

Our Culture – What Unites Us Excellence, Compassion, Motivation, Teamwork and Innovation are more than just values to us. We combine the latest scientific research with first-class service, treat our patients and one another with genuine care and respect, and work together to develop new approaches in neurological rehabilitation. If you are an open-minded and committed professional who enjoys working in an interdisciplinary environment and is eager to continuously develop your skills, we look forward to getting to know you. Join our team and help us shape the future of neurological rehabilitation.
