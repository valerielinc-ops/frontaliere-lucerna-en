Full description

The Catering Services Migros Lucerne is a leading caterer in Central Switzerland and organizes major events such as sports events, company events and weddings. Since May 2024, the Viscose Event Hall has been part of it. As a sous chef, you lead the kitchen and events, ensure a smooth process and convince with fresh dishes. In doing so, you develop further to / the chef.

Mansioni

  • Creation and calculation of menus – tailored to customer requirements and event-specific requirements
  • Orders and management of goods
  • Planning, organisation and management of production
  • Operational leadership of the kitchen team at events
  • Ensuring efficiency, quality and efficiency

Requirements

  • Professional experience: beneficial in the event or banquet sector
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): as a chef
  • Organizational talent, flexibility and a cool head in hectic situations
  • Team players with high quality standards
  • A sense of responsibility with potential for leadership
  • Category B driving licence
  • German (good knowledge)
