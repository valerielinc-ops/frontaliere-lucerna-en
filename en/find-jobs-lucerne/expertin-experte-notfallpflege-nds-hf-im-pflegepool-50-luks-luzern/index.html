Full description

The Lucerne Canton Hospital with its locations in Lucerne, Sursee and Wolhusen is a company of the LUKS Group and thus belongs to one of the leading hospital groups in Switzerland. The more than 8,500 employees are there with warmth, competence and commitment around the clock for the well-being of patients. The clinics and institutes of the LUKS Group offer medical services of the highest quality.

For our team we are looking for reinforcement from 1 October 2026. Tasks: • You are the joker of our care teams at all four locations - always on when reinforcement is required • They are responsible for the professional, competent and patient-oriented initial care and care of emergency patients and their relatives and accompany and support them in crisis situations. • In the interprofessional team you make an important contribution to patient care at a high quality level • They support learners and students in their professional development Requirements • You are an expert in emergency care NDS HF or have an equivalent specialist training in emergency care and several years of practical experience in a Swiss acute care hospital • Drawing your quick comprehension, your understanding of people in exceptional situations and your high resilience You are out and you look forward to a lively operation • You are an empathetic, integrity personality with a high level of service and customer orientation as well as strong communication skills • Your German skills in word and writing correspond to level C1 We offer: • You largely determine your mission and service plan yourself and benefit from maximum flexibility • We offer you employment on an hourly basis and a varied, interesting and diverse working environment. • A state-of-the-art hospital information system that digitally and comprehensively maps the entire patient process and lists all patient data at any time and up-to-date supports you around the clock.
