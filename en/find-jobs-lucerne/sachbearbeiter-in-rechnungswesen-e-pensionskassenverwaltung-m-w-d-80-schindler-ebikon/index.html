Accounting and pension fund administration clerk (m/f/d) 80%

The Schindler pension fund provides occupational pensions for our employees in Switzerland. In this versatile role, you will be responsible for the pension fund's financial and securities accounting, from monitoring and recording all payment flows to preparing the annual financial statements. In addition, you will independently and efficiently carry out all insurance-related and administrative tasks relating to insured person management.

Your new challenges

Accounting • Accounting including preparation of financial statements in accordance with Swiss GAAP FER 26 • Liquidity planning including procurement and transfer of liquidity • Coordination between financial accounting and technical administration (M&S Pension) • Processing of VAT, withholding tax and withholding tax • Compiling the necessary documents for auditors as part of the annual audit • Further development and optimization of accounting processes

Insured person management • Processing of mutations as well as entries and exits, recording of vested benefits • Processing of various event cases such as home ownership promotion or divorces • Monthly contribution votes • Preliminary calculations of retirement benefits • Contact person for the insured • Support and representation in the pension fund

Your ideal conditions

• Basic commercial training with further training to become an accounting clerk or federal. Expertise in finance and accounting • Further training in the social insurance sector, ideally a specialist certificate in social insurance or employee benefits • Experience in financial and securities accounting, preferably in a pension fund • In-depth knowledge of the 2nd pillar • Confident use of MS Office, experience with ABACUS • Distinctive flair for numbers as well as a precise and independent way of working • Discreet and responsible handling of confidential data • Team-oriented personality • Stylish spoken and written German

  • Knowledge of French and Italian is an advantage
