Full description

Are you looking for a meaningful task in which you can accompany and support children in everyday school life? In our supplementary school supervision of the Felsberg/Unterlöchli primary school, you can expect a varied environment, a committed team and the chance to contribute your educational skills with pleasure.

Tasks

  • You actively participate in the care team and design a professional, child-friendly care and promotion over the lunchtime in the children's restaurant.
  • You will maintain contact with teachers, home maintenance and other specialists at the school.
  • You work mainly during the school weeks, on Tuesday and/or Thursday from around 11.15 a.m. to 1.45 p.m.

Requirements

  • You bring a completed socio-educational, socio-cultural or educational education.
  • You have experience and joy in accompanying children from 6 to 13 years old, supporting them and supporting them in their everyday life.
  • You empathize with the children’s world and deal with culturally and age-mixed groups.
  • You convince through your empathetic, appreciative and team-oriented way and actively contribute to our school life.
  • You work independently, reliably and communicate openly, clearly and transparently.
