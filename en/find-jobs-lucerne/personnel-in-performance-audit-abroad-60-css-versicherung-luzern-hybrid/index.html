Full description

We at CSS are a health partner for around 1.7 million insured people – and maybe you will soon be one of them? In our team abroad, everything revolves around performance cases across national borders. Do you love complex questions and keep an overview internationally? Perfect. Exciting cases await you, flexible working hours, home office and a strong team that supports each other. When do we meet?

This is what your everyday life looks like

  • At the heart of it: You have your international benefits invoices under control – because our insured rely on a correct and fast examination.
  • Between insured persons, foreign service providers and internal specialist offices you move safely and clarify open questions.
  • Even in tricky cases, you keep an overview, clarify medical and legal questions and consult internal specialist areas if necessary.
  • In exchange with colleagues, you contribute your knowledge and find solutions for demanding performance cases.
  • You provide telephone and written information on performance issues in an international context.
  • Through your structured way of working, you ensure that quality and service standards are right at all times.

You bring that with you

  • First things first: You have a completed basic commercial education and are interested in medical topics. Paramedical training is an advantage.
  • You express yourself very well in German and English (verbal and written). Every other foreign language is a plus.
  • It’s easy for you to organize, prioritize, and keep track.
  • With digital tools, of course, you deal and quickly find yourself in new systems.
  • A careful, thoughtful way of working and the ability to stay focused even in complex cases set you apart.
  • Challenging requests you meet sovereignly and actively seek solutions.
  • Work Flexible: Adapt your work to your lifestyle annually with part-time options and other flexible models like home office and more.
  • Enjoy your wallet: Benefits such as bonus, Reka, discounted health insurance premium and others that quickly make up a few thousands a year.
  • Choose your holiday model: 5 or 6 weeks holiday? Your decision. Accordingly, you work 41 or 42 hours a week.
  • Improve yourself further: We like to see it if you educate yourself internally or externally and support generously with time and money.
  • Stay fit: Ergonomic workplaces, promotion of mental well-being and many other offers around your health.
  • Engage Socially: Does Life Mean Good to You? Then let others share your happiness and expand your horizons alone or in a team.
