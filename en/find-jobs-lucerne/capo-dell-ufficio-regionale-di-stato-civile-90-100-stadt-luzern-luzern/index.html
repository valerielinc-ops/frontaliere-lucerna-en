Full description

Do you want to lead the civil status office of the most beautiful city in Switzerland? We look forward to your application!

Tasks

  • You will take over the technical and personnel management of the Regional Civil Status Office of the City of Lucerne with the associated municipalities of Malters, Meggen, Greppen, Vitznau, Weggis and Schwarzenberg.
  • You record all civil status events and conduct weddings.
  • You position the Civil Status Office as a competent and reliable point of contact for the population and our partners.
  • You will continuously develop organization, processes and services and promote employees in their development.
  • As a member of the management of the department of population services, you are committed to a modern, effective and efficient service company.

Requirements

  • You have a proven track record as a civil status officer and several years of management experience – ideally in a public administration and at management level.
  • In addition to the federal certificate as a civil status officer, you have developed your leadership skills in a targeted and continuous manner.
  • You lead clearly and bindingly, give orientation, provide impulses for change and promote an appreciative and participatory culture.
  • You maintain a dialogue with different stakeholders and actively contribute to joint solutions.
  • Even in demanding situations, you act calmly, prioritize safely and keep track.
  • You have good foreign language skills.
