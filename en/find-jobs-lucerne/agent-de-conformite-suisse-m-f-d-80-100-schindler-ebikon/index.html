Your new challenges

As Compliance Officer Switzerland, you take over responsibility for compliance at the legal service of Schindler Aufzüge AG in Ebikon and are the central contact person for compliance issues in the company. You are actively involved in shaping the area in a world-leading industrial company and thus make a significant contribution to the further development of our compliance culture.

Tasks

  • Advising the management, departments and subsidiaries on compliance/code of conduct issues
  • Monitoring and enforcement of the Schindler Code of Conduct (especially in the areas of competition and anti-corruption)
  • Preparation and implementation of training on compliance/code of conduct topics
  • Investigation of potential Code of Conduct violations
  • Implementation of Group-wide compliance initiatives
  • Collaboration with other Schindler Compliance Officers, the Regional Compliance Officer and Global Compliance
  • Representation of the company in internal compliance audits
  • Management of the intra-corporate reporting platform
  • Where appropriate, support for the legal service team in legal matters

Your ideal conditions

  • Completed studies, preferably in the field of law (with or without a lawyer's patent)
  • In-depth knowledge of compliance and corresponding professional experience, ideally in an international company
  • Knowledge of relevant laws and regulations (anti-trust law, anti-corruption, unfair competition, trade sanctions, etc.)
  • Fluent German skills, as well as very good French and English skills in word and writing, ideally also Italian skills
  • Very careful and structured working method
  • Integrity, discretion and enforcement
  • Communication strength and safe presence
