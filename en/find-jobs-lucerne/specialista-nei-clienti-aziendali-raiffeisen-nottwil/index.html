Full description

Raiffeisenbank Sempachersee-Rottal Süd is a successful and strongly anchored consulting bank with offices in Buttisholz, Hildisrieden, Neuenkirch, Nottwil, Ruswil and St. Erhard. In order to strengthen our team, we are looking for an independent, committed and customer-oriented personality as a <b>specialist* in corporate customers</b>.

Tasks

  • Appreciative cooperation based on mutual trust
  • Contemporary working conditions with flexible forms of work, the possibility of a partial home office and a modern workplace in newly designed offices
  • Support of corporate client consultants in all administrative matters incl. Pre- and post-processing of conversations
  • Analysis of annual accounts and credit assessments
  • Management of existing credit positions and preparation and implementation of periodic re-authorisations
  • Opening banking relationships and providing new products and services to existing corporate clients
  • Professional support for credit advice in the area of corporate clients as well as needs-based support for consultants during customer discussions
  • Valuation of real estate
  • Ensure compliance with regulatory requirements and internal banking instructions
  • Telephone and written communications with offices, insurance companies and other banks

Requirements

  • Fun at work and a good dose of humor and teamwork, fast comprehension and networked and analytical thinking
  • Completed banking education with many years of experience and extensive expertise in loans and mortgages; your experiences from customer advice or credit processing help you in this function
  • Proven experience in the analysis and interpretation of company figures as well as in the assessment of credit risk and business models
  • practice in assessing different types of property
  • Knowledge of Wüest Dimensions is an advantage
  • High degree of independence, responsible, yet efficient and customer- and service-oriented working methods

We offer

Cooperative values are our fundamental pillars: togetherness. For each other. They shape our cooperation and our interaction with each other. The Du culture creates personal closeness and eye level across all functional levels.
