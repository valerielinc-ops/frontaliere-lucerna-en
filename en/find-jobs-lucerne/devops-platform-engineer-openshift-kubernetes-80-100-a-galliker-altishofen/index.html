DevOps / Platform Engineer (Openshift/Kbernetes) 80-100% (a)

Description

Are you not an administrator but a doer, a creative thinker and an innovator? As a DevOps Engineer, you take ownership of our DevOps activities and actively help shape our platform and processes through automation, GitOps and CI/CD. You improve deployment and operations on OpenShift, challenge the status quo and develop simple, effective solutions—in collaboration with software developers, architects, security and partners. For us, initiative, fast decision-making and the courage to take responsibility are what count.

Galliker is one of Switzerland’s leading logistics and transport companies, with national and international locations. From the Altishofen location, an IT team of around 80 supports more than 20 branches across Europe and, thanks to state-of-the-art technology, a culture of ideas and strong teamwork, ensures smooth day-to-day operations. As an IT team, we strive every day to bring technology and people together in order to deliver fast, high-quality and reliable software to our internal and external customers.

We see ourselves as a service provider for our Software Developer Community: We create the framework conditions that enable development teams to deliver quickly, securely and efficiently. If you have the start-up/scale-up gene, are driven by improvement through simplification, and not only value responsibility, transparency and collaboration but live them, then we want to get to know you as a DevOps Engineer with personality.

Responsibilities

  • Responsibility for operating and further developing central platform components such as OpenShift, Container Registry and monitoring (on-premises)
  • Setting up, maintaining and further developing standardized build, test and release processes
  • Supporting and gradually modernizing our existing DevOps toolchain (TeamCity, IIS)
  • Proactive incident management to ensure stability and performance
  • Ensuring security, compliance and governance requirements in the deployment environment
  • Evaluating new technologies and tools for the continuous improvement of the platform and processes
  • Creating and maintaining technical documentation and best practices
  • Supporting our development teams in containerizing, deploying and operating their applications
  • Close collaboration with development, architecture and IT security

Requirements

  • Completed bachelor's degree in computer science, engineering or a comparable qualification
  • Several years of experience as a DevOps Engineer or in a comparable role, as well as a sound understanding of build, test, release, and DevOps processes
  • Experience with containers and platforms such as Docker, Kubernetes/OpenShift, as well as with container registries (e.g. Harbor)
  • Hands-on experience with CI/CD and Git, ideally with ArgoCD, Tekton, and TeamCity
  • Experience with monitoring and logging (e.g. ELK Stack, Sentry), as well as with security and compliance requirements in container environments
  • Knowledge of C# and/or React is an advantage
  • Strong analytical and conceptual skills, quick comprehension, and excellent problem-solving abilities
  • Enjoyment of designing processes that are simple and effective, with a consistent focus on added value and security
  • Strong communication skills, as well as experience communicating platforms, processes, and knowledge appropriately to the target audience
  • Team-oriented, proactive way of working and high self-motivation
  • Experience with agile working methods and prioritization
  • Very good command of German and English, both written and spoken

What we offer

  • Exciting transport and logistics projects
  • Cool, motivated team
  • State-of-the-art IT infrastructures
  • A high degree of autonomy and appreciation
  • Wide range of activities in the world of transport & logistics with international contacts at branch offices
  • Possibility of occasional remote work

Contact

For this position, we will only consider direct applications.

Please note that, due to the applicable Swiss legislation, we can only consider applications from Swiss citizens, persons with a residence or settlement permit (permit B or C), EU/EFTA citizens, and persons with a valid work permit.

Remote work from abroad is not possible.
